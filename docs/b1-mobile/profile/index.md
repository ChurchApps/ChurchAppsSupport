# Profile

<div class="article-intro">

Your profile in B1 Mobile is where you manage your personal information, your household, and your privacy settings. Everything you update here is reflected across all ChurchApps services, so you only need to make changes in one place.

</div>

## Profile Tabs

The Profile screen is organized into several tabs:

### Profile

The **Profile** tab lets you view and edit your personal information, including your name, contact details, and profile photo. Keeping your profile up to date helps your church stay in touch with you.

### Household

The **Household** tab shows your family members and their roles within your household. This information is used for features like [check-in](../checkin/index.md), where you can check in your whole family at once.

### Visibility

The **Visibility** tab lets you control what other church members can see about you in the [directory](../community/member-directory.md). You decide which personal details are shared and which stay private.

### Pending

The **Pending** tab shows any profile changes you have submitted that are waiting for admin approval.

### Account

The **Account** tab provides access to your security settings.

## My Cards and My Tasks

If your church assigns you [workflow cards](../../b1-admin/serving/workflows.md) or [tasks](../../b1-admin/serving/tasks.md), the **Me** screen shows a **My cards** section and a **My tasks** section, and **Home** shows a short **Your follow-ups** list. These sections only appear when something is assigned to you, and closed tasks never show. Tap **All cards**, **All tasks**, or **All** to see everything assigned to you.

Tap a card to open it. You can:

- See its step, due date, and the person it is about. The person's name opens their directory profile when the directory shows them to you.
- Tap **Complete** (or the step's outcome button) when the step is done.
- **Snooze** it for 1, 3, or 7 days.
- Read and add **Notes**. These are the same notes staff see on the card in B1 Admin.

Tap a plain task to see who it is from, who it is about, and its notes. Closing a plain task still happens in B1 Admin.

## Getting to Your Profile

1. Open the drawer menu.
2. Tap **Profile** to open your profile screen.
3. Use the tabs at the top to navigate between sections.
