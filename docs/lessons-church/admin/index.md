# Administration

<div class="article-intro">

The admin area in Lessons.church is where content creators and administrators manage the lesson library. From here you can build out your curriculum structure, upload resources, review usage statistics, publish year plans, and maintain shared add-ons.

</div>

:::warning
Admin access requires permission to edit lessons, granted in B1 Admin. If you do not see **Admin** in your name menu, contact your church administrator to request access.
:::

## Getting There

Log in, click your name in the header, and choose **Admin**. The **Program Management** page opens, with a secondary menu of **Programs**, **Year Plans**, and **Add-ons**.

## The Program Management Page

The page has three columns:

- **Programs** (left) -- Every program you manage, with its study and lesson counts. Click one to select it and open its details.
- **Studies** (middle) -- The selected program's name, description, and counts, with **Add Study** and a **View Stats** icon. Below, each study is a card showing its lesson count; click a card to expand its lessons and open the study's details, then click a lesson row to open the lesson.
- **Details panel** (right) -- Whatever you last clicked. It has **Details** and **Files** tabs, plus **Venues** for lessons. On narrow screens the panel slides in from the right.

The page header has **Add Program** and **Clear Cache**. Public pages are cached for up to an hour; saving in the panel clears the cache for you, and **Clear Cache** forces it at any time.

## What You Can Do

- **Create and edit programs** -- Set up top-level curriculum series with names, descriptions, images, slugs, and trailers, and group their studies into categories.
- **Manage studies** -- Organize groups of related lessons within each program, with their own images, order, and descriptions.
- **Build lessons** -- Add individual lessons with parent take-home copy, then add venues and fill each venue with sections, roles, and actions.
- **Upload files** -- Attach bundles, resources, variants, assets, and external videos at the program, study, or lesson level.
- **Maintain add-ons** -- Keep a shared library of worship songs, games, and seasonal videos usable in any lesson.
- **Publish year plans** -- Create week 1, 2, 3 sequences for any curriculum. Churches apply them in B1 Admin.
- **View statistics** -- See which studies are being downloaded, by which churches, and where.

Connecting an external curriculum provider is a church-level setting, done from the **External Providers** page under **Schedules** rather than in the admin area. See [Third-Party Providers](./third-party-providers.md).

## Content Hierarchy

As an admin, it helps to understand how content is structured:

1. **Programs** contain one or more studies.
2. **Studies** contain one or more lessons.
3. **Lessons** contain one or more venues (age-group variations).
4. **Venues** contain sections, sections contain roles, and roles contain actions.

Files can be attached at the program, study, or lesson level and are available to every venue beneath that level.
