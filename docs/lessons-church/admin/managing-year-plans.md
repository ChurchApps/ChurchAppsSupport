# Managing Year Plans

<div class="article-intro">

A year plan is an ordered list of lessons — week 1, week 2, week 3 — that churches apply in B1 Admin. You can publish a plan for any curriculum in the library. Optional calendar fields tell B1 Admin which month week 1 belongs to and which weeks must land on Easter or Christmas.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- Have admin permissions for Lessons.church
- Have at least one [program](./managing-programs.md) with published lessons

</div>

## Creating a Plan

1. Open **Admin** and click **Year Plans** in the secondary menu. The **Plans** list shows each plan as **Published** or **Draft**.
2. Click **Add**. The **New Plan** form opens beside the list.
3. Enter a **Name** (for example, "Elementary 2026–27" or "High Voltage Year 1") and an optional **Slug**.
4. Optionally set a **Default venue preference** — comma-separated venue names; the first one that matches a lesson's venues is preselected when you add weeks.
5. Optionally set **Year starts in** (for example January) so churches apply the plan against a real calendar year. Leave it on **Not calendar-anchored** for a plan that starts on any date.
6. Check **Publish (visible to churches in B1 Admin)** when churches should see the plan. Leave it off for a draft.
7. Under **Add a week from any curriculum**, choose a **Source** (Lessons.church or one of your external providers), then the **Program**, **Study**, **Lesson**, and **Venue**, and click **Add**. A single plan can mix programs and providers. Each week shows in the table with its number, lesson, and venue; use the arrows to reorder and the trash can to remove.
8. For seasonal studies, set a week's **Anchor** to **Easter** or **Christmas**. Anchor the last week of the study so the series lands on the Sundays leading up to that holiday.
9. Click **Save**.

To edit a plan later, click its name in the **Plans** list; to remove one, click its **Delete** icon and confirm.

Churches apply a published plan from **B1 Admin → Serving → Plans → Schedule Lesson → Apply Year Plan**. Calendar-anchored plans ask for a target year and date range; other plans still use a first class date and week count. They edit individual weeks there afterward; FreePlay plays whatever is on the plan.
