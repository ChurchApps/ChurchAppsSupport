# Scheduling Lessons

<div class="article-intro">

Once your classrooms are set up, the next step is scheduling lessons so your volunteers know exactly what to teach and when. Scheduling connects the lesson content from Lessons.church to your classroom calendar.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- [Set up at least one classroom](./setting-up-classrooms.md) in the portal
- [Browse the content library](../browsing/index.md) to find a program and study you want to teach

</div>

## Viewing a Classroom Schedule

1. Log in and open **Schedules** (the **Classroom Management** page).
2. In the **Classrooms** box, click the **classroom** you want to schedule.
3. The **Lesson Schedule** box lists every lesson scheduled for that classroom, newest date first. Each one shows its date and is tagged **Upcoming**, **Today**, or **Past**, so you can see at a glance what was recently taught and what is coming up.

Under each lesson's name you will see **Customize** for Lessons.church lessons or **View Lesson** for lessons from an external provider.

## Adding a Lesson to the Schedule

1. In the **Lesson Schedule** box, click **Add Schedule**. If the classroom has nothing scheduled yet, click **Add First Schedule** instead.
2. The **Edit Schedule** form opens. When the classroom already has lessons, it is pre-filled with the most recent entry's program, study, and venue, the **next** lesson in that study, and a **Schedule Date** one week later -- so continuing a series is usually just a matter of clicking **Save**.
3. Set the **Schedule Date**.
4. If you have added any [external providers](../admin/third-party-providers.md), a **Provider** dropdown appears. Leave it on **Lessons.church** to schedule from the Lessons.church library, or choose a provider to load its catalog.
5. Choose the **Program** you want to teach from (for example, "Bible Stories for Kids").
6. Select the **Study** within that program (for example, "Creation and Early Stories").
7. Pick the specific **Lesson**. Lessons already scheduled for this classroom on another date are shown in grey, which helps you avoid repeating one.
8. Choose the appropriate **Venue** for this classroom. Venues represent age-group-specific versions of the lesson content (for example, "Preschool" or "Elementary"). Picking the right venue ensures your volunteers see content tailored to their classroom's age group.
9. Click **Save**. To look the lesson over first, click **Preview / Print**, which opens the venue in a new tab with a print preview.

The lesson appears on the classroom's schedule for that date, named after its study and lesson.

:::info
Programs, studies, and lessons are the content hierarchy on Lessons.church. A program contains multiple studies, and each study contains individual lessons. Venues provide age-specific variations of the same lesson. See [Programs and Studies](../browsing/programs-and-studies.md) for a full explanation.
:::

## Editing or Removing a Scheduled Lesson

1. In the **Lesson Schedule** box, click the **Edit schedule** (pencil) icon on the entry.
2. Change the date, provider, program, study, lesson, or venue and click **Save**, or click **Cancel** to leave it as is.
3. To remove the entry, click the **Delete schedule** (trash can) icon and confirm.

## How Volunteers Use the Schedule

Once you have scheduled lessons, your volunteers can open the lesson several ways: from the portal if they have access, from the classroom's public upcoming-lessons page, or from the **Lessons** tab of the B1 app when the classroom is linked to their team. They can review the lesson content ahead of time, download any materials they need, and arrive prepared. See [Classroom Pages, Feeds, and B1 Groups](./sharing-and-feeds.md) and [Teaching from the B1 App](../presenting/b1-teacher-view.md).

## Tips for Scheduling

- **Plan ahead** -- Churches that run serving plans schedule classroom lessons in [B1 Admin](../../b1-admin/serving/scheduling-lessons.md) instead, including applying a published year plan to a whole year at once.
- **Stay consistent** -- Following a program sequentially helps kids build on what they learned the previous week. The pre-filled **next** lesson makes this the default.
- **Check the history** -- Use the **Past** entries and the greyed-out lessons in the picker to make sure you are not accidentally repeating content.

:::warning
Make sure to select the correct venue for your classroom's age group. Choosing the wrong venue means your volunteers will see content designed for a different age level.
:::
