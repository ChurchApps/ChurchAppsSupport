# Setting Up Classrooms

<div class="article-intro">

Before you can schedule lessons, you need to create classrooms that represent your teaching environments. This guide walks you through creating, naming, and managing classrooms in the Lessons.church portal.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- [Create an account](../getting-started/creating-account.md) and connect to your church
- Familiarize yourself with the [Portal Overview](../getting-started/portal-overview.md)

</div>

## Creating a Classroom

1. Log in to your account at [Lessons.church](https://lessons.church).
2. Open **Schedules** from your name menu. This is the **Classroom Management** page, your church's central hub for classrooms and schedules.
3. In the **Classrooms** box, click **Add**. If you don't have any classrooms yet, click **Add First Classroom** instead.
4. Enter a **Classroom Name**. Choose something descriptive that your volunteers will recognize -- for example, "Preschool," "Elementary K-2," or "Middle School."
5. Optionally choose a **Volunteer Team (optional)** and a **Parent/Student Group (optional)**. These are your church's B1 teams and groups; linking them gives team members the upcoming lessons and group members the recent lessons in the B1 app. You can leave both on **None** and set them later.
6. Click **Save**. Your new classroom appears in the **Classrooms** list right away.

Repeat these steps for each room or age group at your church. You can create as many classrooms as you need.

## First-Time Setup

Until you select a classroom, the left side of the page shows a **Setup Instructions** video. It covers the basics of creating classrooms and scheduling your first lessons and is a great starting point if you are new to Lessons.church.

:::tip
Name your classrooms to match how your volunteers already think about your rooms. If everyone calls it "the Pre-K room," name it "Pre-K" rather than something formal like "Early Childhood Education Room 101."
:::

## Managing Classrooms

Your classrooms appear in the **Classrooms** box, where you can manage them at any time. From here you can:

- **View the schedule** -- Click the classroom name to load its **Lesson Schedule**.
- **Rename or relink a classroom** -- Click the **Edit classroom** (pencil) icon next to it, change the **Classroom Name** or the team and group fields, and click **Save**.
- **Get its links and feed** -- Click the **Subscribe to feed** (RSS) icon. See [Classroom Pages, Feeds, and B1 Groups](./sharing-and-feeds.md).
- **Remove a classroom** -- Click **Edit classroom**, then click the **Delete classroom** (trash can) icon and confirm. Its schedule is removed with it.

## What's Next

Once your classrooms are set up, you are ready to start [scheduling lessons](./scheduling-lessons.md) so your volunteers know what to teach each week.
