# Volunteer Guides

<div class="article-intro">

Every section of a lesson is written as a sequence of Say, Do, Note, and Play actions, organized behind the scenes by volunteer role. This guide explains how that layout works, how to print a lesson for volunteers who prefer paper, and how to send the right lesson to the right people.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- Understand lesson structure by reading [Viewing Lessons](../browsing/viewing-lessons.md)
- Navigate to the lesson you want to prepare guides for

</div>

## How Guides Are Organized

Lessons are broken into **sections** (for example, "Welcome", "Bible Story", "Small Group Activity"). Within each section, **roles** are assigned specific **actions**. There are four action types:

- **Say** -- Scripted text for the volunteer to read or paraphrase.
- **Do** -- A physical action or activity to carry out.
- **Note** -- Background information or reminders for the volunteer (not spoken aloud).
- **Play** -- A video or slide to show the class.

The curriculum provider assigns every action to a role (for example, "Leader" or "Small Group Leader"), and the actions for one role are kept together within the section. The lesson page, the B1 app, and the print formats show the actions in that order but do not print the role names, so the whole venue reads as one continuous guide. The **Sections** list in the sidebar jumps between sections. Coordinators can see which role owns each action on the **Customize Lesson** page, which lists sections, roles, and actions as a tree.

## Printing a Lesson

1. Open the lesson and choose the **Venue** for the room.
2. Click the **Print** icon in the sidebar. The **Print Preview** dialog opens.
3. Pick a **Format** tab:
   - **Color Coded** (the default) -- Each section lists its actions grouped by type, with a SAY, DO, NOTE, or PLAY label beside each group. The lesson image, study and lesson names, venue, and description print at the top.
   - **Script** -- A compact script with the lesson header centered and each section's actions in order, styled by type.
   - **Lessons.church** -- The section cards exactly as they appear on the website.
4. Click **Print** to send it to your printer or save it as a PDF, then **Close**.

Coordinators can also print a scheduled lesson from the portal: open the schedule entry and click **Preview / Print**, which opens the lesson in the Open Lesson Format Builder with the same **Print Preview** button.

## Sharing Lessons with Volunteers

- **Link to the lesson** -- Copy the address of the lesson page and send it. Volunteers do not need an account to open it.
- **Share the upcoming-lessons page** -- Each classroom has a public page listing its next four scheduled lessons. See [Classroom Pages, Feeds, and B1 Groups](../classrooms/sharing-and-feeds.md).
- **Use the B1 app** -- Link the classroom to a volunteer team, and team members open the current lesson from the **Lessons** tab. See [Teaching from the B1 App](./b1-teacher-view.md).

## Multiple Roles

Some lessons have several roles -- for example, a lead teacher, a small-group leader, and a tech volunteer. Because their actions are grouped together, a printed copy can be highlighted once per person. To see exactly which actions belong to which role, open the lesson's **Customize** page from the classroom schedule.

:::info
If a role does not apply to your classroom, you can remove it using the [customization](../customization/customizing-lessons.md) features so its actions disappear from the lesson in the B1 app.
:::
