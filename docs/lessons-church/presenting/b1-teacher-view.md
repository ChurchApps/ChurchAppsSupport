# Teaching from the B1 App

<div class="article-intro">

Volunteers do not need a laptop to follow a lesson. The **Lessons** tab in the B1 mobile app opens a phone-friendly view of each classroom's scheduled lesson, with sticky section tabs, links to the previous and next week, and a print button. This guide walks through what a volunteer sees.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- Install the B1 app and sign in to your church
- Have your church [schedule lessons](../classrooms/scheduling-lessons.md) to its classrooms
- Optionally [link the classroom to a volunteer team](../classrooms/sharing-and-feeds.md) so your rooms are listed first

</div>

## Opening a Lesson

1. In the B1 app, open the **Lessons** tab. The app signs you into Lessons.church automatically; you may briefly see **Signing in...**.
2. A **Select a Room** list appears. If the classroom is linked to a team or group you belong to, you see just those rooms; otherwise you see every classroom at your church. If your church has only one classroom it opens directly.
3. Tap a room. If a lesson is scheduled for today or the last five days, the most recent one opens right away. Otherwise you see the classroom name with a dated list of its scheduled lessons; tap one to open it.

## The Lesson View

- **Back to Classrooms** -- Returns to the room list.
- **Previous and next dates** -- The dates of the lesson before and after this one on the classroom's schedule appear to the left and right of the current date. Tap one to move a week back or forward.
- **Section tabs** -- A bar across the top lists every section in the venue that has content. Tap a tab to scroll to that section; as you scroll, the highlighted tab follows you. The bar stays pinned to the top of the screen.
- **Sections** -- Each section card shows its name, a **Materials:** line when the curriculum lists supplies, and its actions in order. Say, Do, Note, and Play actions look the same as on the website; tapping a Play action opens the video or slide.
- **print** -- A small **print** button above the sections prints the whole venue (or hands the job to the app on a phone).

Any [customizations](../customization/customizing-lessons.md) your church saved for this classroom are applied here, so removed sections and reordered actions match what the coordinator set up.

:::tip
Open the lesson on Saturday and read the **Note** actions. They hold the background and reminders that are not meant to be read aloud, so you are not meeting them for the first time in front of the class.
:::

## Lessons from External Providers

If the scheduled lesson comes from an [external provider](../browsing/external-providers.md), it opens in the same view. The sections, roles, and media come from the provider's feed.
