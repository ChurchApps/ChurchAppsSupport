# Presenter Mode

<div class="article-intro">

Presenter Mode turns a lesson's media into a full-screen slideshow. Every video and slide the lesson plays, in lesson order, is shown one at a time on a black background so that everyone in the room can see it. It is designed for the classroom computer connected to a projector or TV.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- [Schedule a lesson](../classrooms/scheduling-lessons.md) to a classroom, or navigate to a lesson through the [content library](../browsing/index.md)
- Make sure your display (projector, TV, or monitor) is connected and set to mirror or extend your screen

</div>

## Starting the Presentation

1. Open the lesson you want to present and choose the right **Venue** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Start Lesson** at the top of the page.
3. The browser switches to full screen and the first item plays. Videos start automatically; slides stay until you move on.

## Moving Between Items

- Press the **right arrow** key, or click the arrow button on the right edge of the screen, to go to the next video or slide.
- Press the **left arrow** key, or click the arrow button on the left edge, to go back.
- The order follows the lesson: every **Play** action from the first section to the last, including each slide of a slideshow and any add-on worship videos. Nothing else from the lesson (scripts, notes, instructions) is shown, so keep your script open on another device or on paper.

## Ending the Presentation

Click the **X** button in the top-right corner, or press **Escape** to leave full screen. Either returns you to the lesson page.

:::info
Vimeo videos the provider marked as looping (for example, a countdown or background loop) repeat until you press the arrow key. Everything else plays once and then waits.
:::

## Using with FreePlay

**[FreePlay](https://freeplay.church)** lets you display lessons on a TV without a separate computer. Install the app on an Android TV, Fire TV, or Apple TV device, connect it to your church, and the scheduled lesson for each classroom plays automatically with the same media Presenter Mode shows. This is a great hands-free option for classrooms that have a dedicated screen. See the [FreePlay docs](/docs/freeplay/) for setup.

## Using with FreeShow

Lessons.church integrates with [FreeShow](https://freeshow.app), a free presentation application. FreeShow can pull lesson content directly from Lessons.church, allowing you to incorporate lesson media into your existing presentation workflow. This is especially useful if you already use FreeShow for worship lyrics or announcements.

:::tip
Before class, click through the whole presentation once. It confirms every video loads and lets you see where each slide falls in the lesson.
:::
