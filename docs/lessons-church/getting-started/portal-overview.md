# Portal Overview

<div class="article-intro">

After logging in to Lessons.church, you are taken to the **Portal** -- your church's dashboard for managing classrooms and lesson schedules. This is where you will spend most of your time when organizing curriculum for your church.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- [Create an account](./creating-account.md) and connect to your church
- Log in to [Lessons.church](https://lessons.church)

</div>

## Finding the Portal

After login you land on the portal. From anywhere on the public site, click your name in the top-right corner to open the user menu. It contains **Schedules** (the portal), **Admin** if you have content-editing permission, and **Logout**.

## What You See

The portal opens on the **Classroom Management** page, which is organized into two main areas:

- **Classrooms** (right side) -- A list of your church's classrooms. Click a classroom name to view its scheduled lessons. Each row has a **Subscribe to feed** (RSS) icon and an **Edit classroom** (pencil) icon, and the box header has an **Add** button.
- **Lesson Schedule** (left side) -- Once you select a classroom, this area lists the lessons scheduled for it, with **Add Schedule** in its header. Before you select a classroom it shows a **Setup Instructions** video.

Below both boxes, links to download the FreePlay TV app and the B1 mobile app are shown.

## What You Can Do from the Portal

From the portal, you can:

1. **Manage classrooms** -- Add, edit, or remove classrooms, and link each one to a B1 volunteer team (so team members see upcoming lessons in the B1 app) and a parent/student group (so members see recent lessons). See [Setting Up Classrooms](../classrooms/setting-up-classrooms.md).
2. **View and manage scheduled lessons** -- See which lessons are coming up for each classroom, add new ones, and edit or delete entries. See [Scheduling Lessons](../classrooms/scheduling-lessons.md).
3. **Customize a lesson** -- Click **Customize** under a scheduled Lessons.church lesson to remove or reorder its sections for this classroom. See [Customizing Lessons](../customization/customizing-lessons.md).
4. **Get classroom links and feeds** -- Click the **Subscribe to feed** icon to get the classroom's public page, its upcoming-lessons page for teachers, and the playlist feed URL for digital signage. See [Classroom Pages, Feeds, and B1 Groups](../classrooms/sharing-and-feeds.md).
5. **Manage external providers** -- Use the **External Providers** page to add third-party content sources that publish in the Open Lesson Format. See [Third-Party Providers](../admin/third-party-providers.md).
6. **Open the Admin area** -- If you have content-editing permission, an **Admin** option appears. The admin area is where curriculum providers manage programs, studies, lessons, and files.

## Navigation

The portal header has a primary menu with **Home**, **Schedules**, and (for content editors) **Admin**. Under **Schedules**, a secondary menu switches between:

- **Schedules** -- The Classroom Management page described above.
- **External Providers** -- The **External Lesson Providers** page.

Under **Admin**, the secondary menu offers **Programs**, **Year Plans**, and **Add-ons**.

:::info
**Schedules** requires permission to edit schedules for your church, and **Admin** requires permission to edit lessons. Both are granted in B1 Admin by your church's administrator. Most church staff will use the **Schedules** and **External Providers** pages for day-to-day management.
:::

## Getting Started

If you are visiting the portal for the first time and have not created any classrooms yet, start by clicking **Add First Classroom** in the **Classrooms** box. Give your classroom a name (for example, "Preschool" or "3rd-5th Grade") and save it. From there, you can begin [scheduling lessons](../classrooms/scheduling-lessons.md).
