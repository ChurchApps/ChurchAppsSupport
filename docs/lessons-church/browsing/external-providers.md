# External Providers

<div class="article-intro">

Lessons.church is not limited to its own curriculum. Third-party curriculum providers can publish their content through the **Open Lesson Format**. Once your church adds a provider, you can schedule its lessons for your classrooms right next to the Lessons.church library.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- Understand how content is organized by reading [Programs and Studies](./programs-and-studies.md)
- To add a provider connection, you need a [Lessons.church account](../getting-started/creating-account.md) with access to your church's classroom schedules

</div>

## What Are External Providers?

An external provider is any organization that publishes church curriculum using the Open Lesson Format -- a standard that allows lesson content to be shared across platforms. When a provider makes their content available in this format, Lessons.church can read it and show its lessons with the same layout as its own.

This means you get access to a wider selection of curriculum without having to visit multiple websites.

## Adding Provider Connections

If your church uses a curriculum provider that supports the Open Lesson Format:

1. Log in to your Lessons.church account.
2. Click **External Providers** in the navigation.
3. Click **Add Provider**, enter a **Provider Name** and the provider's **API URL**, and click **Save**.

See [Third-Party Providers](../admin/third-party-providers.md) for the full steps.

:::info
An external provider is added for your church only. Its programs don't appear on the public Lessons.church home page or in search.
:::

## Using External Content

Once a provider is added, its programs, studies, and lessons are available when you schedule:

1. When you [add a lesson to a classroom's schedule](../classrooms/scheduling-lessons.md), choose the provider under **Provider**.
2. Pick the **Program**, **Study**, **Lesson**, and **Venue** the same way you would for a Lessons.church lesson.
3. In the classroom's schedule, click **View Lesson** under a scheduled external lesson to open it. It uses the same layout as built-in lessons -- sections, roles, actions, videos, and downloadable materials.

:::note
Lessons from external providers can't be [customized](../customization/customizing-lessons.md). Only Lessons.church lessons show the **Customize** link.
:::

## For Content Creators

If you are a curriculum publisher interested in making your content available on Lessons.church, the Open Lesson Format provides a standardized way to share your programs, studies, and lessons. Contact the Lessons.church team or visit the developer documentation for integration details.

For step-by-step instructions on adding, editing, and deleting a provider, see [Third-Party Providers](../admin/third-party-providers.md).
:::
