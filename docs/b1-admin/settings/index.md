# Settings

<div class="article-intro">

The Settings section is where you configure your church's account, branding, and administrative options in B1 Admin. From here you can manage everything from your church name and subdomain to user permissions and mobile app configuration.

</div>

## What You Will Find Here

The Settings area is organized into the following sub-sections:

1. **Settings** -- Configure your church's basic information, branding, subdomain, region (date and phone format), texting provider, and file storage. See [Church Settings](./church-settings.md) for details. To use your own domain name (e.g. yourchurch.org), see [Custom Domain](./custom-domain.md).
2. **Campuses** -- Add and manage multiple physical locations for your church. Once created, campuses appear on person profiles, in attendance setup, and in the Demographics dashboard. Reached as a section on the main Settings page rather than a separate navigation item. See [Campuses](./campuses.md) for details.
3. **Custom Fields** -- Define your own fields to track on people (a date, number, yes/no answer, or pick-list), then fill them in on profiles and search on them. Also reached as a section on the main Settings page. See [Custom Fields](./custom-fields.md) for details.
4. **Email Templates** -- Save reusable email content for sending manually or from a workflow. See [Email Templates](./email-templates.md) for details.
5. **Audit Log** and **Batches** -- Review changes made across your account ([Audit Log](../reports/audit-log.md)), and review or undo bulk imports and actions (**Batches**).
6. **Server Admin** -- Access advanced administration tools for managing your church's server-level settings.

:::info
**Mobile Apps** configuration and **Forms** now have their own top-level navigation items -- **Mobile** and **Forms** (found under **People**) -- rather than living inside Settings. See [Mobile App Settings](./mobile-app.md) and [Creating Forms](/docs/b1-admin/forms/creating-forms) for details.
:::

## The Main Settings Page

When you first open the Settings page, the panel on the left shows your **church name**, **subdomain**, and address, along with an **Import/Export** link that opens the import/export tool for transferring data between systems.

Below that, a **Configuration** list links to each settings section: **Church Information**, **General**, **Region**, **Giving**, **Texting**, **File Storage**, **Domains**, **Grade Promotion**, **Check-In**, **Campuses**, **Custom Fields**, and **Developer**. Sections that still need setting up (such as Giving, Texting, or Domains) are marked with a small dot. All of the sections are on one page -- click a name in the list to scroll to it, or just scroll down. Each section shows its current settings; click **Edit** in a section to change them.

**Email Templates**, **Audit Log**, and **Batches** are separate pages -- open them from the [Jump menu](../introduction.md#getting-around-with-the-jump-menu) under **Settings**.

Start with the **Church Information** section to edit your church's name, address, and contact information -- see [Church Settings](./church-settings.md). To manage user roles, open the [Jump menu](../introduction.md#getting-around-with-the-jump-menu) and choose **Settings > Roles** -- see [Roles & Permissions](./roles-permissions.md).

:::tip
Start by configuring your church name and branding under **Church Information**, then set up [Roles](./roles-permissions.md) to invite your team members with the right level of access.
:::

:::info
For information about how your data is protected, see [Data Security](./data-security.md).
:::
