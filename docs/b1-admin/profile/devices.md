# Managing Devices

<div class="article-intro">

The Devices page lets you manage ChurchAppsPlayer and FreePlay devices connected to your account. These devices are used for streaming content and displaying church media on screens at your location.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- Have a ChurchAppsPlayer or FreePlay device powered on and connected to the internet
- Note the pairing code displayed on the device's screen
- Review your [profile settings](./managing-profile.md) if you have not already

</div>

## Opening the Devices Page

Open the [Jump menu](../introduction.md#getting-around-with-the-jump-menu) (the search bar at the top-left of B1 Admin), type **Devices**, and choose it. While you're on your Profile page, the Jump menu also lists **Profile** and **Devices** side by side.

## Viewing Your Devices

When you open the Devices page, you will see a table listing the ChurchAppsPlayer and FreePlay screens paired with your account, with the following information:

- **Label** -- The name you have assigned to the device (**Device** until you give it one).
- **Registration Date** -- When the device was first paired with your account.
- **Last Active Date** -- The most recent time the device connected to your account.

## Adding a New Device

1. Click the **Add Screen** button at the top of the Devices page.
2. Find the pairing code displayed on the ChurchAppsPlayer or FreePlay device you want to connect.
3. Enter it in the **Pairing Code** field.
4. If your church has [plan types](../serving/plans.md) set up, a **Show Plans For** dropdown lets you pick the plan type a FreePlay screen should follow — leave it on **None (notifications only)** to use the device for browsing and notifications only.
5. Click **Save**. The device appears in your devices table once it's paired. If the code is wrong or has expired, you'll see *"Invalid pairing code"*.

:::tip
Make sure the device is powered on and connected to the internet before attempting to pair it. The pairing code is displayed on the device's screen.
:::

## Editing a Device Label

1. Click **Edit** next to the device in the devices table.
2. Update the **Label** to something descriptive (for example, "Sanctuary Main Screen" or "Lobby Display").
3. Click **Save**.

The **Edit Device** form also has an **Associated Content** list of your church's Lessons.church classrooms. Check a classroom to associate it with the screen; each checkbox saves as soon as you click it.

:::tip
Using clear, descriptive labels makes it easy to identify which device is which, especially when you have multiple screens set up at your church.
:::

## Managing Device Access

Use the Devices page to keep track of which devices have access to your church's streaming and content. If a device is no longer in use, remove it from your account:

1. Click **Edit** next to the device.
2. Click **Remove**, then **Remove** again to confirm.

The screen will need a new pairing code to connect again.

:::warning
Regularly review your connected devices to make sure only active, authorized devices are linked to your account. Remove any devices that are no longer in use to maintain security.
:::
