# Demographics

<div class="article-intro">

The **Demographics** dashboard gives you a visual snapshot of your congregation — who they are, how many there are, and how your membership is distributed across age groups, genders, marital statuses, and campuses. Every chart is interactive: click any segment to jump straight to a filtered People search showing exactly those people.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- People must exist in your directory. See [Adding People](./adding-people.md) or [Importing Data](./importing-data.md) to get started.
- To break the Campus chart down by campus, you first need campuses configured in [Settings → Campuses](../settings/campuses.md).

</div>

## Opening Demographics

From the **People** page, click **Demographics** in the page header. The page shows your total person count (**Total**) at the top, followed by the charts described below. Each donut chart also shows its total below the chart. Click **People** at the top right to go back to the directory.

## Charts

### Age

A stacked bar chart showing how many people fall into each age bracket: 0–3, 4–11, 12–18, 19–25, 26–35, 36–50, 51–64, and 65+. Each bar is split by gender — **Female**, **Male**, and, when anyone in that bracket has no gender recorded, **Unassigned** — so you can see the gender breakdown within each age group at a glance. People without a birth date aren't counted in this chart.

### Membership Types

A donut chart breaking down your congregation by membership status (for example, Member, Regular Attendee, Visitor, and so on). People with no status appear as **Unassigned**.

### Gender

A donut chart showing the proportion of female, male, and unassigned records in your directory.

### Marital Status

A donut chart showing the breakdown across marital status values (for example, Married, Single, Widowed).

### Campus

A donut chart showing how people are distributed across your church's campuses. People with no campus assigned appear as **Unassigned**, so a church without campuses sees a single **Unassigned** slice.

## Drilling Into the Data

Click any slice of a donut chart (or its name in the legend) or any bar segment on the age chart to navigate to the **People** page pre-filtered to show exactly those people. The filter stays applied when the page finishes loading, and the count above the list shows how many households and people matched. Use this to quickly build a contact list, run a bulk action, or export a segment — for example, click the **Visitor** slice in Membership Status to see all visitors and then send them to a workflow.

:::tip
After drilling into a filtered result, use **Save as List** in the People search to save that filter for future use. See [Saved Lists](./lists.md).
:::
