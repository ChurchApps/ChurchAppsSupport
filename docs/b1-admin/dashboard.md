# Dashboard

<div class="article-intro">

The Dashboard is the B1 Admin home page. It opens when you sign in and is built around your church's weekly service: the order of service for the coming plan, who is serving, who is in the room on Sunday, anything waiting on you, and your own tasks and groups.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- Sign in to [B1 Admin](https://admin.b1.church). Every signed-in user has a Dashboard, but what it shows depends on your [role and permissions](./settings/roles-permissions.md).
- The service sections fill in once you use [Plans](./serving/plans.md) and [Attendance](./attendance/index.md) or [Check-In](./attendance/check-in.md).

</div>

## Opening the Dashboard

- Sign in -- B1 Admin starts on the Dashboard.
- Click the small church logo at the top-left of any page.
- Open the [Jump menu](./introduction.md#getting-around-with-the-jump-menu) (click the search bar or press **Ctrl+K** / **Cmd+K**) and choose **Dashboard**.

## This Week's Service

The large card at the top shows one service plan: the next upcoming plan between last Sunday and this coming Sunday, or last Sunday's plan if nothing is coming up. If several plans fall on the same day, a plan that uses an order of service comes first, then the one with the earliest service time.

- **Date line** -- the plan's day, date and first service time (for example, *Sunday · November 15 · 9:00 AM*).
- **Title** -- the plan's name. If you can open the plan (you can edit plans, belong to the plan's ministry, or are serving on it), the title links to the [plan](./serving/plans.md).
- **Order of service** -- each item from the plan's [service order](./serving/service-order.md) with the clock time it starts, worked out from the first service time and each item's length. While the service is running, the current item is highlighted.

If there is no plan this week, the card reads **No service planned this week.**, and if the plan has no order of service it reads **No order of service on this plan.** People who can edit plans see a **Plans** link next to either message.

### Who is serving and who is in the room

Next to the order of service, the Dashboard adds what is happening around the service once there is data for it:

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **in the room** | How many people have checked in or been marked present. On Sunday it is today's count, with a **Live** marker; during the week it shows **Last Sunday**'s count. If no one was recorded by name, it uses that day's [headcounts](./attendance/headcount-entry.md). |
| **Serving Sunday** / **Serving this hour** | Everyone scheduled on the plan with their position, linked to their person record. Declined assignments are left out. |
| **First time** / **First time last Sunday** | First-time guests (people with a *Visitor* or *Guest* membership status, or checked in as a guest), grouped by household. |
| **Rooms** | Check-ins per class or room for that week, with a capacity bar when the group has a **Capacity** set. |

Before any of this exists, the card shows a short hint with links to **People**, **Attendance** and **Plans** (only the ones you can open).

:::info
The counts and names come from your attendance and serving data, so they only appear for people with permission to see them. For example, guest names and the serving list need permission to view people, and the room counts need permission to view attendance.
:::

## Needs Attention

When something is waiting on you, a **Needs attention** card lists it with a link:

- **pending calendar approvals** -- events and room or resource bookings that need approval. Opens [Approvals](./calendars/approvals.md). Shown to people who can edit content.
- **pending group join requests** -- people asking to join a group. Opens the [pending requests](./groups/group-join-requests.md) list. Shown to people who can edit group members.

The card is hidden when there is nothing to do.

## My Tasks and My Groups

At the bottom of the page:

- **My tasks** lists up to four open [tasks](./serving/tasks.md) assigned to you; click one to open it. Click **Add Task** to create a task right here (choose who it is about and who it is assigned to, then enter a title, due date and notes), or **All tasks** to open **Serving > My Work**.
- **My Groups** lists the groups you belong to; click one to open it. If you can manage groups or view people, **All groups** opens the full [Groups](./groups/index.md) list.

## Quick Actions

The setup shortcuts that used to sit on the Dashboard (such as **Add Your Church Logo**, **Set Up Online Giving** and **Create Your First Group**) now live in the Jump menu under **Quick Actions**. See [Quick Actions](./introduction.md#quick-actions).
