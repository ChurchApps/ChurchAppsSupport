# Notifications & Messages

<div class="article-intro">

B1 Admin keeps your notifications and private messages in the user menu at the top-right of every page. A red number on your profile picture shows how many unread notifications and messages are waiting, and it updates while you work, without refreshing the page.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- Sign in to [B1 Admin](https://admin.b1.church). Every signed-in user has notifications and messages.
- Notifications and messages are personal: you only see the ones sent to you.

</div>

## Opening the User Menu

Click your profile picture (or the arrow next to it) at the top-right. The menu shows your **Current church** and these items:

- **Messages** and **Notifications**, each with its own unread count
- **Edit Profile** -- **Edit Account** opens your [profile](./profile/managing-profile.md), and **Edit Church Profile** opens your directory profile on your church's B1 site
- **Theme Preferences** -- **Default**, **Classic** or **Custom** colors, and **Light Mode** or **Dark Mode**
- **Logout**, **Switch App** (B1.Church or Lessons.church) and **Switch Church** (lists the churches your account belongs to)

## Notifications

Notifications tell you about things that need you, for example:

- A [task](./serving/tasks.md) was assigned to you, or a group join request task was created for you
- You were scheduled to serve on a [plan](./serving/plans.md)
- A new submission arrived on a [form](./forms/managing-submissions.md) you manage
- Someone posted in one of your groups or sent an announcement

To read them:

1. Open the user menu and click **Notifications**.
2. Each notification shows a short message, how long ago it arrived (such as **1d**), and a **New** tag if you haven't seen it yet. Opening the list marks them as read and clears the count.
3. Click **View Details** (or the notification) to go to what it is about:
   - A task opens the task in B1 Admin.
   - A form submission opens the form's submissions in B1 Admin.
   - A serving assignment opens the plan on your church's B1 site in a new tab, where you can accept or decline it.
   - Other notifications open their link, if they have one, in a new tab.
4. Click the trash icon to delete one notification, or **Clear All** (and confirm) to delete them all.

When there is nothing left, the list reads **No notifications -- You're all caught up!**

:::tip
How you are notified (push, email, the in-app bell or text) and quiet hours are set in your [Notification Preferences](../b1-church/getting-started/notification-preferences.md) on your church's B1 site or in the B1 app.
:::

## Messages

**Messages** holds your private, one-to-one conversations with people in your church -- the same conversations you see on your church's B1 site and in the B1 app.

### Reading and replying

1. Open the user menu and click **Messages**. Each conversation shows the other person, the latest message (with **You:** in front if you sent it), when it was sent, and a **New** tag if it is unread.
2. Click a conversation to open it. Type in **Type a message...** and click the send arrow. The emoji button adds an emoji.
3. Click the back arrow to return to the list.

To delete a conversation, click its trash icon and confirm.

### Starting a new message

1. In **Messages**, click the round **+** button.
2. Under **Search for a person**, type a name and click **Search**.
3. Click the person, write your message, and send it.
