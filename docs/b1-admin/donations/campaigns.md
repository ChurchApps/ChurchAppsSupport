# Campaigns & Pledges

<div class="article-intro">

Campaigns let you run a fundraising drive toward a specific goal — a building fund, a missions trip, a special project — and track pledges from members alongside actual donations so you can see your real-time progress.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- Set up your [donation funds](funds) — each campaign is tied to a fund
- You need access to the Donations section of B1 Admin

</div>

## Opening Campaigns

In B1 Admin, open the [Jump menu](../introduction.md#getting-around-with-the-jump-menu) (the search bar at the top-left), expand **Donations**, and click **Campaigns**. You will see a table of all campaigns with their fund, dates, goal amount, total pledged, and total given so far. A summary line above the table shows the number of campaigns and the combined pledged and given amounts.

## Creating a Campaign

1. Click **Add Campaign** at the top of the page.
2. Fill in the campaign details:
   - **Name** *(required)* — the display name for this campaign (for example, "Building Fund 2026").
   - **Fund** — the donation fund this campaign is associated with.
   - **Start Date** / **End Date** — the fundraising window.
   - **Goal** — the target dollar amount for the campaign.
3. Click **Save**.

## Tracking Progress

Each campaign row shows:

- **Goal** — your target amount
- **Pledged** — the total amount members have pledged to give
- **Given** — the total amount actually donated to this campaign's fund during the campaign window
- **Progress** — a bar and percentage showing how far toward your goal you have come

Click a campaign's name to open the detail view. It shows the percentage of the goal reached and lists individual pledges with each donor's pledged and given amounts and a status (**Not Started**, **In Progress**, **Fulfilled**, **Beyond Pledged**, or **Non-Pledged** for donors who gave without pledging). Donors are listed by last name, then first name; click a column header to sort by that column instead. Click **Export** at the top of the detail view to download the pledge list as a CSV.

## Adding Pledges

Pledges are commitments from members to give toward a campaign. To record a pledge:

1. Open a campaign.
2. Click **Add Pledge** at the top of the page.
3. Select the **person** making the pledge.
4. Enter the pledge **amount**.
5. Optionally set a **date** for the pledge commitment.
6. Click **Save**.

Pledges appear in the campaign detail and contribute to the **Pledged** total in the campaign list. To change a pledge, click **Edit** on its row.

:::tip
Pledges are separate from actual donations. A pledge tracks a commitment; a donation records the actual gift. Both are shown on the campaign so you can see how well pledges are being fulfilled.
:::

## Editing or Deleting a Campaign

Click **Edit** on any campaign row (or **Edit Campaign** at the top of the campaign's detail view) to update its name, goal, dates, or fund. Click **Delete** to permanently remove the campaign and its pledges. Deleting a campaign does not delete any donations that were recorded to its fund.
