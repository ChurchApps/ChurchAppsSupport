# Calendars

<div class="article-intro">

The Calendars section in B1 Admin lets you create curated calendars for your church by pulling together events from your groups. Group leaders manage their own group's events, and as an admin you can build calendars that display those events on your B1.church website for your congregation to see.

</div>

## Viewing Your Calendars

1. In B1 Admin, open the [Jump menu](../introduction.md#getting-around-with-the-jump-menu) (the search bar at the top-left), expand **Calendars**, and click **Calendars**.
2. The Calendars page lists all your calendars in a table with their name and status. Use the **Find a calendar** box to filter the list; a line such as *2 of 5 calendars* shows how many match, with **Clear** to reset. If booking or event requests are waiting, a **pending approvals** link at the top opens [Approvals](./approvals.md).
3. Hover over a row to show its **Manage Events** link, which opens the calendar, and its **Edit** link, which lets you rename or delete it.

The Calendars section, and each page in it, needs the **Edit Content** permission. The **Calendars** menu holds **Calendars** (**/calendars**), **Availability** (**/calendars/availability**), **Rooms & Resources** (**/calendars/rooms**), **Approvals** (**/calendars/approvals**), and **Registrations** (**/registrations**). Two more permissions, both in the **Content** section of [Roles & Permissions](../settings/roles-permissions.md), widen or narrow what people can do here: **Resolve Calendar Conflicts & Approvals** lets someone decide requests on the Approvals page without **Edit Content**, and **View Registrations** / **Edit Registrations** control who can read and change registration rosters.

## What You Can Do

- **Add a new calendar** -- Click **Add Calendar** at the top of the page to create a calendar for a specific ministry, event type, or purpose. See [Creating Calendars](./creating-calendars.md) for step-by-step instructions.
- **Edit a calendar** -- Click **Manage Events** on any calendar to open it and manage which groups are included in it.
- **Publish calendars** -- Calendars can be displayed on your B1.church website so visitors can see upcoming events at your church.
- **Create curated calendars** -- Pull events from multiple groups into a single calendar view for a comprehensive look at everything happening at your church. See [Curated Calendar](./curated-calendar.md) for details.
- **Manage rooms and resources** -- Track bookable spaces and equipment and handle approval requests. See [Rooms & Resources](./rooms-resources.md) for details.
- **View availability** -- See all room and resource bookings on a single calendar and book spaces for upcoming events. See [Availability Calendar](./availability.md) for details.
- **Approve requests** -- Approve or reject room, resource, and event requests, with an optional reason. See [Calendar Approvals](./approvals.md).
- **Remind people** -- Send push, email, and text reminders before an event to its group or its registrants. See [Event Reminders](./event-reminders.md).
- **Take registrations** -- Turn on event registration, then work the roster: add attendees, export, cancel, restore, and promote. See [Enabling Event Registration](./creating-calendars.md#enabling-event-registration) and [Managing Registrations](./managing-registrations.md).
- **Charge for events** -- Priced attendee types, add-ons, discount codes, deposits, pay later, and recording payments and refunds. See [Paid Registrations](./paid-registrations.md).

:::info
Calendars work closely with [Groups](../groups/creating-groups.md). When groups have events scheduled, those events can be pulled into your calendars. See the [Creating Calendars](./creating-calendars.md) page to learn how to connect groups to calendars.
:::
