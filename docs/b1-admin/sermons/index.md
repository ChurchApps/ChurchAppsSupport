# Sermons

<div class="article-intro">

The Sermons section in B1 Admin lets you manage your church's sermon library and live streaming. You can add individual sermons, organize them into playlists, publish them as a podcast, schedule live stream services, choose the tabs shown beside your stream, and import existing content in bulk from YouTube, Vimeo, or a podcast feed.

</div>

:::info
To access the Sermons section, you need the **Edit Services** permission in the **Content** section of your role. Without it, Sermons is missing from your menu and its pages show "You don't have permission to view this page." The ready-made **Content Admin** role includes this permission along with **Edit Content** and **Host Chat**. If you do not see Sermons in your menu, ask your church administrator to grant you the appropriate role. See [Roles & Permissions](../settings/roles-permissions.md) for details on managing permissions.
:::

## What You Can Do

In the [Jump menu](../introduction.md#getting-around-with-the-jump-menu), **Sermons** has three pages: **Sermons** (**/sermons** -- your library, playlists, and podcast feed), **Live Stream Times** (**/sermons/times**), and **Settings** (**/sermons/settings**, titled **Stream Settings**). **Bulk Import** (**/sermons/bulk**) is reached from the **Add Sermon** button on the Sermons page. The section covers these areas:

### Sermons

Add and manage individual sermon entries in your library. Each sermon can include a title, description, publish date, duration, thumbnail, a link to video hosted on YouTube, Vimeo, or Facebook (or a custom embed URL), and an audio file for your podcast. See [Managing Sermons](./managing-sermons.md) for full details.

### Playlists

Organize your sermons into series or collections. Playlists make it easy for your congregation to browse through a sermon series in order. Each playlist has its own title, description, publish date, and thumbnail. See [Playlists](./playlists.md) for more information.

### Podcast Feed

Every church gets an automatic podcast RSS feed of its sermons that have an audio or video file attached. Copy the feed address from the **Podcast Feed** panel and submit it to Apple Podcasts, Spotify, or other directories. See [Podcast Feed](./podcast-feed.md).

### Live Stream Times

Configure your church's live streaming schedule. Set up recurring weekly services or one-time events, set when chat opens and closes, start the video early for a countdown, and choose which sermon or live URL plays. Visitors see the next scheduled service time on your streaming page. See [Live Streaming](./live-streaming.md) for setup instructions.

### Stream Settings

The **Settings** page under **Sermons** controls the **Content Tabs** -- the built-in **Chat** and **Prayer** tabs and your own **External URL** links -- shown beside your live stream. See [Stream Settings](./stream-settings.md).

### Bulk Import

Quickly populate your sermon library by importing videos from a YouTube channel or Vimeo channel, or episodes from a podcast RSS feed. This is the fastest way to migrate your existing sermon content into B1. See [Bulk Import](./bulk-import.md) to get started.

:::tip
If you are setting up sermons for the first time, start by creating a [playlist](./playlists.md), then either [add sermons individually](./managing-sermons.md) or [import them in bulk](./bulk-import.md). The [Build Your Sermon Library & Live Stream](../guides/sermon-library.md) guide walks through the whole process in order.
:::
