# Playlists

<div class="article-intro">

Playlists let you organize your sermons into series or collections. When visitors browse your sermon library on your B1.church website or in the member app, playlists help them find and follow along with a complete sermon series in the right order. A playlist's thumbnail also stands in for any sermon in it that has no image of its own.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- You need the **Edit Services** permission in the **Content** section of your role. See [Roles & Permissions](../settings/roles-permissions.md) if you do not have access.
- Have a title, description, and a 16:9 thumbnail image (1280x720 pixels recommended) ready for your playlist

</div>

## Viewing Playlists

1. In B1 Admin, open the [Jump menu](../introduction.md#getting-around-with-the-jump-menu) (the search bar at the top-left), expand **Sermons**, and click **Sermons**.
2. Find the **Playlists** panel in the right-hand column of the **Sermons** page (above the **Podcast Feed** panel).
3. The panel lists your playlists by title, newest **Publish Date** first, with a count beside the **Playlists** heading. When you have more than five, a **Search playlists...** box appears above the list; it matches against both the title and the description. If nothing matches you see "No playlists match your search".

Hover over a playlist to show its **Edit** button.

## Creating a Playlist

1. Click **Add playlist** at the top of the **Playlists** panel, or, if you have no playlists yet, **Create First Playlist** in the empty panel. A **Create New Playlist** form opens above the panel. (If a sermon form was open, it closes.)
2. Under **Basic Information**, enter a **Playlist Title** -- for example, the sermon series title like "Faith Foundations" or "Summer in the Psalms" -- and a **Description** to give visitors a brief overview of the series.
3. Under **Publishing & Schedule**, set the **Publish Date**. New playlists start with today's date. See [What the Publish Date Does](#what-the-publish-date-does) below.
4. Under **Thumbnail Image**, click **Add Thumbnail**. An image editor opens above the form: choose a picture, crop it to 16:9, and click its save button. The form shows the new image and the button changes to **Change Thumbnail**. "Recommended size: 1280x720 pixels (16:9 aspect ratio)".
5. Click **Save** to create your playlist, or **Cancel** to discard it.

:::tip
Create your playlists first before adding sermons. This way you can assign each sermon to the correct playlist as you go, rather than reorganizing later.
:::

## Editing a Playlist

1. Hover over a playlist in the **Playlists** panel and click **Edit**. The **Edit Playlist** form opens above the panel.
2. Change any of the fields. Use **Change Thumbnail** to crop a new image.
3. Click **Save**, or **Cancel** to leave the playlist as it was.

## Deleting a Playlist

1. Open the playlist with **Edit** and click **Delete**.
2. Confirm "Are you sure you wish to delete this playlist?".

Deleting a playlist does not delete the sermons in it. They stay in your library and show **No Playlist** in the sermon list, so you can open each one and assign it to another playlist. Delete the sermons themselves with [Delete Selected](./managing-sermons.md#deleting-several-sermons-at-once) if you do not want to keep them.

## What the Publish Date Does

The playlist's **Publish Date** orders your playlists (newest first, in B1 Admin and in the member app) and is shown beside the series in the app. It does not hide the playlist: a playlist is listed as soon as you save it. To hold a series back until launch day, give its *sermons* a future **Publish Date** instead -- a sermon stays off your website, the app, and the podcast feed until its own publish date arrives. See [Sermon Details](./managing-sermons.md#sermon-details).

## Adding Sermons to a Playlist

When you [add or edit a sermon](./managing-sermons.md), you assign it to a playlist using the **Playlist** dropdown (or leave it at **None**). All sermons assigned to the same playlist are grouped together and displayed newest first on your website and in the app. To list one sermon in a second series as well, open it and use **Add to another playlist**, which saves a copy of the sermon in that playlist.

:::info
You can also assign sermons to playlists during [Bulk Import](./bulk-import.md). Choose a playlist from the **Import Into Playlist** dropdown before clicking **Import**; a playlist is required there.
:::

## How Playlists Appear to Members

Playlists are displayed on your B1.church website and in the member app as browsable collections. Visitors can:

- See all available sermon series at a glance, each with its thumbnail
- Click into a playlist to view all sermons in that series, newest first
- Watch or listen to sermons in order

See [Sermons (Members)](../../b1-church/content/sermons.md) for the member view.
