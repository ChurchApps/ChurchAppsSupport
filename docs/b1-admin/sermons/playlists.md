# Playlists

<div class="article-intro">

Playlists let you organize your sermons into series or collections. When visitors browse your sermon library on your B1.church website, playlists help them find and follow along with a complete sermon series in the right order.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- You need the **Edit Services** permission in the **Content** section of your role. See [Roles & Permissions](../settings/roles-permissions.md) if you do not have access.
- Have a name, description, and thumbnail image ready for your playlist

</div>

## Viewing Playlists

1. In B1 Admin, open the [Jump menu](../introduction.md#getting-around-with-the-jump-menu) (the search bar at the top-left), expand **Sermons**, and click **Sermons**.
2. Find the **Playlists** panel on the **Sermons** page (alongside the sermon list).
3. You will see a list of all your existing playlists by name, with a count beside the **Playlists** heading. When you have more than five, a **Search playlists...** box appears above the list.

## Creating a Playlist

1. Click the **Create First Playlist** button (if you have no playlists yet) or click the **Add playlist** button in the **Playlists** panel. A **Create New Playlist** form opens above the panel.
2. Enter a **Playlist Title** -- for example, the sermon series title like "Faith Foundations" or "Summer in the Psalms."
3. Add a **Description** to give visitors a brief overview of the series.
4. Set a **Publish Date** to control when the playlist becomes visible.
5. Click **Add Thumbnail** to upload and crop a **Thumbnail Image** to represent the series visually (1280x720 pixels, 16:9, is recommended).
6. Click **Save** to create your playlist.

## Editing or Deleting a Playlist

Hover over a playlist in the **Playlists** panel and click **Edit**. Change any of the fields (use **Change Thumbnail** for a new image) and click **Save**, or click **Delete** and confirm to remove the playlist.

:::tip
Create your playlists first before adding sermons. This way you can assign each sermon to the correct playlist as you go, rather than reorganizing later.
:::

## Adding Sermons to a Playlist

When you [add or edit a sermon](./managing-sermons.md), you assign it to a playlist using the **Playlist** dropdown. All sermons assigned to the same playlist are grouped together and displayed in order on your website.

:::info
You can also assign sermons to playlists during [Bulk Import](./bulk-import.md). When importing from YouTube or Vimeo, select a playlist from the **Import Into Playlist** dropdown before clicking **Import**.
:::

## How Playlists Appear on Your Website

Playlists are displayed on your B1.church website as browsable collections. Visitors can:

- See all available sermon series at a glance
- Click into a playlist to view all sermons in that series
- Watch or listen to sermons in order
