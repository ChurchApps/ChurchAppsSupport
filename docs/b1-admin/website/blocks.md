# Reusable Blocks

<div class="article-intro">

Blocks are pieces of website content you build once and reuse on as many pages as you like -- a call-to-action banner, a contact strip, or a service-times panel. When you edit a block, every page that uses it shows the change.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- You need permission to edit website content
- Use a computer or tablet; the block editor is the same full-screen editor used for pages

</div>

## Opening Blocks

1. In B1 Admin, open the [Jump menu](../introduction.md#getting-around-with-the-jump-menu) (the search bar at the top-left) and expand **Website**.
2. Click **Blocks**. The **Reusable Blocks** page lists each block with its **Name** and **Type**.

If your church has more than one website, use the site switcher at the top of the page to see the blocks for each site. Your site footer is a block too, but it is edited from **Site Footer** in [Appearance](./appearance.md) rather than listed here.

## Adding a Block

1. Click **Add Block**.
2. Enter a **Name** your team will recognize.
3. Choose the **Block Type**:
   - **Element(s)** -- one or more elements (text, images, buttons, and so on) that you drop inside a section on a page.
   - **Section(s)** -- one or more whole sections that you drop onto a page between its other sections.
4. Click **Save**. The block appears in the list.

## Building a Block's Content

1. Click the block's name, or **Edit** on its row. The block opens in the [page editor](./page-editor.md).
2. Add and style content the same way you would on a page, starting with a section.
3. Changes save automatically. Click **Exit** in the toolbar to return to the Blocks list.

## Using a Block on a Page

1. Open a page in the [page editor](./page-editor.md) and click **Add Content**.
2. Select the **Blocks** tab in the **Add Elements** panel. (The tab only appears once you have at least one block.)
3. Drag the block onto the page: an element block into a section, or a section block between sections.

The page shows the block's current content. To change it, edit the block itself from the **Blocks** page; every page that uses it updates.

## Renaming or Deleting a Block

1. Click **Rename** on the block's row.
2. Change the **Name** or **Block Type** and click **Save**, or click **Delete** and confirm to remove the block permanently.

:::warning
Pages that use a deleted block no longer show its content. Check your pages before deleting a block that is in use.
:::
