# Reports

<div class="article-intro">

The Reports page gives you quick access to pre-built reports that help you understand your church data. Use these reports to track attendance trends, monitor giving activity, and stay connected with your congregation through birthday celebrations.

</div>

## Available Reports

Reports don't have an entry in the B1 Admin navigation menu -- go directly to the URL for the report you want (bookmarking it is the easiest way to get back). Each report opens straight to its filters.

- **[Birthday Report](./birthday-report)** (`admin.b1.church/reports/birthdays`) -- View upcoming birthdays for your congregation members.
- **[Anniversary Report](./anniversary-report)** (`admin.b1.church/reports/anniversaries`) -- View upcoming wedding anniversaries for your congregation members.
- **[Attendance Trend](./attendance-reports)** (`admin.b1.church/reports/attendanceTrend`) -- See how attendance patterns change over time across your services.
- **[Group Attendance](./attendance-reports)** (`admin.b1.church/reports/groupAttendance`) -- View attendance totals by group for a selected date range.
- **[Daily Group Attendance](./attendance-reports)** (`admin.b1.church/reports/dailyGroupAttendance`) -- Get a day-by-day breakdown of group attendance data.
- **[Donation Summary](./donation-summary)** (`admin.b1.church/reports/donationSummary`) -- Review giving totals and trends for financial planning.
- **[Audit Log](./audit-log)** -- Track all changes and actions across your church management system (found under Settings > Audit Log).

## Using Reports

1. Go directly to the report's URL, such as **admin.b1.church/reports/birthdays**.
2. Use the **filters** at the top of the report to narrow the data by date range, group, service, or other criteria.
3. Review the results displayed in the report.

:::tip
Run reports regularly to stay on top of attendance trends, upcoming birthdays, and giving patterns. This data helps you plan ministries and outreach more effectively.
:::

:::info
Report data depends on consistent data entry. Make sure your team is [tracking attendance](../attendance/tracking-attendance.md), [recording donations](../donations/recording-donations.md), and keeping [people records](../people/adding-people.md) up to date for the most accurate results.
:::
