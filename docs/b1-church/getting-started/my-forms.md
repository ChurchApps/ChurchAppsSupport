# My Forms

<div class="article-intro">

**My Forms** keeps a record of the forms you have filled out for your church -- a prayer request card, a trip permission slip, a survey -- and lets you reread your answers whenever you need them.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- You must be [logged in](./logging-in.md).
- You need to have submitted at least one form. Until then the screen says "You haven't submitted any forms yet."

</div>

## Opening My Forms

Open the **Me** page and find the **My Forms** section. It lists your most recent submissions with the date of each. Tap a form, or **All forms**, to open the **My Forms** screen (`/mobile/myForms`), headed "Forms you've submitted. Tap one to see your answers." Tap **Back to Me** to return.

If your church has turned on **My Forms** for Home (see [Home Screen Sections](../../b1-admin/settings/mobile-app.md#home-screen-sections)), the same section also appears on the [Home dashboard](./navigating.md#home) with your two newest submissions and an **All forms** link. It is always on the Me page regardless.

## Reading Your Answers

Submissions are listed newest first, each with the form's name and the date you submitted it.

1. Tap a form to expand it. Each question you answered appears with your answer underneath; questions you left blank are not shown. If none of your answers had a value, the entry says "No answers to show."
2. Tap the form again to collapse it.

Your answers are read-only. If something needs to change -- a wrong phone number, say -- contact your church office; staff can update the submission in B1 Admin.

## Which Forms Are Listed

My Forms shows:

- Stand-alone forms you filled out yourself while signed in, including forms linked to your groups (see [Linked Forms](../groups/group-details.md#linked-forms)).
- Stand-alone form submissions that are linked to your person record -- for example, one matched to you by your email address, or one church staff [moved to you](../../b1-admin/forms/managing-submissions.md#changing-the-person-on-a-submission).

It does not show forms that staff complete about you on your record in B1 Admin, or event registration answers (those live with your [registrations](../events/my-registrations.md)).
