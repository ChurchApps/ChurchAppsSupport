# Filling Out Forms

<div class="article-intro">

Churches build forms in B1 Admin for all sorts of things -- a connect card, a prayer request, a camp permission slip, a survey -- and share them as a link or QR code, or attach them to a group. Every form opens on your church's B1.church site at `/forms/` followed by the form's ID, where you answer the questions and submit.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- You need the form's link or QR code from your church, or a group that has the form linked to it.
- Public forms need no account. A **Restricted** form asks you to sign in first.

</div>

## Opening a Form

You can reach a form in three ways:

- **A link or QR code** your church shares -- in a bulletin, an email, a text, or on a slide. It opens the form on your church's website.
- **A group's Forms section** in the member portal -- tap **Fill out** next to the form (see [Linked Forms](../groups/group-details.md#linked-forms)). This opens the same website form page.
- **An event's registration questions** -- these are a form too, but you answer them as part of [registering](../events/registering.md), or later from [My Registrations](../events/my-registrations.md#answering-questions-later).

The page shows the form's name, its header photo if the church added one, and the church's description or instructions, followed by the questions.

If the form isn't open, you'll see one of these instead of the questions:

- "Login to view this form." -- the form is **Restricted** to signed-in members. Tap **Login**; you come back to the form after signing in.
- "This form isn't available until" a date -- the church set a start date. Check back then.
- "This form closed on" a date -- the church's deadline has passed.
- "This form could not be loaded." -- the link is wrong or the form was deleted; ask your church for a new link.

## Answering the Questions

Each question is labelled with its title and, when the church added one, a hint or placeholder. Depending on how the church built the form you may see short answer boxes, longer text areas, multiple-choice and **Yes**/**No** lists, checkboxes, number, date, phone, and email fields, and section headings that group questions together. Required questions must be answered before you can submit; if you skip one, a message names it -- for example "Email is required".

Your church picks one of two layouts:

- **Standard** -- every question is on one page. Answer them in any order and tap **Submit** at the bottom (**Cancel** leaves the form and returns to the church's home page).
- **Conversational** -- one question at a time, with a progress bar and "2 of 6" above the question. Answer and tap **Continue** to move on, **Back** to change an earlier answer, and **Submit** on the last question. A required question has to be answered before **Continue** works.

If you aren't signed in, you also complete an **I'm not a robot** check before submitting ("Please complete the reCAPTCHA verification" reminds you if you forget).

## After You Submit

The questions are replaced by the church's thank-you message, or "Your form has been successfully submitted." when the church didn't write one. Your church sees the submission in B1 Admin right away and may follow up by email if it set that up.

If you were signed in, the submission is linked to your church record and appears under [My Forms](./my-forms.md) on your Me page, where you can reread your answers. Submissions made while signed out are matched to you by email address when the church reviews them.

:::info
Forms are one-way: you can't edit or withdraw a submission yourself. If you made a mistake, contact your church office -- staff can correct it in B1 Admin.
:::
