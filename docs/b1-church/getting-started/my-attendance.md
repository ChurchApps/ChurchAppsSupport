# My Attendance

<div class="article-intro">

**My Attendance** shows your own check-in history -- every service and group you were checked in to over the last 12 months, grouped by month -- along with a quick count of how often you attended in the last 90 days.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- You must be [logged in](./logging-in.md).
- Attendance is recorded when you [check in](../checkin/self-checkin.md) from the portal, a volunteer checks you in at a [B1 Checkin](/docs/b1-checkin/) kiosk, or a group leader [takes attendance](../groups/group-details.md#meeting-notes-and-attendance). Until something is recorded, the screen says "No attendance recorded in the last 12 months."

</div>

## Opening My Attendance

Open the **Me** page and find the **My Attendance** section. It shows "*N* times in the last 90 days" followed by your most recent check-ins. Tap a row, or **History**, to open the **My Attendance** screen (`/mobile/myAttendance`), headed "Services and groups you were checked in to over the last 12 months." Tap **Back to Me** to return.

If your church has turned on **My Attendance** for Home (see [Home Screen Sections](../../b1-admin/settings/mobile-app.md#home-screen-sections)), the same count and your two most recent visits also appear on the [Home dashboard](./navigating.md#home) with a **History** link. The section is always on the Me page regardless.

## Reading the List

Check-ins are grouped under month headings (for example "November 2026"), newest first. Each row shows:

- The **group** you were checked in to, when it was a group you belong to -- "Kids Room 1" or "Tuesday Bible Study", for example. For a check-in with no group, or a group you have since left, the row shows the **service** instead (such as "Sunday Morning").
- The **date**, followed by the service or service time when it adds something the first line doesn't say -- for example "Nov 23, 2026 · 9:30 AM".

The list is yours alone: it only ever shows your own records, not your household's. To see a child's attendance, ask your church office.

:::info
Only the last 12 months are shown. Your church keeps the full history in B1 Admin -- see [Attendance Reports](../../b1-admin/attendance/index.md).
:::
