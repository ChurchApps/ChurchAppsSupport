# The Me Page

<div class="article-intro">

The **Me** page is your personal dashboard in the member portal — one page with everything coming up for you (serving assignments, event registrations, and your groups' upcoming events) along with your household, groups, giving, submitted forms, attendance history, registrations, and recent notifications.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- You need to be [logged in](./logging-in.md) — the Me page only appears for signed-in members

</div>

## Opening the Me Page

Tap **More** in the tab bar, then tap **Me** under **Account**. The page opens with your photo, your name, and your membership status, along with two buttons:

- **Edit Profile** -- See [Editing Your Profile](./editing-your-profile.md).
- **Settings** -- Opens your [Notification Preferences](./notification-preferences.md).

If you are signed out, you'll see "Sign in to see your week" and a **Sign In** button instead.

## Upcoming

The **Upcoming** section lists your next three items in date order, each tagged with a type badge. If you have more, tap **All upcoming** to see the full list, and **Back to Me** to return.

- **Serving** -- Your volunteer positions on upcoming service plans. Tap one to open the plan.
- **Registration** -- Events you have [registered for](../events/registering). Tap through to your [registrations](../events/my-registrations).
- **Event** -- Upcoming events from groups you belong to. Tap one to open the group's events.

If an event or registration has a photo, a small thumbnail of it appears at the start of the row.

If nothing is scheduled, you'll see "Nothing coming up" — your serving times, events, and registrations will appear here as they are scheduled.

## Household, Groups, and Giving

- **Household** -- The other people in your household. Tap a name to view their profile, or tap **Manage** to edit your household from your profile. Hidden if no one else is linked to your household.
- **My Groups** -- The groups you belong to. Tap a group to open it, or tap **All groups** for the full list.
- **Giving** -- How much you have given this year and how many gifts. Tap **History** to open the giving page. Hidden if your church does not accept online donations, or if your church has limited members' giving history to an earlier year or hidden it (see [Donation History](../giving/donation-history.md#if-your-church-limits-giving-history)).

## My Forms

The **My Forms** section lists the forms you've most recently submitted, with the date of each. If you haven't submitted any, you'll see "You haven't submitted any forms yet."

Tap a form or **All forms** to open the **My Forms** page, which lists your submitted forms, newest first. Tap a form to expand it and see your answers; tap it again to collapse it. Your answers are read-only here -- contact your church if something needs to change.

:::info
My Forms shows forms you filled out yourself. Forms that staff complete about you in B1 Admin are not listed.
:::

## My Attendance

The **My Attendance** section shows how many times you attended in the last 90 days, followed by your most recent check-ins. Each row shows the group name (or the service, if you checked in to a service) and the date.

Tap a row or **History** to open the **My Attendance** page, which lists the services and groups you were checked in to over the last 12 months, grouped by month. If nothing was recorded, you'll see "No attendance recorded in the last 12 months."

## Requests & Registrations

The **Requests & registrations** section shows how many event registrations you have, with a **Registrations** link to manage them.

If your church has bookable rooms or resources, two more links appear:

- **Request an Event** -- Ask to hold an event with the rooms and equipment you need. See [Requesting Events & Rooms](../events/requesting-events).
- **My Requests** -- Track the status of your requests and cancel pending ones.

If your church hasn't set up rooms or resources, these links are hidden.

## Inbox

The **Inbox** section shows your two most recent notifications so you can catch up without opening each area of the portal. Tap **Open inbox** to see all of your notifications and messages, or tap **Messages** to go to your private conversations.
