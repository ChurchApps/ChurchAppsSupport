# My Tasks and Cards

<div class="article-intro">

When church staff assign you follow-up work -- a workflow card such as "Call a first-time visitor", or a plain task -- it shows up in the member portal under **My tasks**. Open a card to see who it is about and how to reach them, add notes, mark it **Complete**, or snooze it for a few days. You never need to open B1 Admin.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- You must be [logged in](./logging-in.md). Signed out, the screen says "Sign in to see your tasks".
- Someone at your church must have assigned a card or task to you. Until then the screen says "Nothing is assigned to you right now." and the sections that lead here are hidden.

</div>

## Where Your Tasks Appear

Your open cards and tasks show in three places, always newest first:

- **Home** -- a **Your follow-ups** section with your two newest items (cards first), and an **All** link.
- **Me** -- a **My cards** section and a **My tasks** section, each with its three newest items and an **All cards** / **All tasks** link.
- **My tasks** (`/mobile/myTasks`) -- the full list, with a **Cards** section and a **Tasks** section. Tap **Back to Me** to return.

Each row shows the item's title (or, for a task without a title, the person it is about). A card's row shows its due date ("Due" and the date) and a badge when it is **Overdue** or **Snoozed**; a task's row shows who it is about.

When a notification arrives about a task or card -- a new assignment, or a new note on one -- tapping it opens **My tasks** with that item already open, whether you tap it in your inbox or as a push notification.

## Working a Card

Cards come from the workflows church staff set up in B1 Admin (for example, a visitor follow-up workflow). Tap a card to open it. The sheet shows:

- **Step** -- the workflow step the card is on (shown when you can view your church's task boards).
- **Due** -- its due date.
- **Snoozed until** -- when you have snoozed it, the date it comes back.
- **About** -- the person the card is about. When the directory shares that person with you, their name is a link to their [directory profile](../community/member-directory.md).
- **Call** and **Email** -- the person's mobile number and email address, when they are on file and shared with you. Tap the number to call, or the address to start an email.
- **Created** -- when the card was created.

Then take an action:

- **Complete** -- finishes the card and closes the sheet. If your church set up named outcomes for this step (for example **Reached** and **No answer**), a button for each outcome appears in place of **Complete**; tap the one that fits and the card moves on accordingly.
- **Snooze** -- tap **1 day**, **3 days**, or **7 days** to set the card aside. It stays in your list marked **Snoozed** and shows **Snoozed until** with the return date.
- **Notes** -- a running thread of notes on this card. Type a note and send it; staff watching the card see it in B1 Admin, and their notes show here. The first note you add starts the thread.

If an action fails you'll see "That didn't work. Try again." under the buttons.

## Viewing a Task

Plain tasks (ones staff created outside a workflow) open read-only. The sheet shows **From** (who created it), **About** (the person it concerns, with the same **Call** and **Email** rows), **Created**, and the task's existing **Notes**, if any. You can read notes and add to a thread that already exists, but completing or closing a plain task still happens in B1 Admin -- ask the person who assigned it if you need it closed.

:::info
Only items assigned to you personally are listed. Tasks assigned to a group you belong to, or ones you created yourself for someone else, don't appear here.
:::
