# Song Library

<div class="article-intro">

The **Songs** screen lists every song your worship team has added in B1 Admin, in every key it has been arranged for. Search by title or artist, tap a key, and the same song sheet you get from a service plan opens -- lyrics with chords you can transpose, chord chart PDFs, rehearsal audio, and links to the song on streaming services.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- You must be [logged in](../getting-started/logging-in.md). Signed out, the screen says "Sign in to see the song library" -- the library is open to anyone signed in to your church, not just worship team members.
- Your church must have added a **Songs** tab to the member portal in B1 Admin (**Mobile → Navigation**). If you don't see it in the tab bar or on the **More** page, ask your church to turn it on.
- Songs come from B1 Admin under **Serving › Songs**. Until the worship team adds some, the screen says "No songs yet. Songs added in B1 Admin under Serving › Songs appear here."

</div>

## Opening the Library

Tap **Songs** in the tab bar or on the [More page](../getting-started/navigating.md#the-more-page) (it opens at `/mobile/songs`). Each song is listed with its title and, when known, its artist. Under the title is a row of pills, one for each key the song has been arranged in -- for example **Key of G** or **Key of D**. When a song has more than one arrangement, the pill also names the arrangement (such as "Acoustic") or shows the short description your worship leader gave that key, so you can tell them apart.

The list shows the whole library at once (up to 500 keys), sorted by title and then artist.

## Searching

Type in **Search songs** at the top. The list narrows as you type to songs whose title or artist contains your words, in either order, and the matching text is highlighted in each result. Clear the box to see everything again. If nothing matches, the screen says "No songs match your search."

## Opening a Song

Tap a key pill to open the song sheet. It is the same sheet that opens when you tap a song in a plan's [service order](./service-order.md#viewing-song-lyrics-and-chords), so what you see depends on what the worship team has filled in:

- The song's title, with the artist underneath.
- **Sequence** -- the order of the arrangement's parts (for example "Intro, Verse 1, Chorus, Verse 2, Chorus, Bridge, Chorus"), when one is set.
- **Key Signature** -- a row of pills for all twelve keys (C, C#, D, D#, E, F, F#, G, G#, A, A#, B), with the arrangement's own key selected. Tap another key to transpose every chord in the lyrics on the fly -- useful when your instrument or voice needs a different key than the one planned. Shown only when the song has lyrics and a known key.
- **Lyrics** -- the words with the chords written above them.
- **Files** -- chord chart PDFs your worship leader uploaded for this arrangement, links added to this key (a link to an audio file plays right in the sheet), and, when the song is connected to PraiseCharts, the PraiseCharts products available in this key. Tap one to open or download it.
- **Audio Tracks** -- rehearsal recordings uploaded for the arrangement, each with its own player.
- Song facts -- the cover picture plus **Artist**, **Release Date**, **Album**, **Language**, **BPM**, **Key Signature**, **Keys**, **Meter**, and **Length** (minutes:seconds), whichever are known.
- **Links** -- logos linking to the song on services such as Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, CCLI, Genius, Hymnary, MusicBrainz, and PraiseCharts. They open in a new tab.

Tap **Close** to go back to the list.

:::tip
The library is handy for practicing between services. Open the song in the key you'll be playing, transpose it if needed, and play the rehearsal track from **Audio Tracks** while you follow the chords.
:::

:::info
Members can read and play everything here but can't change it. Lyrics, keys, charts, and audio are managed by the worship team in B1 Admin -- see [Songs (B1 Admin)](../../b1-admin/serving/songs.md).
:::
