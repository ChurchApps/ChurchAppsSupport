# Service Order

<div class="article-intro">

When you open a plan, the **Service Order** section shows the first few items for that service -- tap **Full service order** to see the whole sequence. This gives you and your team a shared view of the service flow, including timing, song details, and descriptions for each element.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- You must be [logged in](../getting-started/logging-in.md) to your B1.church account.
- Open a plan from your [Plans list](./viewing-plans.md) to view its service order.

</div>

## What the Service Order Shows

The service order is displayed as a list of items in the order they will occur during the service. Each item includes:

- **Cumulative time** -- The running time from the start of the service, so you know when each element is expected to begin.
- **Item label and description** -- The name of the element (such as a song title, prayer, or announcement) along with any notes or descriptions.
- **Duration** -- How long the item is expected to take.

Items are organized under **headers** that group related elements together (for example, "Worship Set" or "Message"). Each header shows the total time of its items. This makes it easy to scan the overall structure of the service.

Songs show a music note before their title. An item with a link or file attached opens in a pop-up when you tap it -- a video plays there, an audio link gets a player, and documents and pictures are shown in the pop-up; tap **Close** to go back. If an item can't be shown, the pop-up says so ("Preview not available for this content."). The start time of each item is shown on the left and its length on the right. If your church turned on **Show volunteer names** for the plan, items tied to a serving position also list who is filling it.

Tapping a lesson or curriculum section opens it in a pop-up that lists the actions inside it. Tap an action to see its content -- the words to say, a video to play, an activity -- and use the back arrow (or **Back**) to return to the list, so you can step through everything your team will walk through.

## Viewing Song Lyrics and Chords

Click a song in the service order to open its details: the artist, the arrangement's **Sequence** (for example, Intro, Verse, Chorus, Bridge) when one is set, the **Key Signature** pills, the **Lyrics** (with chords when the song has them), song facts -- the cover picture plus **Artist**, **Release Date**, **Album**, **Language**, **BPM**, **Key Signature**, **Keys**, **Meter**, and **Length**, whichever are known -- and **Links** to the song on services like Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, CCLI, Genius, Hymnary, MusicBrainz, and PraiseCharts. Tap one of the twelve keys under **Key Signature** to transpose the chords on the fly -- handy if your instrument or vocal range needs a different key than the one the worship leader planned for (the pills only appear when the song has lyrics and a known key). Tap **Close** when you're done. Only songs the worship team has linked to the song library open this way; a song typed in as plain text has nothing to open. If your church has added a **Songs** tab, the same sheet opens from the [Song Library](./songs.md) for every song in every key.

Below the lyrics, **Files** lists chord chart PDFs your worship leader uploaded for the arrangement, links added to this key (a link to an audio file plays right in the sheet), and, when the song is connected to PraiseCharts, the PraiseCharts products available in this key. Tap one to open or download it. **Audio Tracks** lists rehearsal recordings, which play right in the panel. Staff upload these files on the song's arrangement in B1 Admin (see [Rehearsal Audio and Chart PDFs](../../b1-admin/serving/songs.md#rehearsal-audio-and-chart-pdfs)).

## Curriculum and Lesson Content

If the plan has associated curriculum or lesson content from a provider like [Lessons.church](../content/lessons.md), you will see a preview of that content within the service order. This can include lesson sections, presentation materials, and other resources your team needs for the service.

## Printing the Service Order

The B1.church member app does not have a print option for plans. If you need a printed copy with the team roster and the full service order, ask a staff member to print it from B1 Admin. Staff can also include a chord chart page for each song in that printout. See [Printing Plans](../../b1-admin/serving/plans.md#printing-plans).
