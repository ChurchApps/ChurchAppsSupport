# Viewing Plans

<div class="article-intro">

The Plans page gives you a clear view of all the service plans you are assigned to. It shows what needs your reply, what is coming up, and what you have served, and lets you open any plan for the details.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- You must be [logged in](../getting-started/logging-in.md) to your B1.church account.
- You must be assigned to at least one service plan. If you do not see any plans, contact your church administrator or ministry leader.

</div>

## Accessing Your Plans

1. Tap **Plans** in the tab bar (when your church has given it a slot), on the [More page](../getting-started/navigating.md#the-more-page), or tap **All plans** in the **You're serving** section on Home.
2. You will see the plans you are scheduled on.

## The Plans Page

The top of the page shows how many of your upcoming assignments are confirmed ("3 confirmed", counting both accepted and confirmed ones) and, when any are waiting, how many need a reply ("· 1 needs a reply"), plus a **Sign up to serve** button that opens the [volunteer signup](../serving/volunteer-signup.md) page. Below that, the page is organized into sections:

- **Needs your reply** -- Assignments you have not answered yet, each with **Accept** and **Decline** buttons so you can respond without opening the plan. Tapping **Decline** asks you to confirm first, and lets you add a reason. When more than one request is waiting, tap **Accept all** to accept them all at once. Only shown when something is waiting on you.
- **Upcoming** -- Your next three assignments that you have already answered. Tap **All upcoming** (shown once you have more than three upcoming assignments in total) to see the full list, which includes the ones still waiting for a reply with their **Needs reply** badge; tap **Back to Plans** to return. If nothing is scheduled, it says "No upcoming serving times."
- **Blockout dates** -- Dates when you are unavailable for serving. Tap **Add dates**, choose a **Start date** and **End date** (both start on today), and tap **Save**. Each blockout shows its range (or the single date), and only blockouts that haven't ended yet are listed ("No blockout dates." when there are none). Tap **Remove** next to a blockout, then confirm **Delete** in the **Delete blockout date?** sheet to delete it. Your team leaders see these dates so they can schedule around you, and the [volunteer signup](../serving/volunteer-signup.md#conflict-flags-in-the-member-portal) screen flags positions that fall on them.
- **Scheduling preferences** -- How often and when you like to serve. See [Scheduling Preferences](#scheduling-preferences).
- **Serving calendar** -- A calendar subscription of the times you have accepted. See [Adding Your Serving Times to Your Calendar](#serving-calendar).
- **Past** -- How many times you were scheduled this year and your most recent past assignment ("5 times in 2026 · Last served Nov 16, 2026, Greeter"), or "No past assignments." if you haven't served yet. The count includes every past assignment, whatever its status. Tap **Past assignments** to see the full list; use the **Year** pills (or **All**) to filter it. Past assignments don't show a status badge unless you declined them.

Each assignment shows the **service date**, then the **plan name** and your **position** ("Sunday Morning · Greeter"), and a status badge -- **Accepted**, **Confirmed**, **Declined**, **Pending Response**, or **Needs reply** if you have not responded yet.

:::info
Your church may send **reminders** before the services you are scheduled for, by email and as an app notification. If your church sends texts, you can also get them by text by turning on **Text messages** in your [Notification Preferences](../getting-started/notification-preferences.md). If you have not responded yet, you can **Accept** or **Decline** right from the reminder email — no sign-in needed.
:::

## Accepting Several Requests at Once

When more than one assignment is waiting in **Needs your reply**, an **Accept all** link appears at the top of that section. Tap it to accept every waiting request in one step. If any of them can't be accepted, those stay in the list with a note such as "Accepted 2 of 3. The rest still need your reply; try again."

## Scheduling Preferences {#scheduling-preferences}

Tell your schedulers how you like to serve. They and the church's auto-scheduling use these preferences when they fill positions.

1. On the Plans page, find **Scheduling preferences** and tap **Edit**.
2. Fill in any of these:
   - **Max times per month** -- the most times you want to serve in a month. Leave it blank for no limit. It has to be a whole number of 1 or more; otherwise the sheet says "Enter a whole number of 1 or more, or leave it blank." and you can't save.
   - **Preferred service times** -- the services you would rather serve at, for example "9:00 am" or "First Service". Separate several with commas.
   - **Household** -- **Schedule us together**, **Not on the same day**, or **No preference**.
3. Tap **Save**.

The section then shows your preferences -- **No limit** when you left the maximum blank, and **Any time** when you named no preferred services -- or **No preferences set.** if you haven't set any. If saving fails you'll see "Couldn't save your preferences. Try again." Staff can see and change the same preferences in B1 Admin (see [Scheduling Preferences](../../b1-admin/serving/plans.md#scheduling-preferences)).

## Adding Your Serving Times to Your Calendar {#serving-calendar}

The **Serving calendar** section on the Plans page lets you see your serving schedule in your own calendar app, such as Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook.

1. On the Plans page, find **Serving calendar** and tap **Subscribe**. The section explains: "Adds the times you have accepted to your calendar app. Anyone with this link can see them."
2. Your device opens the subscription in your calendar app. Confirm there to add it.

The calendar shows the assignments you have **accepted**, with the plan, your position, and the service times you are needed (or an all-day event if the plan has no times). Requests you haven't answered and assignments you declined are not included. Your calendar app checks for changes on its own schedule, so updates can take a while to appear.

:::warning
Anyone with your calendar link can see your serving times. If you shared it by mistake, tap **Reset link**. The old link stops working right away ("Link reset. The old link no longer works; subscribe again to keep your calendar up to date."); tap **Subscribe** again to add the new one to your calendar.
:::

## Viewing Plan Details

Tap any plan to open it. The plan page shows the plan name and service date at the top, followed by these sections:

- **Your assignment** -- Each position you are assigned on this plan, with its team, your status, and the **Service Times** you are needed (each with its name, date, start, and end time). If you have not yet responded, tap **Accept** or **Decline** right here. **Decline** asks you to confirm first.
- **Service Order** -- The first five items of the service, with a count of how many there are ("12 items"). If the plan's order of service comes straight from a scheduled lesson, the first three lesson items are shown instead, prefixed with the lesson's name. Tap **Full service order** to see everything. See [Service Order](./service-order.md) for more details.
- **Serving Teams** -- Who is serving alongside you and in which positions, as pills ("Pat Jones · Greeter"), with a count of people and teams (for example, "10 serving · 3 teams"). People who declined their position are left out. Tap **All teams** to see every team and its members.
- **Plan Notes** -- Any notes the plan creator added for the team. Only shown when there are notes.
- **Downloads** -- Files attached to the plan's lesson content, if any. They open in a new tab.

Tap **Back to** *plan name* at the top of the full service order or team list to return to the plan.

## Declining an Accepted Assignment

If something comes up after you have accepted, you can still back out from the plan page:

1. Open the plan and find your position under **Your assignment**. It shows an **Accepted** badge.
2. Tap the red **Decline** link under the position.
3. A confirmation asks **Decline this assignment?** -- "Your team leaders will be notified that you can no longer serve in this position."
4. Optionally type a short note in **Reason (optional)**, such as "Out of town".
5. Tap **Decline** to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep the assignment.

Your status changes to **Declined**. The same confirmation, with the same **Reason (optional)** box, appears whenever you tap **Decline** -- on the plan page, in **Needs your reply** on the Plans page, or in **You're serving** on Home -- so a mis-tap never declines an assignment. Declining from the **Decline** button in a reminder email also offers a **Reason (optional)** box.

:::info
If you give a reason, it is added to the notification your ministry leaders get when you decline. Only they and staff who can edit plans see it. A reason is limited to one line of up to 255 characters.
:::

## Accepting a Declined Assignment {#accepting-a-declined-assignment}

Changed your mind after declining? Open the plan; under your position, which shows a **Declined** badge, tap **Accept**. Your status changes back to **Accepted**.

The **Decline** and **Accept** links are only available until your service time is over -- after that, your assignment can no longer be changed from the plan page.

If a plan can't be opened -- it was removed or isn't shared with you -- the page says "Plan not available" with a **Back to Plans** link.

If you never responded to an assignment and its service time has passed, the plan page shows a **Response closed** badge instead of **Accept** and **Decline**, with the note *"This serving time has passed, so you can no longer respond."*

:::tip
If you have multiple assignments across different plans, the list is sorted by date so your upcoming commitments appear first. Check back regularly to stay on top of your schedule.
:::

## Teacher View for Lessons

If your plan includes associated curriculum from a content provider (such as Lessons.church), the **Service Order** section shows the lesson's sections for a quick review, and a **Teach** button appears at the top of the plan page.

Click **Teach** to open an immersive, full-screen lesson presentation designed for teachers, with formatted instructions, embedded media, notes, and step-by-step guidance. Close it to return to the plan.

The Teach view organizes lesson content into sections. You can:

- Tap **Sections** at the top to see the list of sections and jump directly to any part of the lesson
- Scroll through the lesson content -- the heading shows which section you are in and its name (for example, "Section 2 of 5 · Worship")
- Click on media items (videos, images, activities) to view them in a modal dialog; audio links (song recordings, sermon clips) play inline with a built-in audio player
- See action types formatted distinctively: **Say** appears in conversation bubbles, **Do** in bold text, and **Note** in highlighted callouts

:::tip
The Teach view is especially helpful when leading a classroom or small group, as it presents all your lesson content in a clean, distraction-free format optimized for mobile and tablet use.
:::
