# Viewing Plans

<div class="article-intro">

The Plans page gives you a clear view of all the service plans you are assigned to. It shows what needs your reply, what is coming up, and what you have served, and lets you open any plan for the details.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- You must be [logged in](../getting-started/logging-in.md) to your B1.church account.
- You must be assigned to at least one service plan. If you do not see any plans, contact your church administrator or ministry leader.

</div>

## Accessing Your Plans

1. Tap **Plans** in the tab bar, on the [More page](../getting-started/navigating.md#the-more-page), or tap **All plans** in the **You're serving** section on Home.
2. You will see the plans you are scheduled on.

## The Plans Page

The top of the page shows how many of your upcoming assignments are confirmed and how many still need a reply, plus a **Sign up to serve** button that opens the [volunteer signup](../serving/volunteer-signup.md) page. Below that, the page is organized into sections:

- **Needs your reply** -- Assignments you have not answered yet, each with **Accept** and **Decline** buttons so you can respond without opening the plan. Only shown when something is waiting on you.
- **Upcoming** -- Your next three assignments. Tap **All upcoming** to see the rest.
- **Blockout dates** -- Dates when you are unavailable for serving. Tap **Add dates**, choose a **Start date** and **End date**, and tap **Save**. Tap **Remove** next to a blockout to delete it. Your team leaders see these dates so they can schedule around you.
- **Past** -- How many times you have served this year and when you last served. Tap **Past assignments** to see the full list, which you can filter by year.

Each assignment shows the **service date**, the **plan name**, your **position**, and a status badge -- **Accepted**, **Confirmed**, **Declined**, **Pending Response**, or **Needs reply** if you have not responded yet.

:::info
Your church may send **reminders** before the services you are scheduled for, by email and as an app notification. If your church sends texts, you can also get them by text by turning on **Text messages** in your [Notification Preferences](../getting-started/notification-preferences.md). If you have not responded yet, you can **Accept** or **Decline** right from the reminder email — no sign-in needed.
:::

## Viewing Plan Details

Tap any plan to open it. The plan page shows the plan name and service date at the top, followed by these sections:

- **Your assignment** -- Each position you are assigned on this plan, with its team, your status, and the **Service Times** you are needed. If you have not yet responded, tap **Accept** or **Decline** right here.
- **Service Order** -- The first few items of the service, with a count of how many there are. Tap **Full service order** to see everything. See [Service Order](./service-order.md) for more details.
- **Serving Teams** -- Who is serving alongside you and in which positions, with a count of people and teams. Tap **All teams** to see every team and its members.
- **Plan Notes** -- Any notes the plan creator added for the team.
- **Downloads** -- Files attached to the plan's lesson content, if any.

Tap **Back to** *plan name* at the top of the full service order or team list to return to the plan.

## Declining an Accepted Assignment

If something comes up after you have accepted, you can still back out from the plan page:

1. Open the plan and find your position under **Your assignment**. It shows an **Accepted** badge.
2. Tap the red **Decline** link under the position.
3. A confirmation asks **Decline this assignment?** -- "Your team leaders will be notified that you can no longer serve in this position."
4. Tap **Decline** to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep the assignment.

Your status changes to **Declined**. The **Decline** link is only available until your service time is over -- after that, your assignment can no longer be changed from the plan page.

:::tip
If you have multiple assignments across different plans, the list is sorted by date so your upcoming commitments appear first. Check back regularly to stay on top of your schedule.
:::

## Teacher View for Lessons

If your plan includes associated curriculum from a content provider (such as Lessons.church), the **Service Order** section shows the lesson's sections for a quick review, and a **Teach** button appears at the top of the plan page.

Click **Teach** to open an immersive, full-screen lesson presentation designed for teachers, with formatted instructions, embedded media, notes, and step-by-step guidance. Close it to return to the plan.

The Teach view organizes lesson content into sections. You can:

- Tap **Sections** at the top to see the list of sections and jump directly to any part of the lesson
- Scroll through the lesson content -- the heading shows which section you are in (for example, "Section 2 of 5")
- Click on media items (videos, images, activities) to view them in a modal dialog; audio links (song recordings, sermon clips) play inline with a built-in audio player
- See action types formatted distinctively: **Say** appears in conversation bubbles, **Do** in bold text, and **Note** in highlighted callouts

:::tip
The Teach view is especially helpful when leading a classroom or small group, as it presents all your lesson content in a clean, distraction-free format optimized for mobile and tablet use.
:::
