# B1.church

<div class="article-intro">

B1.church is the member-facing portal for your church. It is the place where your congregation goes to stay connected, access church tools, and engage with your community throughout the week. Each church gets its own URL at `https://yourchurchname.b1.church`.

</div>

## Training Video

<YouTube id="yiKP0zuJMVU" title="B1.church for Your Members" next="Logging In and Installing the B1.church App" nextUrl="/docs/b1-church/getting-started/logging-in" />

When members visit your B1.church site, they see your church's public website with your branding, navigation links, and content pages. After logging in, they can access the **Member Portal** where all of their personal church tools live in one place.

## Key Features

- **Home and Me** -- The member portal opens on a [Home dashboard](./getting-started/navigating.md) with previews of your upcoming serving assignments, your groups, and your giving this year, and the [Me page](./getting-started/me-page.md) lists what is coming up for you -- serving assignments, event registrations, group events -- along with your recent notifications, the forms you've submitted, and your attendance history. Switch **All upcoming** to a month calendar and subscribe to it from your own calendar app. Parents can send updates to a child's profile for church staff to approve.
- **Groups** -- View the groups you belong to, see group members, join conversations, track attendance, and fill out forms linked to your group (leaders can read and print the answers). Share a group as a link or QR code, mute a group you don't want notifications from, and, as a leader, approve or decline join requests from the group page.
- **Giving** -- Make donations to your church online and view your giving history and statements, even if your church doesn't take gifts online. Statements follow your church's statement year (for example July to June) and show its Tax ID and tax disclaimer.
- **Events** -- Browse upcoming events (with their location) and register yourself and your family. Pay a deposit or choose pay later when the church allows it, and answer registration questions later from [My Registrations](./events/my-registrations.md).
- **Serving** -- View service plans, your volunteer positions, and sign up for open volunteer opportunities. Choose to get serving and event reminders by text in your [notification preferences](./getting-started/notification-preferences.md) (reply STOP at any time to stop).
- **Plans** -- See upcoming service plans, accept all your requests at once or decline with a reason, set your [scheduling preferences](./plans/viewing-plans.md#scheduling-preferences), subscribe to your serving times in your calendar app, and access song details, chart PDFs and service orders.
- **Check-In** -- Check in yourself and your household members for services and events. Guests can register via QR code, and returning guests are matched to their existing profile instead of being added twice.
- **Community Directory** -- Browse your church's member directory, view profiles, and send direct messages. Control who can see your own contact info from your [profile's Privacy section](./getting-started/editing-your-profile.md#privacy).
- **Sign-in security** -- Log in with an authenticator-app code as well as your password once you've turned on [two-factor sign-in](../b1-admin/profile/managing-profile.md#two-factor-sign-in), or skip the password and [sign in with a code sent to your email](./getting-started/logging-in.md).
- **Content** -- Access sermons, read the Bible, watch live streams, and view, share or download the daily verse (and earlier ones), all from your church's site. (Curriculum playback now lives in [FreePlay](/docs/freeplay/) — the in-portal Lessons tab is deprecated.)
