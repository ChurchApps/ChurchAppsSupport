# Events

<div class="article-intro">

The Events section on B1.church lets you browse upcoming church events and register for them directly from the website. When your church enables registration for an event, you can sign up yourself and your family members in just a few steps — and for paid events, choose attendee types and options, apply discount codes, and pay securely as part of signing up.

</div>

## Browsing Events

Events appear on your church's website, either on a month calendar or as tiles of upcoming events. Click an event to open its own page, which shows:

- **Event photo** -- If the church added one.
- **Event title and description** -- What the event is about.
- **When** -- The date and time of the next occurrence, or the repeat pattern for a recurring event.
- **Where** -- The rooms the event is booked in, if any.
- **Register for this Event** -- A button that takes you to the registration form. Only shown when registration is turned on for the event.
- **Add to my calendar** -- Downloads the event as a calendar file you can open in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, or another calendar app.

Tap **All events** at the top of the page to go back. Above the upcoming-event tiles, a **Subscribe** button lets you copy the whole calendar's ICS link or download it, so new events show up in your own calendar app.

## Registration Status

Events may show different statuses depending on timing and capacity:

- **Open** -- Registration is available and you can sign up.
- **Not Yet Open** -- The registration window has not started. Check back after the opening date.
- **Closed** -- The registration deadline has passed.
- **Full** -- All available spots have been taken.

:::tip
If an event is full, you may still be able to join the **waitlist** when your church has enabled it -- you'll be emailed automatically if a spot opens. Otherwise, check back later; spots may open up if someone cancels.
:::

## What You Need

- A B1.church account (or you can register as a guest)
- Access to your church's B1.church website
