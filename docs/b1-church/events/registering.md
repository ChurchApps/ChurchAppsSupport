# Registering for Events

<div class="article-intro">

When your church enables registration for an event, you can sign up through a simple step-by-step process. You can register yourself and add family members, whether you have an account or are visiting as a guest. For paid events, you choose an attendee type, add any optional extras, apply a discount code, and pay securely at the end.

</div>

## Step 1: Enter Your Information

1. From the church calendar, open the event you want to register for and click **Register for this Event**.
2. The registration page loads showing the event title, description, and date. An event that runs over several days shows both its start and end dates. In the member portal each step is headed "Step 1 of 4 · Your Information" and so on, and an **Event** section with the description and "4 / 50 spots filled" stays at the top.
3. If you are **logged in**, the form shows **Registering as** with your name, so you are registering under your account.
4. If you are a **guest** (not logged in), fill in:
   - **First Name** (required)
   - **Last Name** (required)
   - **Email** (required)
   - **Phone** (optional; labelled **Phone (optional)** in the member portal)
5. If the event has attendee types (for example, Adult and Child at different prices), choose **Your registration type**. Each type shows its price, and types that have run out show as **Sold out**.
6. Click **Continue**. If you are logged in and already registered for this event, you'll see "You're already registered for this event." instead; check [My Registrations](./my-registrations.md) to see or change your registration.

:::info
If the event has a capacity limit, a progress bar shows how many spots remain. If the event is full and your church has enabled a waitlist, you can still complete the signup to join the waitlist — see [Joining a Waitlist](#joining-a-waitlist) below.
:::

## Step 2: Add Family Members

1. If you are logged in, the **Additional Members** step lists your household (under **Your Household** on the website), with you already checked. Check each family member who is coming. If the event has attendee types, pick a type for each one. When a type has an age range (for example, Child for ages 0 to 12) and a family member's birthdate on file fits it, that type is picked for them when you check their name; you can still change it.
   - A family member who is already on another registration for this event is marked **Already registered** and can't be checked, so nobody is signed up twice.
2. To add someone who isn't in your household, click **Add Member** and enter their **First Name** and **Last Name** (up to 10 people besides yourself).
3. If the event has attendee types, pick a type for each person you add.
4. Click the remove button next to any member to take them off the registration.
5. Click **Continue** (the button says **Complete Registration** when the event has no options or payment). If you are only registering yourself, leave everyone else unchecked and continue.

## Step 3: Choose Options & Selections (If Offered)

Some events offer optional priced add-ons — T-shirts, meals, activity upgrades.

1. If the event has selections, an **Options & Selections** step appears.
2. Use the quantity steppers to choose how many of each you want. Free options show as **Free**, and limited options show how many are remaining.
3. Click **Continue**.

## Step 4: Answer Questions (If Asked)

Some events ask registrants to answer a few extra questions -- dietary restrictions, T-shirt size, emergency contact, and similar details.

1. If the event has questions attached, a **Questions** step appears next.
2. Fill in your answers and continue.

If the event has no questions attached, this step is skipped automatically. If church staff registered you, or you registered before the questions were added, you can answer them later from [My Registrations](./my-registrations.md#answering-questions-later).

## Step 5: Pay (Paid Events)

If your registration has a cost, the **Payment** step shows an **Order Summary** listing each attendee, each selection, any discount, and the **Total**.

- **Discount code** -- If your church gave you a code, enter it and click **Apply**. The discount is reflected immediately ("Code SAVE10 applied", with **Remove** to take it off), and you'll see a clear message if the code is not valid, is no longer active, or has been used the maximum number of times. Under the total, a footnote shows the subtotal before discounts.
- **Logged-in members** can pay with a saved card (listed as the card name and its last four digits) or choose **Use a new card**.
- **Guests** enter their card details directly and tick the **I'm not a robot** check before paying.

Click the **Pay & Register** button, which shows the amount being charged (for example, **Pay $25.00 & Register**), to complete the payment and registration in one step. Your card details go directly to the church's payment provider and are never stored by B1.

- **Deposits** -- Some attendee types only need a deposit to hold your spot. When they do, the order summary shows **Due today** (what you pay now) and **Balance due later** (what you'll still owe). Options and selections are paid in full today. Pay the rest later from [My Registrations](./my-registrations.md#completing-a-payment), or pay the church in person.
- **Pay later** -- If the church allows it, the payment step also says "Or register now and pay by cash or check at the event." with a **Register and Pay Later** button. Your registration is saved with the full amount as a balance due, and nothing is charged.

Free registrations skip this step and go straight to confirmation.

If your church does not take payments online, the payment step says *"Online payment is not available for this event. You can register now and pay the church directly."* Click **Register and Pay Later** to finish. Your registration is saved with the amount shown as a balance due, which you pay to the church directly.

## Step 6: Confirm

On the website, **Registration Confirmed!** appears with the event, its date, your status, and the list of **Registered Members**. You will also receive a **confirmation email** at the email address associated with your account (or the email you provided as a guest). The church may add its own note to that email, such as directions or what to bring.

In the member portal the confirmation lists the **Registered Members** and what you paid ("Total: $25.00 / $25.00"), with a **Done** button that returns to Home. If you chose **Register and Pay Later**, the member portal shows **Registration Pending** instead, with a **Pending** badge, "Your spot is held for" the event, and the balance due (for example, "Balance due $25.00 of $25.00"); on the website the status line simply reads "pending" with "Total: $0.00 / $25.00".

Once you're registered, the event's **Register** link on your group's page is replaced by your status (**Registered**, **Pending**, or **Waitlisted**), and the event appears under **Upcoming events** on your Home screen tagged **Registration**.

## Joining a Waitlist

If the event is full and your church enabled the waitlist, the first step shows: *"This event is full. You can join the waitlist and we will notify you if a spot opens."* Complete the signup as normal — payment is skipped while you are waitlisted. When a spot opens, you receive an email; if a payment is due, it includes a link to complete it. You can also check your status and pay from [My Registrations](./my-registrations.md).

## Changing or Canceling a Registration

If you are logged in, you can edit or cancel your registration yourself from the **Registrations** page in the member portal — see [My Registrations](./my-registrations.md). Some events don't allow cancelling online; for those, contact the church office. Guests should contact the church office, and staff can make the change from the B1 Admin registrations page.
