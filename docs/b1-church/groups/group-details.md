# Group Details

<div class="article-intro">

When you click on a group from the groups page, you are taken to the group detail page. What you see depends on whether you are logged in or not, with logged-in members seeing additional sections and features.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- Find a group to view by [browsing groups](./browsing-groups.md).
- For full access to every section, [log in](../getting-started/logging-in.md) to your account.

</div>

## Visitor View (Not Logged In)

If you are not logged in, you will see the following:

- **Header** -- The group's name with its meeting time and location. On a computer, the group's photo appears above it.
- **Sign in to join** -- A button under the group's name. Sign in (or create an account) to join the group or request to join. After you sign in, you are brought back to the group and taken straight to the join step.
- **About** -- The full description of the group explaining its purpose and what to expect.
- **Leaders** -- The names of the group's leaders. You can tap a leader's name to view their profile.
- **Upcoming Events** -- Up to three upcoming events for the group, each showing the date, time, and event title, with a **Register** link when the event takes registrations. If the group has no upcoming events, this section is hidden.

## Member View (Logged In)

If you are logged in but not a member of the group, you see the group's header and **About** section along with the join option for the group (see [Joining a Group](#joining-a-group) below). The sections described next appear once you are a member.

When you are a member, the group's header shows its name, meeting day, time, and location, and an **Open chat** button if your church has turned on group chat. If you lead the group, a **You · Leader** tag appears under the name. On a computer, the header sits in a column on the left with the group's photo, and the sections appear to its right.

The page is one sheet of sections. Each shows a short preview, and a link in the section's header opens the full view; tap **Back to** *group name* to return. The available sections are:

- **Next event** -- The group's next upcoming event, with **Going** / **Maybe** / **Can't go** buttons (see [Event RSVPs](./event-rsvps.md)). Tap **All events** to open the group's calendar: use the arrows to move between months, and switch between **Calendar** (a month grid where you tap a day to see its events) and **List** (the month's events in date order, grouped by day). When you are looking at the current month, the list starts from today, so it only shows what's still coming up. If the group's events have tags, you can filter by tag.
- **This week's lesson** -- If your group is associated with a Lessons.church plan type and this week's lesson has parent take-home content, it appears under **Next event** (members only).
- **Messages** -- The latest messages from the group chat. Tap **Open chat** to read and post. Only shown to members, and only if your church has turned on group chat for this group. See [Group Conversations](./conversations.md) for more details.
- **Members** -- A preview of the group's members (leaders first) and a count of members and leaders. Tap **All members** for the full list, which you can search with **Search Members**. Tap a member's name to visit their profile in the [member directory](../community/member-directory.md).
- **Resources** -- The first few shared files and links posted for the group. Tap **All resources** to see them all; you can download uploaded files or open linked resources.
- **Plans** -- The group's next service plan. Tap **All plans** to see them all. Only shown if the group has plans.
- **Attendance** -- Visible to group leaders (and staff with attendance permission) only. Shows when attendance was last taken; tap **Take attendance** to record who attended a group meeting.
- **About** -- The group's full description, rendered with rich text formatting. Only shown if the group has a description.

Members also see a **Leave Group** link at the bottom of the page.

## Leader Capabilities

If you are a leader of the group, you have additional editing capabilities:

- **Create and edit events** -- Tap **All events**, then **Add Event** to add an event to the group calendar. When creating an event, you can reserve rooms and resources alongside it — select from available rooms and resources, set optional setup and teardown times, and the booking request is automatically submitted for approval.
- **Edit repeating events** -- When you open a repeating event to edit it, B1 first asks which dates to change: **Just this date**, **This and all following dates**, or **All dates**. Choose one and click **Save** to open the event. If you chose **Just this date**, the **Recurring** switch is hidden and your changes (or a delete) apply only to that one date.
- **Set event reminders** -- When editing an existing event, turn on **Send reminders** to remind people before it starts. Pick when they go out, the **Time of Day**, **Who should be reminded?**, and an optional **Message**, then choose **Push**, **Email**, or, if your church has texting set up, **Text** under **Send By**. Texts only go to people who turned on text reminders in their [Notification Preferences](../getting-started/notification-preferences.md).
- **Manage resources** -- Upload files (up to 100 MB of storage), add links, or delete existing resources.
- **Track attendance** -- Record attendance for group sessions with **Take attendance** in the **Attendance** section.

To change the group's name, description, or member list, use B1 Admin. See [Group Members](../../b1-admin/groups/group-members.md).

## Joining a Group

When you are logged in and not yet a member, a join button appears on the group detail page. The button label depends on the group's join policy:

- **Join Group** -- The group is open. Clicking this immediately adds you as a member.
- **Request to Join** -- The group requires approval. Clicking this opens a dialog where you can include an optional message to the group leader, then submit your request. See [Requesting to Join a Group](./join-requests.md) for details on managing your requests.

After you send a request, the button is replaced by a **Request Pending** notice: "Your request to join has been sent. A group leader will review it."

If you were not logged in and clicked **Sign in to join**, you return to the group after signing in and the join step starts automatically: an open group adds you right away, and a group that requires approval opens the **Request to Join** dialog.

If neither button appears, the group is closed and members must be added manually by a leader or administrator.

:::info
You must be signed in to join or request to join a group. If a group is closed and you want to join, contact your church office or one of the group's leaders.
:::
