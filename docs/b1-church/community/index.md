# Community

<div class="article-intro">

The **Community** section in B1.church helps you stay connected with your church family. It provides the member directory for finding and learning about fellow church members.

</div>

## What You Will Find

- **[Member Directory](./member-directory.md)** -- Browse and search your church's member directory. Look up people by name or filter by group to find who you are looking for. Click on a member to view their profile details.
- **[Timeline](./timeline.md)** -- The old combined activity feed, now retired. This page explains where each kind of update lives today.

## Getting Started

You must be [logged in](../getting-started/logging-in.md) to access community features. If you are not signed in, the **Community** tab shows a Sign In prompt instead of the directory.

The member portal opens on the [Home dashboard](../getting-started/navigating.md). The **Community** tab provides access to the member directory.

:::info
The members and information visible to you in the directory depend on your church's privacy settings. You may not be able to see every member's full profile. If you have questions about what is shared, contact your church administrator.
:::

:::tip
Use the community features alongside [Groups](../groups/) to stay engaged with your church throughout the week.
:::
