# News

<div class="article-intro">

**News** brings your church's blog into the member portal. When your church turns it on, the latest posts appear as photo cards at the top of Home, and the **All news** list lets you catch up on everything your church has published recently -- all without leaving the app.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- Your church must turn on **News** for the member portal's Home screen in B1 Admin -- see [Home Screen Sections](../../b1-admin/settings/mobile-app.md#home-screen-sections). If it hasn't, the News strip and the News screen don't appear.
- Your church must have published at least one blog post. See [Blog](./blog.md) for the website side of the same posts.
- No login is needed. News shows for signed-out visitors too.

</div>

## News on Home

When News is on and there is at least one post, a **News** section appears at the top of the [Home dashboard](../getting-started/navigating.md#home), above your church's photo tiles. The five newest posts are shown as wide photo cards with the post's title and date over the picture. When there is more than one, the cards slide along on their own; swipe to move through them yourself.

- Tap a card to read the whole post in a sheet that slides up over Home -- the photo, the author and date, and the full text. Close the sheet to return to where you were.
- Tap **All news** in the section header to open the News screen.

If your church picked a single blog category for News, only posts from that category appear here and on the News screen; the rest stay on the website blog.

## The News Screen

The **News** screen (`/mobile/news`) lists the 20 most recent posts, newest first, each with a thumbnail, the title, and the publish date. Tap a post to open it on its own page (`/mobile/news/` followed by the post's web address slug), with the photo at the top, the author and date under the title, and the post itself.

If there is nothing to show, the screen says "No news yet." Opening a link to a post that has been removed shows "This post isn't available."

## Reading a Post

Posts are written in B1 Admin and keep their formatting here -- headings, bold text, lists, links, and images. Links open in a new browser tab.

At the bottom of every post is a **View on website** link. It opens the same post on your church's public website (`/blog/` followed by the slug) in a new tab, where you can also see its category and tags, find related posts, and share the web address -- see [Blog](./blog.md).

:::tip
News is the same content as your church's blog, just delivered inside the app. If you prefer a feed reader, subscribe to the blog's RSS feed instead -- see [Subscribing via RSS](./blog.md#subscribing-via-rss).
:::
