# Donation History

<div class="article-intro">

When you are logged in, you can view a complete record of your past donations and generate giving statements for tax purposes. Your donation history is available from the **Giving** page of the member portal.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- You must be [logged in](../getting-started/logging-in.md) to your B1.church account.
- You need to have made at least one donation. See [Making a Donation](./making-donations.md) to get started.

</div>

## Viewing Your History

1. Log in to your B1.church account.
2. Open the **Giving** page (your church's **Donate** tab), or tap **History** in the **Giving** section of your [Home dashboard](../getting-started/navigating.md#home).
3. Tap **All history** to view your past donations. (The Home **History** link opens this view directly.)

At the top, pick a year (or **All**) to see your total for that period, how many gifts it includes, and how many are still pending. If donations in that period were given in more than one currency, the total is converted to your church currency and a **Converted at current exchange rates** note appears underneath.

Below the total, your donations are grouped by month, each showing:

- **Fund** -- Which fund the donation was directed to (for example, General Fund, Building Fund).
- **Date** and **Method** -- When the donation was made and how you paid (for example, "Card - Visa" or "Bank - ACH").
- **Amount** -- The amount of the donation, in the currency it was given.

:::info
Donations that are still being processed show a **Pending** badge under the amount.
:::

Tap **Back to Giving** to return to the Giving page.

## Exporting and Printing Statements

Tap **Print Statement** at the top of the Giving page to open a printable giving statement covering the current calendar year in a new tab. To print a different year, open **All history**, pick that year, and tap **Print Statement** next to the total.

:::tip
Print your statement early in the new year, before you need it for tax filing, while last year's donations are still fresh in your history.
:::

## Recurring Donations

If you have any scheduled gifts, the **Recurring** section on the Giving page lists each one with its amount, fund, interval, and payment method. Tap **Manage** to open **Recurring gifts and payment methods**, where you can change them.

If a recurring donation is charged through **Stripe**, you can **pause** it instead of canceling -- click the pause icon next to the donation and confirm. Giving stops until you click the same icon to **resume**; a paused donation is marked **Paused** in the list. Recurring donations through other payment providers can still be edited or canceled, but not paused.

To add, remove, or change your saved payment methods, tap **Manage** in the **Payment methods** section.
