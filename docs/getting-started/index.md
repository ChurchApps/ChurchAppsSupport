## ChurchApps Products

ChurchApps includes several products that work together:

- **B1 Admin** -- Your church management dashboard. Manage people, groups, attendance, donations, website content, and more from one central location. [B1 Admin guide](/docs/b1-admin/)
- **B1.church** -- Your public church website and member portal. Members can view sermons, access directories, give online, and stay connected. [B1.church guide](/docs/b1-church/)
- **Lessons.church** -- Free church curriculum for Sunday school, small groups, and Bible studies. Access a growing library of lessons at no cost. [Lessons.church guide](/docs/lessons-church/)
- **FreePlay** -- A free TV app that plays your lessons, worship videos, and announcements on classroom and hallway TVs (Fire TV, Android TV, and Apple TV), even on a schedule. [Set up FreePlay](/docs/freeplay/)
- **FreeShow** -- Free presentation software for your worship services. Display lyrics, slides, and media during your gatherings. [freeshow.app](https://freeshow.app)
- **WorshipCommons** -- An open library of worship music your church can sing free: public domain hymns and songs shared by writers, with chord charts, lyrics, transposition, and audio. [worshipcommons.org](https://worshipcommons.org)

:::info
All ChurchApps tools are **100% free** with no limits, no premium tiers, and no hidden fees. ChurchApps is a nonprofit organization supported by donations and volunteers.
:::

## What This Documentation Covers

This documentation will walk you through everything you need to get up and running with ChurchApps. You will learn how to:

1. Create your account and register your church.
2. Configure your church's basic settings and branding.
3. Set up your team with the right roles and permissions.
4. Start using the tools to manage your congregation.
