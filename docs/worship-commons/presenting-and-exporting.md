# Presenting and Exporting WorshipCommons Songs

<div class="article-intro">

WorshipCommons can put a song's lyrics on a screen straight from your browser, with nothing to install. If your church already uses presentation software, download the song's slides for FreeShow, OpenLP, ProPresenter, or PowerPoint instead, copy it into OnSong or Planning Center, or add the song to your B1 Admin song library.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- Open the song's page on [worshipcommons.org](https://worshipcommons.org). See [Finding Songs](./finding-songs.md).
- To show the lyrics on a TV or projector, connect your computer to it with an HDMI cable (or cast your screen).

</div>

## Leading Worship from the Browser

**Lead worship** is a full-screen view that plays the music and shows the words line by line, highlighting each word as it's sung. It's built for a small group, a classroom, or a house church with no band.

1. On the song page, choose the key you want, then click **Lead worship**. (The button appears when the song has a melody file or a recording to play along with.)
2. Check the label next to the play button. It tells you what will play: **Instrumental** (the vocal-free mix built from the song's stems), **Demo recording**, **Accompaniment**, or **Preview (synthesized)** (a piano playing the melody file).
3. Choose the **Key**. The list is split into **Published keys** (the song's recommended key is marked **(recommended)**) and **Preview — not yet reviewed** for every other key. When a recording plays, the key is locked to the key it was sung in.
4. Set the **Tempo** slider (50%–150%). The BPM updates as you move it.
5. When the song plays a piano preview, pick **Piano** or **Organ**.
6. Click **Verses** to choose which sections to sing. Tick or untick each one and use **↑** and **↓** to change the order. The **Run order** list below shows the result and highlights the section you're on.
7. Leave **Count-in** checked for one bar of clicks before the music starts.
8. Click **▶ Play**. The button reads **Counting in…** during the count-in and **❚❚ Pause** while playing.

The screen shows the current section name, the current line with the sung words highlighted, and the next line (or the next section's first line, with its name). A progress bar at the bottom shows how far through the set of verses you are. When the last section ends, playback stops and returns to the start.

Use **Blank** to hide the words (click the screen or press **B** to bring them back), **Contrast** for a high-contrast screen, and **Fullscreen** to fill the screen. Click **Put this on the TV** for three quick steps: plug in an HDMI cable or cast the whole screen, press **F** for fullscreen, and mirror the display.

:::tip
Keyboard shortcuts: **Space** play/pause, **← →** move a line, **↑ ↓** move a section, **B** blank, **H** contrast, **F** fullscreen, **Esc** closes an open panel or goes back to the song. Click **Show keys** or **Hide keys** at the bottom of the screen to toggle the legend. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](./keyboard-shortcuts.md).
:::

:::info
If a song has no word timings, Lead worship spreads the lyrics evenly across the tune so **▶ Play** still works; the highlighting is approximate. Songs with the **Word-by-word highlighting** output on the About & rights tab follow the music exactly.
:::

## Projecting Lyrics

**Open projector** shows one section of lyrics per screen, and you move through them by hand. Use it when you have live musicians.

1. On the song page, find the **Projection** panel and click **Open projector**.
2. Press **F** or click **Fullscreen** to fill the screen.
3. Click the screen, or press **Space**, **→**, **↓**, or **Page Down**, to go to the next slide. Press **←**, **↑**, **Page Up**, or **Backspace** to go back. **Home** and **End** jump to the first and last slide. Click a section name at the bottom to jump straight to it.
4. Press **B** (or **.**) to blank the screen and **H** for high contrast.
5. Press **Esc** (or click **← Back to song**) when you're done.

The bottom bar shows the current section, the slide count (for example **3 / 7**), and **Next:** with the following section's name. The song's credit line appears on screen as well.

## Exporting Slides

The **Projection** panel on the song page has a button for each tool. Click one to download the song:

| Button | What you get | How to bring it in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **FreeShow** | A `.show` file | In [FreeShow](https://freeshow.app), use **Import** and choose **FreeShow** |
| **PowerPoint** | A `.pptx` deck, white text on black, one section per slide | Open it in PowerPoint, Keynote, or LibreOffice |
| **OpenLP** | An OpenLyrics `.xml` file | In OpenLP, use **Songs**, then **Import**, and choose **OpenLyrics** |
| **ProPresenter** | A plain `.txt` file with a section name before each slide | In ProPresenter, use **File**, then **Import**, then **File…** |
| **OnSong / PCO** | Copies the song to your clipboard as ChordPro text, with sections as `Verse 1:` lines | Paste into OnSong or Planning Center |

Every export is built from the same slide model as the web projector, so what you see in **Open projector** is what the file contains. Chords are stripped, band directions such as **(2x)** or **Repeat** are dropped, and the last slide carries the song's attribution and license credit. The FreeShow and OpenLyrics files also carry the title, writer, copyright, and CCLI number as metadata.

The note under the buttons tells you how the slides are split. If the song has an approved form map, slides follow it; otherwise slides follow the stanzas as written.

:::info
Exports from the song page use the song's original key and default section order. To export in the key you chose, with the sections you want, and for several songs at once, build a [service plan](./service-plans.md) and use the export buttons there.
:::

## Adding WorshipCommons Songs to B1

If your church plans services in B1 Admin, you don't need to download anything. Sunday-ready songs from WorshipCommons appear in B1 Admin's song search under **WorshipCommons — free**. Click one to add it to your song library, linked back to its WorshipCommons page.

See [Songs in B1 Admin](../b1-admin/serving/songs.md#free-songs-from-worshipcommons) for the steps.
