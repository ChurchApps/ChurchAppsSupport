# Presenting and Exporting WorshipCommons Songs

<div class="article-intro">

WorshipCommons can put a song's lyrics on a screen straight from your browser, with nothing to install. If your church already uses presentation software, download the song's slides for FreeShow, OpenLP, ProPresenter, or PowerPoint instead, or add the song to your B1 Admin song library.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- Open the song's page on [worshipcommons.org](https://worshipcommons.org). See [Finding Songs](./finding-songs.md).
- To show the lyrics on a TV or projector, connect your computer to it with an HDMI cable (or cast your screen).

</div>

## Leading Worship from the Browser

**Lead worship** is a full-screen view that plays the music and shows the words line by line, highlighting each word as it's sung. It's built for a small group, a classroom, or a house church with no band.

1. On the song page, click **Lead worship**. (The button appears when the song has music to play along with.)
2. Choose the **Key** and **Tempo**. When the song plays a piano preview, you can pick **Piano** or **Organ**.
3. Click **Verses** to choose which verses to sing and to change their order with the arrow buttons.
4. Turn on **Count-in** if you want a count before the music starts.
5. Click **▶ Play**.

Use **Blank** to hide the words, **Contrast** for a high-contrast screen, and **Fullscreen** to fill the screen. Click **Put this on the TV** for quick steps to show it on a TV.

:::tip
Keyboard shortcuts: **Space** play/pause, **← →** move a line, **↑ ↓** move a verse, **B** blank, **H** contrast, **F** fullscreen, **Esc** back to the song. Click **Show keys** at the bottom of the screen to see them.
:::

## Projecting Lyrics

**Open projector** shows one section of lyrics per screen, and you move through them by hand. Use it when you have live musicians.

1. On the song page, find the **Projection** panel and click **Open projector**.
2. Press **F** or click **Fullscreen** to fill the screen.
3. Click the screen, or press **Space** or the **→** key, to go to the next slide. Press **←** to go back. Click a section name at the bottom to jump straight to it.
4. Press **B** to blank the screen and **H** for high contrast.
5. Press **Esc** (or click **← Back to song**) when you're done.

The bottom bar shows the current section, the slide count, and what's next. The song's credit line appears on screen as well.

## Exporting Slides

The **Projection** panel on the song page has a button for each tool. Click one to download the song:

| Button | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **FreeShow** | A show file to import into [FreeShow](https://freeshow.app) |
| **PowerPoint** | A PPTX slide deck |
| **OpenLP** | An OpenLyrics file to import into OpenLP |
| **ProPresenter** | A text file to import into ProPresenter |
| **OnSong / PCO** | Copies the song to your clipboard to paste into OnSong or Planning Center |

The note under the buttons tells you how the slides are split. If the song has an approved form map, slides follow it; otherwise slides follow the verses as written. Every export includes the song's attribution and license credit.

:::tip
To export a whole set at once, in the keys and sections you chose, build a [service plan](./service-plans.md) and use the export buttons there.
:::

## Adding WorshipCommons Songs to B1

If your church plans services in B1 Admin, you don't need to download anything. Sunday-ready songs from WorshipCommons appear in B1 Admin's song search under **WorshipCommons — free**. Click one to add it to your song library, linked back to its WorshipCommons page.

See [Songs in B1 Admin](../b1-admin/serving/songs.md#free-songs-from-worshipcommons) for the steps.
