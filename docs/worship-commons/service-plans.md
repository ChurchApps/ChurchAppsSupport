# WorshipCommons Service Plans

<div class="article-intro">

A service plan on WorshipCommons is a set list for Sunday. Put songs in order, choose the key, capo, and sections for each one, then share a single link with your band. From the plan you can open a stage view for a music stand, print a booklet, or export the whole set to your presentation software.

</div>

:::info
Service plans are saved in your web browser, not in your account, and you don't need to sign in to use them. A share link carries the whole set, so save the link if you switch devices or clear your browser. If your church plans services in B1 Admin, see [Plans](../b1-admin/serving/plans.md) instead.
:::

## Creating a Service Plan

1. Click **Service plans** in the top menu.
2. Type a name, such as "Sunday 14 Sept" or "Youth night", and click **Create service plan**.

You can also start a plan from any song. On the song page, click **+ Add to service plan**, type a name under the list, and click **Create**.

## Adding Songs

1. Open a song's page and click **+ Add to service plan**.
2. The pop-up heading shows the key the song will join in, for example **Key of G**. This is the song's recommended key when it has one, otherwise its original key. You can change it in the plan afterwards.
3. Click the plan's name. A check mark shows the song is in that plan. Click it again to take it out.

The button then reads **✓ In** followed by the plan's name (or **✓ In 2 service plans**). Click the **Open … →** link in the pop-up to go to the plan, or **+ Add more songs** at the bottom of a plan to go back to the library.

## Arranging the Set

Open a plan from the **Service plans** page. Click the plan's name at the top to rename it. For each song you can:

- **Reorder** -- Use the **↑** and **↓** buttons, or drag the song to a new spot. Click **Remove** to take it out.
- **Key** -- Choose from the song's **Published** keys or any other key (listed under **Preview (any key)**).
- **Capo** -- Choose **None** or a fret from 1 to 7. The option shows which chord shapes you'll play, for example **2 — C shapes**.
- **Sections** -- Set the order of verses and choruses. Use the arrows to move a section, **×** to drop it, and **+ Add section** to add one (for example, a repeated chorus). Click **Reset to default** to go back to the song's usual order.
- **Arrangement** -- Type a short note for the band, such as "Chorus ×2 at the end, last verse a cappella". It appears on the shared plan, in stage mode, and in the booklet.

Click a song's title to open its song page. The top of the plan shows the number of songs and the length of the set; a **≈** means at least one song's length is estimated from its chart rather than a recording. Changes save automatically.

If a song is later removed from the library, its row reads **This song is no longer in the commons.** Click **Remove** to drop it.

## Sharing with Your Team

1. Click **Share link**. The link is copied to your clipboard and shown in a box under the buttons so you can copy it by hand.
2. Send it to your band by text or email.

Anyone who opens the link sees **Shared service plan** with every key, capo, section, and note, without needing an account. Each song shows a one-line summary such as **Key of G · Capo 2 — E shapes · Verse 1 → Chorus → Verse 2**. They can click **Save a copy** to keep their own editable copy in their browser, open **Stage mode** and **Print booklet**, and download the same exports you can.

On the **Service plans** page each plan shows its song count, length, and when it was last updated. You can also **Share**, **Rename**, **Duplicate** (makes "Name (copy)"), or **Delete** a plan there.

## Using the Plan on Sunday

- **Stage mode** -- Shows one chart per screen, in the plan's key and capo, for a tablet on a music stand. Use the **←** and **→** buttons (or the arrow keys, **Space**, **Page Up**, and **Page Down**) to move between songs, **A−** and **A+** to change the text size, and **Fullscreen** to fill the screen. The footer shows **Next:** and the following song. Stage mode asks the device to keep the screen on; the bar shows **● Keeping the screen awake** or **○ Screen may sleep**. Click **✕ Exit** to leave.
- **Print booklet** -- Prints every chart in order, one per page, with a cover listing the songs and keys and a license line under each chart. Choose **Large text** or **Show chords**, then click **Print**.
- **Copy for OnSong / Planning Center** -- Copies every chart as ChordPro text in your keys, capos, and sections, ready to paste.

## Exporting the Set

Click **FreeShow**, **OpenLP**, **ProPresenter**, or **PowerPoint** to download the whole set for that tool, in the keys and sections you chose. The file is named after the plan; FreeShow, OpenLP, and ProPresenter exports of more than one song come as a ZIP with one file per song, and PowerPoint comes as a single deck. See [Presenting and Exporting Songs](./presenting-and-exporting.md#exporting-slides) for how each tool imports the file.

Click **House-church pack** to download a ZIP for a group with no presentation software. It holds, for each song, a numbered folder with the chord chart in your key as a ChordPro file (`chart.G.cho`), the lyrics as text, the slides, and the attribution, plus a `setlist.txt` listing the songs, keys, capos, and notes, and one `LICENSE.txt` covering every song.

:::warning
Songs licensed CC BY-SA are left out of the house-church pack unless you check **Share-alike pack (include CC BY-SA songs)**. If you include them, anything you make from the pack must be shared under the same license. The charts, stage mode, and booklet always include these songs. See [Licenses and Rights](./licenses-and-rights.md).
:::
