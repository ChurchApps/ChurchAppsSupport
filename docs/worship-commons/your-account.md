# Your WorshipCommons Account and Writer Page

<div class="article-intro">

You can browse, play, print, and project songs on WorshipCommons without an account. A free account lets you save songs, share songs of your own, propose edits, and keep track of everything you've sent. If you write songs, your account also lets you edit the writer page churches see.

</div>

## Signing In or Creating an Account

WorshipCommons uses ChurchApps accounts. If you already have one (for example, from B1), you can use the same email and password.

1. Click **Sign in** in the top menu.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**, then click **Sign in**.

**To create an account:**

1. On the sign-in page, click **Create an account**.
2. Enter your **First name**, **Last name**, and **Email**, then click **Create account**.
3. Check your email for a 6-digit code, type it in, and click **Verify code**.
4. Choose a password of at least 8 characters, type it again under **Verify password**, and click **Set password & sign in**.

**Forgot your password?** Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page, enter your email, and click **Send code**. Then enter the code from your email and set a new password.

Once you're signed in, your first name appears in the top menu. Click it to open **My submissions** or **Report**, or to **Sign out**.

## Saved Songs

Click the heart on any song page to save it. Click **Saved songs** in the top menu to see every song you've saved. Saved songs are stored in your account, so they appear on every device you sign in from. Click **Remove** to take a song off the list.

:::tip
Saved songs are a simple favorites list. To plan a set for a specific Sunday, with keys and sections, use [Service Plans](./service-plans.md).
:::

## My Submissions

Click your name in the top menu and choose **My submissions** to see every song and change you've started or sent, and where each one stands. Use the tabs at the top -- **All**, **Drafts**, **In review**, **Live**, and **Not accepted** -- to filter the list.

Each entry shows its type (such as **New song**, **Translation**, **Arrangement**, **Correction**, or **Removal request**) and its status:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Started but not sent yet. Click **Continue** to finish it or **Delete draft** to remove it. |
| **Changes requested** | A reviewer asked for changes. Click **Continue**, make them, and send it again. |
| **In review** | A person reads every song before it goes live, usually within a few days. Click **Withdraw** to pull it back into a draft. |
| **Live** | In the library and free for churches to use. You'll see its download count and its link. |
| **Not accepted** | It didn't make it into the library. The reviewer's reason is shown. |
| **Withdrawn** | You pulled it back before it was reviewed. |
| **Taken down** | The song was removed from the library, as you asked. |

For a live song, click **Copy link** to share its page, **Propose a change** to send an edit, or **Request removal** to ask for it to be taken down. See [Proposing Edits and Reporting Songs](./proposing-edits.md).

:::info
If the WorshipCommons team adds you as a co-publisher of a song -- for example, a song you co-wrote that someone else uploaded -- it appears in **My submissions** as **Live**, even though you didn't send it yourself. As a publisher of the song, you can make changes even when its license closes it to public edits.
:::

## Writer Pages

Every writer in the library has a writer page that lists their songs, with each song's year, key, themes, and license. Churches reach it by clicking a writer's name on a song page. When a song has several writers, each name links to its own page.

A writer page can also show a portrait, a short bio, links, and support links where churches can support the writer's other work.

### Editing Your Writer Page

You'll have a writer page once one of your songs is live and credits you on your own.

1. Click your name in the top menu and choose **My submissions**.
2. Click **Writer profile**.
3. Under **About you**, write a few sentences about who you are and the songs you write (up to 2,000 characters).
4. Under **Links**, add up to five links, such as your website. Give each a **Label** and its web address. Click **Add a link** for another row.
5. Under **Support links**, add places where churches can support your work, such as a store or ministry page. Click **Add a support link** for another row.
6. Click **Save profile**.

Click **View your writer page** to see it the way churches do. Your support links also appear as a **Songwriter's page** button on each of your song pages.

:::tip
Co-wrote your songs, or already have a writer page that isn't linked to your account? Email [support@churchapps.org](mailto:support@churchapps.org) and ask to have it linked.
:::
