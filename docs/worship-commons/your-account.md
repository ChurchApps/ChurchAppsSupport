# Your WorshipCommons Account and Writer Page

<div class="article-intro">

You can browse, play, print, and project songs on WorshipCommons without an account. A free account lets you save songs, share songs of your own, propose edits, and keep track of everything you've sent. If you write songs, your account also lets you edit the writer page churches see.

</div>

## Signing In or Creating an Account

WorshipCommons uses ChurchApps accounts. If you already have one (for example, from B1), you can use the same email and password.

1. Click **Sign in** in the top menu.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**, then click **Sign in**.

**To create an account:**

1. On the sign-in page, click **Create an account**. (If you arrived by clicking **For Songwriters**, the page starts on **Create account** already.)
2. Enter your **First name**, **Last name**, and **Email**, then click **Create account**.
3. Check your email for a 6-digit code, type it in, and click **Verify code**.
4. Choose a password of at least 8 characters, type it again under **Verify password**, and click **Set password & sign in**.

**Forgot your password?** Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page, enter your email, and click **Send code**. Then enter the code from your email and set a new password.

Once you're signed in, your first name appears in the top menu. Click it to open **My submissions** or **Report**, or to **Sign out**.

## Saved Songs

Saved songs are a simple favorites list stored in your account, so they appear on every device you sign in from. You can save a song from several places:

- Click the heart on any [song page](./song-page.md).
- Click the bookmark on an album card on the home page.
- Click **+ Save song** on the [Listen](./finding-songs.md#listening-to-new-songs) page.

Click **Saved songs** in the top menu to see every song you've saved, with its writer, year, and key. Click **Remove** to take a song off the list.

:::tip
To plan a set for a specific Sunday, with keys and sections, use [Service Plans](./service-plans.md) instead. Service plans don't need an account, and the **Saved songs** page links to them.
:::

## My Submissions

Click your name in the top menu and choose **My submissions** to see every song and change you've started or sent, and where each one stands. Use the tabs at the top -- **All**, **Drafts**, **In review**, **Live**, and **Not accepted** -- to filter the list. A tab only appears when it has something in it, and each one shows a count.

Each entry shows its type (**New song**, **Translation**, **Arrangement**, **Correction**, **Additional file**, **Master recording**, or **Removal request**), the writer and key, your note, when it was started or submitted, and its status:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Started but not sent yet. Click **Continue** to finish it or **Delete draft** to remove it and its files (this can't be undone). |
| **Changes requested** | A reviewer asked for changes. Their note is shown. Click **Continue**, make them, and send it again. |
| **In review** | A person reads every song before it goes live, usually within a few days. Click **Withdraw** to pull it back into a draft; your files stay. |
| **Live** | In the library and free for churches to use. You'll see its download count and its link. |
| **Not accepted** | It didn't make it into the library. The reason is shown (see below). |
| **Withdrawn** | You pulled it back before it was reviewed. |
| **Taken down** | The song was removed from the library, as you asked. |

While a submission is a **Draft** or **In review**, click its title to open a read-only preview of the song as the reviewer will see it: the details, license, chart, and any recording or score you attached. For a live song, click **Copy link** to share its page, **Propose a change** to send an edit, or **Request removal** to ask for it to be taken down. See [Proposing Edits and Reporting Songs](./proposing-edits.md).

### Why a Song Might Not Be Accepted

When a submission is **Not accepted**, the entry shows one of these reasons, often with a note from the reviewer:

- The review team felt this one isn't ready for the library yet.
- This looks like a song that's already in the library.
- There's a rights or licensing issue with this submission.
- This looks like a song in the CCLI catalog, so it can't be released here. A **Find it on SongSelect** link is shown.
- The words or melody appear to be AI-generated. The library only takes songs written by people; AI-assisted recordings of a human-written song are fine.
- This doesn't fit the worship-song library.
- This submission is missing something we need to publish it.

Questions about a decision go to [support@churchapps.org](mailto:support@churchapps.org).

:::info
If the WorshipCommons team adds you as a co-publisher of a song -- for example, a song you co-wrote that someone else uploaded -- it appears in **My submissions** as **Live**, even though you didn't send it yourself. As a publisher of the song, you can make changes even when its license closes it to public edits. Each account can share 20 songs; email support to raise the limit.
:::

## Writer Pages

Every writer in the library has a writer page that lists their songs, with each song's year, key, themes, and license. Churches reach it by clicking a writer's name on a song page. When a song has several writers, each name links to its own page.

A writer page can also show a portrait, a short bio, links, and support links where churches can support the writer's other work. For many historic hymn writers, the portrait and bio come from Wikipedia.

### Editing Your Writer Page

You'll have a writer page once one of your songs is live and credits you on your own.

1. Click your name in the top menu and choose **My submissions**.
2. Click **Writer profile**.
3. Under **About you**, write a few sentences about who you are and the songs you write (up to 2,000 characters).
4. Under **Links**, add up to five links, such as your website. Give each a **Label** and its web address. Click **Add a link** for another row and **Remove** to drop one.
5. Under **Support links**, add places where churches can support your work, such as a store or ministry page. Click **Add a support link** for another row. Worship use is always free; these links are never a condition of the grant.
6. Click **Save profile**.

Click **View your writer page** to see it the way churches do. Your support links also appear as a **Songwriter's page** button on each of your song pages (or a **Songwriter's pages** menu when there's more than one).

:::tip
Co-wrote your songs, or already have a writer page that isn't linked to your account? Email [support@churchapps.org](mailto:support@churchapps.org) and ask to have it linked.
:::

## Site Language

WorshipCommons is available in nine languages: **English**, **Español**, **Deutsch**, **Français**, **Português**, **Русский**, **Magyar**, **Shqip**, and **മലയാളം**. The site starts in your browser's language when it's one of these, and in English otherwise.

To change it, use the globe picker at the bottom of any page and choose a language. The choice is remembered in your browser, not in your account. Changing the language also changes the default **Language** filter on the Discover page, so you see songs in that language first (see [Finding Songs](./finding-songs.md#filtering-songs)). The legal text of the WorshipCommons License stays in English in every language.
