# Finding Songs on WorshipCommons

<div class="article-intro">

The **Discover** page is the WorshipCommons song library. Search for a song you already know, or narrow the list with filters to find something new for Sunday. The home page, the seasonal collections, and the **Listen** page are quicker ways in. You don't need to sign in to search or play songs.

</div>

## Starting from the Home Page

The home page at [worshipcommons.org](https://worshipcommons.org) is a shortcut into the library:

- Type in the search box and click **Search** to open Discover with your search already applied.
- Click a chip under the search box to open a ready-made list: **All Songs**, **Start here** (forty well-known hymns with a score and a chart), **Timeless Hymns** (public domain), **Modern Worship**, **Acoustic** (songs that work with just a guitar), **New Releases**, or **Kids & VBS**.
- The first row of album cards shows the top songs in your site language, labeled **Sunday-ready** or **Start here** depending on what the library holds. Click **Explore all songs →** (or **See all 40 →**) for the full list.
- **Just added to the commons** shows songs published in the last 30 days that have cover art. Click **See what's new →** for every new song.

Each album card has a play button (a demo recording when the writer shared one, otherwise a piano preview of the melody) and a bookmark that saves the song to your account. You'll be asked to sign in the first time you save.

## Searching the Library

1. Click **Discover** (or **Browse Songs**) at the top of any page.
2. Type in the search box. You can search by title, first line, tune name, theme, scripture reference, or writer. Results update as you type.
3. Use the **Sort by** menu to order results by **Most downloaded**, **Most saved**, **Newest**, or **Title A–Z**.
4. Click a song title to open its [song page](./song-page.md).

:::tip
Press the `/` key anywhere on the Discover page to jump to the search box.
:::

Results come 50 to a page; use the numbered buttons under the list to move between pages. Each row shows:

- A play button. Click it to hear a demo recording or a piano preview without leaving the list. The button is greyed out, with the tooltip **No demo yet**, when the song has nothing to play.
- The song's cover, writer, year, and scripture reference, plus a **stems** flag when a multitrack pack is available.
- Download and save counts.
- A short reason the song ranks where it does, such as **in 200+ hymnals**, **has score**, or **chart with chords**.
- Up to three themes, the key, and the tempo in BPM.

**Most downloaded** is the default sort. It blends how often a song is used with how complete it is, so songs with a score and a chart rise toward the top.

## Filtering Songs

Click **Filters** to open or close the filter panel (it starts open on larger screens). Within a group you can pick more than one value; across groups, a song must match every filter you set. The number next to each option shows how many songs match. Click a group's heading to collapse it.

- **Theme** -- Topics such as Praise, Grace, Hope, or Christmas. The five most common themes show first; click **Show more** for the full list.
- **Confidence** -- How complete the song's materials are. See the table below.
- **Ready to use** -- **Works with just a guitar** (a chord chart plus accompaniment or a score), **Has accompaniment**, **Has chart**, **Has score**, and **Has stems**.
- **Key** and **Meter** -- Pick a specific key or hymn meter. The **Meter** group only appears when songs in the library carry a meter.
- **Tempo** -- **Slow · under 73**, **Moderate · 73–100**, or **Upbeat · 100+** BPM.
- **Language** -- Songs in a particular language. Languages marked **browse** have songs you can search but don't yet have a full catalog of their own.
- **Source** -- The song's license: **Public domain**, **Free for worship**, one of the Creative Commons licenses, or **Custom** (a writer's own grant). Licenses marked with ⚠ are non-commercial. See [Licenses and Rights](./licenses-and-rights.md).
- **Extras** -- **Has demo recording**.

Active filters appear as chips above the results. Click a chip's **X** to remove it, or click **Clear all filters** to start over.

### What the Confidence Levels Mean

| Confidence | What the song has |
| --- | --- |
| **Sunday-ready** | Score, listened-to audio, chart, slides, and rights that agree |
| **Score** | A melody score from a written source or Open Hymnal notation |
| **Unchecked score** | A score made by machine from a MIDI file or recording; check it before you print |
| **Chart only** | Lyrics with chords, but no melody score yet |
| **Lyrics only** | Words only, with no chords or score yet |

:::info
The **Language** filter starts on the language you've picked for the site, so you see songs in your language first. To see songs in every language, remove the language chip above the results. To change the site's language, use the globe picker at the bottom of any page (see [Site Language](./your-account.md#site-language)).
:::

## When a Song Isn't There

If nothing matches, you'll see **Nothing matches yet**. Loosen a filter or clear your search. WorshipCommons only holds songs that are in the public domain or that writers chose to share, so most copyrighted songs aren't here. When a search comes up empty you can click **share the song the commons is missing** to upload one, or **Search SongSelect →** to look for the song in the CCLI catalog instead.

## Collections

Collections are ready-made lists with a short explanation of what the songs may be used for. The links at the bottom of every page lead to four of them:

- **New songs** -- Every song added to the library, newest first, grouped by month. Shows the last 100; click **Browse all songs** for the rest.
- **Public domain hymns** -- English hymns first published in 1930 or earlier, which are free for every use in the United States.
- **No CCLI needed** -- Up to 80 of the most-used songs your church can use without reporting to CCLI, with answers to common licensing questions.
- **Christmas songs** -- Carols and Christmas hymns.

Seasonal collections for **Advent**, **Easter**, **Communion**, and **Thanksgiving** are linked at the bottom of every collection page. Each collection row shows the writer, year, key, and license, and longer collections are grouped by language with English first.

## Listening to New Songs

Click **Listen** in the top menu to hear recently shared songs from writers (not hymns), shuffled, one after another. The page plays the newest 50 English songs that have a recording.

- Press play on the player. When a song ends the next one starts on its own.
- Use **⏮** and **⏭** to skip, **+15s** to jump ahead fifteen seconds, or click any track in the playlist below to play it.
- Click **+ Save song** to add the song to your saved songs (you'll be asked to sign in), or **Song details** to open its page.

On a phone, the lock-screen and headphone controls skip tracks too.
