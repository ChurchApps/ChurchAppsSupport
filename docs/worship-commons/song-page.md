# The WorshipCommons Song Page

<div class="article-intro">

Every song on WorshipCommons has its own page with the lyrics and chords, a way to hear the tune, practice tools, printable charts, and downloads. Everything on this page works without signing in, except saving the song to your account.

</div>

## What's at the Top

The top of the page shows the song's cover, whether it's a **Hymn** or a **Song**, its title, first line, writer and year, and the hymn tune name when there is one. Below that you'll see:

- **License badge** -- For example **Public domain**, **WC** (Free for worship), a Creative Commons badge such as **CC BY**, or **Custom** for a writer's own grant. See [Licenses and Rights](./licenses-and-rights.md).
- **CCLI number** -- Shown as a tag when the writer listed one. Reporting to CCLI is optional for songs on this site.
- **Themes and meter** -- Click a theme or meter tag to find other songs that share it.
- **Key, BPM, time signature, and estimated sing time**, plus a **Recommended key** (with the reason) when the song has one.

On the right are the main actions:

- **Lead worship** -- Opens a full-screen, follow-along lyrics view with music. See [Presenting and Exporting Songs](./presenting-and-exporting.md).
- **+ Add to service plan** -- Adds the song to one of your [service plans](./service-plans.md).
- **Heart (Save song)** -- Saves the song to your account. You'll be asked to sign in first.
- **Songwriter's page** -- Appears when the writer has added links where churches can support their work.

Click the writer's name to open their [writer page](./your-account.md#writer-pages), which lists all of their songs in the library. When a song credits several people, each name links to its own page.

## Hearing the Song

When a song has audio, a player strip appears under the title. Click the play button to hear it.

- If the writer shared a recording, you'll hear the **Demo recording** or **Master recording**.
- Otherwise you'll hear a **Piano preview** -- a piano playing the melody file. The piano preview follows the key you choose on the page, so you hear the song where you'll sing it. The play button reads **Preview (synthesized)** to remind you it isn't a recording.

:::info
A translation plays its original song's recording when it has none of its own. The player says so: **Sung in English · as shared by** the original writer.
:::

The **Recordings** panel on the right lists every recording separately, each with its own player: the **Master recording** (with its license), the **Demo recording**, and the **Accompaniment** track without the lead vocal. If the writer added a video link, click **Watch a performance** to open it on YouTube.

## Chords & Lyrics

The **Chords & lyrics** tab shows the chart. Use the toolbar above it to set it up for your team:

1. **Key** -- Choose a new key and every chord transposes. The original key is marked **(original)**.
2. **Capo** -- Pick a capo fret from 1 to 7. The chart shows the chord shapes to play (for example, **2 — C shapes**) while the song still sounds in the key you chose.
3. **Transpose** -- Click **−** or **+** to move the key down or up one half step at a time. The number between them shows how far you are from the original key.
4. **Display** -- Turn **Chords** off for a lyrics-only view, or turn on **Nashville** to show chords as numbers. (**Nashville** is only available while **Chords** is on.)
5. **Text size** -- **Small**, **Medium**, or **Large**.
6. **Columns** -- Show the chart in **1** or **2** columns.

Hover over or tap any chord to see how to play it on **guitar** and **piano**. With a capo set, the guitar diagram shows the shape to play and the piano diagram shows the chord that sounds. For songs with more than two sections, use the **Jump to** buttons to scroll straight to a verse or chorus.

:::info
Songs that only have lyrics don't show the key, capo, or chord controls, because there are no chords to change.
:::

Under the last verse the song's copyright and license line is printed. Under the chart you'll find:

- **Print / PDF** -- Opens a printable chart in the key, capo, and chord setting you chose. On that page, pick a **Text size** (**Small**, **Medium**, **Large**, or **Large print** for a music stand), choose **2 columns** or **Show chords**, then click **Print**. Use your browser's print dialog to save it as a PDF. The footer carries the attribution, the CCLI number if there is one, and the license of each part of the song.
- **Chart PDF** -- Appears when the song has a ready-made chart PDF.
- **ChordPro** and **Lyrics (TXT)** -- Download the song as a ChordPro file or plain text, in the original key.
- **Copy lyrics** -- Copies the words (without chords) to your clipboard.

A line at the bottom tells you whether the song needs to be reported to CCLI. Most songs on WorshipCommons read **Free to sing, print, project and stream. No reporting required.**

## Sheet Music

Songs with a score, a sheet music PDF, or a melody file show a **Sheet music** tab.

- **Melody** -- The melody line of the first verse, engraved in the key you've chosen on the page. Click **Full score with all parts →** to open the full score. On that page, pick a **Key** and a **Part** (**All parts**, or **Soprano**, **Alto**, **Tenor**, **Bass** when the score has them) and click **Print**.
- **Sheet music** -- If the writer uploaded a sheet music PDF, it appears here with **Open full size →** and **Download PDF** links.
- If a song has a melody file but no engraved score, a link reading **No sheet music yet — help transcribe it** appears for signed-in users. See [Transcribing a Score](./proposing-edits.md#transcribing-a-score).

:::info
When a hymn is sung to a tune borrowed from another hymn, the score shows the music without words.
:::

## About & Rights

The **About & rights** tab answers "where does this come from, and may we use it?" It shows:

- **About the writer** -- For many hymn writers, a portrait and short bio from Wikipedia. Otherwise the writer's name and year.
- The song's scripture passage, its **Themes**, its **Meter** and tune, and how many hymnals it appears in.
- **Sing it to another tune** -- Other hymn tunes in the same meter that carry this text.
- **Contributors & changes** -- Who has worked on the song and the edits that were approved, with dates and notes.
- **Sources & outputs** -- A diagram of the files the song has (lyrics, chords, word timings, recording, melody MIDI, scores, sheet music, video, cover art) and what each one unlocks. Point at a source or an output to trace the connection. Green means the song has it, amber means it could be built, grey means it's missing. If you have a missing file, click **Add it to this song →** to propose it.
- **Rights** -- What the license lets you do, what it doesn't, and what you must do, with a use-by-use table and **Copy attribution** to copy the credit line for a bulletin or slide. See [Licenses and Rights](./licenses-and-rights.md).

If the song's license allows it, the tab ends with **Made an arrangement or translation? Add it back.**

## Practice Tools

The **Practice** panel helps your team rehearse:

- **Tempo** -- Appears when the song has a melody file. Slow the music down or speed it up (50%–150%) using the slider or the **−** and **+** buttons. The BPM shown updates as you move it.
- **Practice parts** -- When the melody file has more than one part (such as **Soprano**, **Alto**, **Tenor**, and **Bass**), click a part to hear it alone, or **All** to hear them together.
- **▶ Metronome** -- Hear a click at the current tempo, in the song's time signature. Click **■ Stop metronome** to stop.
- **Play G** (or whichever key is on the page) -- Plays the starting note of the key, so you can pitch the room.
- **Click** -- A downloadable click track, when the song's pack includes one.

## Downloads and Projection

The **Downloads** panel always offers **Chord chart (print)** in your current key and capo. Depending on what the writer shared, you may also see:

- **Composition pack** (ZIP) -- Chord chart, lead sheet, sheet music, MIDI, ChordPro, and license.
- **Audio pack** (ZIP) -- Master recording, full mix, instrumental, extras, and license.
- **Multitracks (ZIP)** -- Stems in the recorded key.

The panel also shows the song's **Downloads** and **Saves** counts.

The **Projection** panel opens the lyrics projector and exports slides to FreeShow, PowerPoint, OpenLP, ProPresenter, or OnSong and Planning Center. See [Presenting and Exporting Songs](./presenting-and-exporting.md).

## Improve It

The **Improve it** panel has **Propose an edit**, **Add a master recording** (when the song has none), and **Report this song**. See [Proposing Edits and Reporting Songs](./proposing-edits.md).

## Scripture, Related Songs, and Translations

At the bottom of the page:

- **Related songs** -- Other versions of the song in the same language (the **Original** and its arrangements) and songs with similar titles, each with a short reason. Click **View all →** to jump to the About & rights tab.
- **Scripture connection** -- Shows the song's scripture reference with the passage text from the World English Bible. If a song has no reference yet, click **Propose one →** to suggest one.
- **Translations** -- The song in other languages. Click **Add one →** to share a translation (see [Sharing a Song](./sharing-a-song.md)). If the song's license doesn't allow translations, you'll see **Translations need the writer's permission.**
