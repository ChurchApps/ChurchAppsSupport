# Getting Around FreePlay Admin

<div class="article-intro">

FreePlay Admin at [admin.freeplay.church](https://admin.freeplay.church) is where you manage TVs that run in Scheduled Mode. This page covers signing in, the four pages across the top, the search box that doubles as a command palette, and the small settings in your user menu.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- Have a ChurchApps login for your church. It's the same account you use for B1 Admin
- Use a computer or tablet. The pages work on a phone, but the calendar grid is easier to read on a wider screen

</div>

## Signing In

1. Go to [admin.freeplay.church](https://admin.freeplay.church).
2. Sign in with your ChurchApps email and password, the same login you use for B1 Admin. There's no separate FreePlay account.
3. If your login belongs to more than one church, pick the church whose screens you want to manage. You can switch churches later from the user menu at the top right; FreePlay Admin returns to the **Screens** page when you do.

After signing in you land on **Screens**. If you opened a specific link, such as a pairing link from a TV's QR code, FreePlay Admin takes you there instead.

:::tip
Scanning the QR code on a TV's **Pair this screen** screen opens **freeplay.church/pair** with the code already filled in. Sign in if asked, and the **Pair screen** form appears on the **Screens** page with the code typed for you. See [Pairing a Screen](./pairing-a-screen.md).
:::

## The Four Pages

The header has a menu with the four main pages:

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Screens** | Every TV paired to your church, their status, and the **Groups** view for screen groups. See [Managing Screens and Groups](./managing-screens.md) |
| **Playlists** | Loops of images and videos you pick from your providers. See [Connecting Providers and Building Playlists](./building-playlists.md) |
| **Calendar** | What each screen group plays, and when. See [Scheduling the Calendar](./scheduling-the-calendar.md) |
| **Providers** | The content sources your playlists pull from, connected once for the whole church. See [Connecting Providers](./building-playlists.md#connecting-providers) |

The search box in the header shows where you are, for example **Screens › Lobby left**. Click the FreePlay logo next to it to return to **Screens**.

## The Command Palette

The search box in the header is a command palette. Open it by clicking it, by pressing **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac), or by pressing **/** when you aren't typing in a field. Then type a few letters and press **Enter**, or use the arrow keys:

- **Sections** - Jump to **Screens** (with its **Groups** view underneath), **Playlists**, **Calendar**, or **Providers**. Press the **Right arrow** on **Screens** to reveal **Groups**
- **Actions** - **Pair screen**, **Add playlist**, and **Add schedule** open the matching form right away
- **Also** - **Open B1 Admin** in a new tab, and **Help · support.churchapps.org/freeplay**, which opens these support pages

Press **Esc** to close the palette. If nothing matches what you typed, it says **Nothing for that.**

## Your User Menu

Click your name or avatar at the top right to open the user menu. Besides switching churches and signing out, it has a **Theme** toggle with **Light** and **Dark**. FreePlay Admin starts in whichever mode your device prefers and remembers your choice in that browser.

## The Get Started Checklist

Until each step is done, the **Screens** page shows a **Get started** checklist with four steps and a link for each: **Connect a provider**, **Build a playlist**, **Put it on the calendar**, and **Pair a screen**. Completed steps show a check mark. Click **Hide** to dismiss the checklist for good in this browser. See [Scheduled Mode](./index.md#getting-started) for what each step involves.
