# Scheduled Mode

<div class="article-intro">

Scheduled Mode lets a FreePlay TV run itself. You build playlists and put them on a calendar in **FreePlay Admin** at [admin.freeplay.church](https://admin.freeplay.church). Each TV paired to that calendar downloads the week ahead and plays whatever is scheduled, by the clock, with nobody pressing Start. Because the files are already downloaded, playback keeps going even if the Wi-Fi drops.

</div>

:::tip
FreePlay Admin uses your ChurchApps login, the same one you use for B1 Admin. There's no separate account to create.
:::

## How It Fits Together

FreePlay Admin has four main pages, listed across the top of the site:

- **[Screens](./managing-screens)** - The TVs you've paired, their status, and the **screen groups** they belong to. Screens in a group share one calendar and one default loop
- **[Playlists](./building-playlists)** - Loops of images and videos you pick from your content providers
- **[Calendar](./scheduling-the-calendar)** - What each screen group plays, and when
- **[Providers](./building-playlists#connecting-providers)** - The content sources your playlists pull from. You connect each one once for the whole church

When nothing is scheduled, a group's screens play its **default loop**. If the group has no default loop, the screens go dark.

## How It Differs from Plan Mode

- **[Plan Mode](../plan-mode/)** follows one service plan type from B1 Admin. Someone presses **Start Plan** on the TV, and the lesson plays when they do
- **Scheduled Mode** follows a calendar. Content starts and stops at the times you set, so it works well for lobby signage, announcement loops, and classrooms where the lesson should be up before anyone walks in

A calendar entry can still play a service plan. Pick the plan type under **Service plans** when you add a schedule, and the screen plays whatever plan is current in B1 Admin that week.

## Getting Started

Until you've done each of these, the **Screens** page shows a **Get started** checklist with a link for every step:

1. **Connect a provider** - Link Dropbox, OneDrive, Lessons.church, or another source on the **Providers** page
2. **Build a playlist** - Pick images and videos from a provider
3. **Put it on the calendar** - Choose what plays when, or set a default loop for the group
4. **Pair a screen** - Enter the four-letter code shown on the TV

Click **Hide** to dismiss the checklist.

:::tip
Press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac), or click the search box in the header, to open the command palette. From there you can jump to any page or start an action like **Pair screen**, **Add playlist**, or **Add schedule**.
:::
