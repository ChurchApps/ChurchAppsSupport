# Browsing and Downloading Content

<div class="article-intro">

Once you have connected to a content provider, you can browse its library, download files, and play them on your TV. The content browser provides a visual grid of folders and files that you navigate with your TV remote.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- Connect to at least one content provider -- see [Connecting to Providers](./connecting-providers.md)
- Have your TV remote ready for navigation

</div>

## The Content Browser

The content browser displays items in a 3-column grid of cards. Each card shows a thumbnail image and a title. There are two types of items:

- **Folders** - Represented by a thumbnail or folder icon. Select a folder to see its contents.
- **Files** - Individual media items (videos or images). Video files display a play icon overlay on their thumbnail.

The header at the top of the screen shows the name of the current folder, or the provider name if you are at the root level. Inside a folder, a breadcrumb trail below the header shows the path you took, for example **Lessons.church › Lessons › Preschool**. Each card shows its title underneath, and file cards also say **Image** or **Video**.

While FreePlay loads a folder it shows placeholder cards, and while it opens the one you selected it dims the screen with **Loading content...**.

## Navigating Folders

1. Use the directional pad on your remote to highlight a folder
2. Press **Select** to open it
3. The browser loads the folder's contents, which may be more folders or playable files
4. Press **Back** on your remote to go up one level

You can drill as deep as the provider's content structure allows. The browser keeps track of your folder path so you can navigate back through each level, and it remembers which card you had highlighted when you come back up.

Pressing **Back** at the top level of a provider opens the sidebar instead.

## Browsing Lessons.church

The [Lessons.church](../../lessons-church/index.md) library is organized the same way as its website. At the top level you'll see two folders:

- **Lessons** - Drill in by **program**, then **study**, then **lesson**. Each lesson then lists its **venues**, which are versions of the lesson for a particular age group or setting, such as Preschool or Elementary. Select a venue to open the download screen for that version of the lesson
- **Add-Ons** - Extra videos and resources, grouped by category

Press **Back** at any level to go up one step.

## Browsing GO! Curriculum

After you [sign in with your gocurriculum.com account](./connecting-providers.md#phone-sign-in-qr-code-only), GO! Curriculum opens to its age groups, **Elementary** and **Preschool**. Drill in by age group, then collection, then lesson, and select a lesson to open its download screen. Small Group guides are text-only, so FreePlay leaves them out.

What you see depends on your Go membership:

- Only the age groups your membership covers are listed. If your login has no Go membership, the library is empty
- Some videos are only for certain membership levels. Videos your membership doesn't include are left out of the lesson

## Downloading and Playing Content

When you select a folder that contains playable files (or select an individual file), FreePlay shows the content title over a cover image and two choices:

- **Download** - Save every file to the TV first, so playback doesn't depend on Wi-Fi. A progress bar fills as each file is saved and reads **Downloading Item X of Y**. When it's done, the **Start** button appears with a play icon. Press **Select** to begin playback
- **Stream** - Start playing right away over the internet without saving the files

Selecting a single file plays its whole folder, starting from that file. Press **Back** on this screen to return to the folder you came from.

If every file in the folder is already on the TV, FreePlay skips the choice and shows **Start** right away.

:::tip
Download lessons before Sunday morning while your building has a strong internet connection. Once downloaded, the content plays offline, and if you play it again later it loads instantly without downloading again.
:::

:::info
Before a download starts, FreePlay checks the TV's free space and removes old downloads if it's running low. See [Downloads and Storage](../getting-started/downloads-and-storage.md).
:::

## Managing Downloads

Open **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar, then select **Downloads** to see everything saved on this TV. Select a download to play it, or highlight it and select the trash can icon in its corner to delete it. See [Downloads and Storage](../getting-started/downloads-and-storage.md) for the details.

## Playing Provider Content

Use these controls during playback:

- **Right arrow** - Next item
- **Left arrow** - Previous item
- **Select / Play-Pause** - Pause or resume video
- **Up arrow** - Open the message selector to jump to a specific item
- **Back** - Exit to the content browser

When you exit the player, you return to the content browser in the folder that holds the content you were playing.

:::info
For a complete reference of all player controls, see [Playing Content](./playing-content.md).
:::

## Switching Between Providers

Connected providers appear as items under the **Browse** heading in the sidebar on the left. To switch to a different provider:

1. Press the **Left** arrow on your remote to expand the sidebar
2. Select the provider you want to browse
3. The content browser refreshes with that provider's library

:::warning
If a folder shows **No content available** with **Try Different Folder**, the provider does not have any items at that level. Press **Back** and pick another folder, or check back later.
:::
