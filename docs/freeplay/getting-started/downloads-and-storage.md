# Downloads and Storage

<div class="article-intro">

Everything FreePlay plays offline lives in the TV's storage. The **Downloads** screen under **Settings** lists the lessons and folders you've saved, lets you play them again without a connection, and lets you delete them to free up space. FreePlay also cleans up old downloads by itself when the device gets full.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- Install FreePlay and launch it. See [Getting Started](./index.md)
- Download something first, for example a lesson from a provider. See [Browsing and Downloading Content](../content-providers/browsing-content.md)

</div>

## Opening the Downloads Screen

1. Open **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Select **Downloads** (the row reads **Play or delete content saved on this device**).

The **Downloads** screen shows a grid of cards, three to a row. Each card has a cover image (or a download icon when there's no image), a title, and a description when there is one. The newest download is listed first.

If nothing has been saved to this TV, the screen says **No downloads yet**.

## What Is Listed Here

The Downloads screen lists the content you saved on purpose:

- **Provider downloads** - A lesson or folder you downloaded with the **Download** button from a content provider
- **Plans** - The current service plan downloaded in [Plan Mode](../plan-mode/index.md)

Two kinds of saved files are managed elsewhere and don't appear in this list:

- **Scheduled content** - Files a TV in [Scheduled Mode](../scheduled-mode/index.md) downloads for its calendar. FreePlay adds and removes these on its own as the calendar changes
- **Announcement slides** - Managed from the provider's **Provider Settings**. See [Announcements](../content-providers/announcements.md)

:::info
FreePlay checks each download when the screen opens. If any of a download's files are missing from storage, that download is dropped from the list. Download it again from the provider to get it back.
:::

## Playing a Download

1. Highlight a card with your remote and press **Select**.
2. The player opens and plays the content from storage, so it works with no internet connection.
3. Press **Back** to return to the **Downloads** screen.

See [Playing Content](../content-providers/playing-content.md) for the remote controls during playback.

## Deleting a Download

1. Highlight the card you want to remove. A trash can icon appears in its top-right corner.
2. Move to the trash can icon and press **Select**. You can also press and hold **Select** on the card.
3. The download's files are removed right away and the card disappears. There is no confirmation step.

A deleted lesson can be downloaded again from its provider at any time.

## How FreePlay Frees Space on Its Own

When free space falls below about 500 MB, FreePlay removes the downloads you haven't played for the longest time until about 1 GB is free again. It removes whole lessons, never half of one, and it never removes the lesson it's downloading right now.

This check runs each time FreePlay starts a new download, including when a TV in Scheduled Mode fetches its calendar content. A TV that is paired to a calendar can therefore clear out old provider downloads to make room for what's scheduled.

:::warning
A TV in Scheduled Mode stops downloading calendar content when less than about 300 MB is free and nothing else can be removed. In FreePlay Admin, such a screen shows **N files missing**. Delete downloads you no longer need, or move to a device with more storage. See [Choosing a Device](./choosing-a-device.md).
:::

## Checking Free Space

FreePlay doesn't show storage figures on the TV. Your device's own settings do, for example under **Settings > My Fire TV > About** on a Fire TV.

For a TV paired to a calendar, FreePlay Admin shows storage too. Open the screen from the **Screens** page and look at **Storage** in the **Device** section, which reads **N GB free**. See [Managing Screens and Groups](../scheduled-mode/managing-screens.md).
