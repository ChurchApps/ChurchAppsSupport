# Assigning Groups

<div class="article-intro">

When you tap a service time button on the household review screen, the app opens the **Select a Group** screen. Here you choose which group or class a family member should attend for that specific service time.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- [Review your household](./household-review.md) on the household review screen
- Expand the member you want to assign and tap a service time button
- Ensure your church administrator has [created groups](../../b1-admin/groups/creating-groups.md) and linked them to service times in B1 Admin

</div>

## How Groups Are Organized

The top of the screen shows who you are choosing for -- **Choosing for Emma Smith -- 9:00 AM**, with the person's photo -- so there is no mixing up siblings.

Groups are displayed in **categories**. Each category appears as a card showing:

- The **category name** (for example, "Children", "Youth", "Adults")
- The **number of groups** in that category

Tap a category card to expand it and see the individual groups inside. Tap it again to collapse it. If there is only one category, it is expanded for you; if the person already has a group for this service time, that group's category opens automatically.

## Selecting a Group

1. Tap a **category** to expand it.
2. Review the list of groups within that category. Each group shows its name with a selection circle on the right; the group currently assigned is highlighted with a check mark.
3. Tap the **group name** you want to assign.

The app saves your selection and returns you to the [household review screen](./household-review.md). The service time button for that member now shows the name of the group you selected.

## Age and Grade Guidance

If your church has set age or grade ranges on its rooms, the list guides you to the right one:

- Rooms the person qualifies for carry an **Eligible** badge, are tinted green, and float to the top of their category.
- Rooms outside their age or grade carry an **Outside age range** badge, are dimmed, and sink to the bottom.
- Rooms with no range set, or people with no birth date or grade on file, show no badge.

A dimmed room can still be chosen: tapping it asks **Emma Smith is outside Nursery's age range -- check in anyway?** Tap **Check In Anyway** to assign it or **Cancel** to pick another room. Ages and grades are figured from your church's grade promotion date, so rooms stay accurate across the school-year rollover. Staff configure the ranges in B1 Admin; see [Age and Grade Room Guidance](../../b1-admin/attendance/checkin-safety.md#age-and-grade-room-guidance).

## Closed Rooms

A room that staff have closed in B1 Admin shows a red **Closed** badge and is dimmed. Tapping it asks **Nursery is closed for check-in -- check in anyway?** Tap **Check In Anyway** only if a staff member says so; otherwise **Cancel** and choose an open room. Even if you proceed, the server may still refuse the check-in if the room is full or closed; see [Full Rooms and Volunteer Ratios](./completing-checkin.md#full-rooms-and-volunteer-ratios).

## Choosing No Group

If a family member should not be assigned to any group for this service time, tap **No group this time** at the bottom of the screen. This clears any existing group assignment for that service time and returns you to the household review screen.

:::tip
Use **No group this time** for adults who attend the main worship service and do not need to check into a specific group or class. A person with no group for any service time is not checked in at all.
:::

## Changing a Group Assignment

To change a group assignment that has already been made:

1. Go back to the [household review screen](./household-review.md).
2. Expand the member whose group you want to change.
3. Tap the service time button (which now shows the currently assigned group name).
4. Select a different group or tap **No group this time**.

Tap the **back arrow** next to the **Select a Group** title to return without changing anything.

:::info
Groups and categories are configured by your church administrator in B1 Admin. If a group is missing or in the wrong category, ask your admin to update the [group settings](../../b1-admin/groups/creating-groups.md). If the screen says **No groups available for this time**, no groups have been linked to that service time yet.
:::
