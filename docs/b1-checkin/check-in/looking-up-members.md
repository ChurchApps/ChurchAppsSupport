# Looking Up Members

<div class="article-intro">

After selecting a service, you arrive at the member lookup screen -- the screen the kiosk sits on between families. It greets you with **Welcome! Let's check you in.** and a phone keypad. Search by phone number or by last name, tap your name, and the kiosk loads your household.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Before You Begin</h4>

- [Select a service](./selecting-service.md) from the services screen
- Ensure your household has been [added to the system](../../b1-admin/people/adding-people.md) by a church administrator

</div>

## Searching by Phone Number

The lookup screen opens in phone mode with a large on-screen keypad.

1. Type at least the **last 4 digits** of a phone number on file for anyone in your household. The digits appear above the keypad, formatted as a phone number as you go; the kiosk accepts up to 10 digits. The hint **Your full number works too** reminds you that typing more is fine.
2. Matching people start appearing above the keypad a moment after you stop typing (see [Results as You Type](#results-as-you-type)). Tap your name to continue, or tap **Search** for the full results screen.

If you type exactly 4 digits, the kiosk matches them against the **last 4 digits** of the phone numbers on file. If you type more than 4, it matches the full number you entered. Use the backspace key to correct a digit or the clear key to start over.

## Searching by Name

1. Tap **Search by name** (the keyboard key at the bottom-left of the keypad).
2. In the **Enter last name** box, type at least **2 letters** of the last name. The screen reminds you: **Please enter at least two letters of the last name.**
3. Matching people appear below the box as you type. Tap your name, or tap **Search** (or the keyboard's search key) for the full results screen.

To go back to the keypad, tap **Use phone number**.

## Results as You Type

You don't have to tap **Search**. Once you've entered at least 4 digits or 2 letters, matching people appear under the keypad or the name box a moment after you stop typing, and the list updates as you keep typing. Tap a person in the list to continue. If nothing matches yet, the list says **No Matches Found**; keep typing or check the number.

Tapping **Search** shows the full results screen instead, headed with the number of results (for example **3 results**) and a **Back to keypad** button. Each result shows the person's **name** and **photo** (if one is on file).

## Selecting a Person

Tap a person's row. The kiosk loads every member of that person's household and opens the [household review screen](./household-review.md). The row shows a spinner while it loads.

If no results are found, the screen shows **No Matches Found** with a hint to try a different phone number or last name. Tap **Try again** to clear the search, or **Search by name** / **Use phone number** to switch modes. If the search itself fails -- usually because the tablet lost its connection -- the kiosk shows **Unable to complete search. Please try again.**

:::tip
Searching by the last 4 digits of a phone number is the fastest way to look up your family. Encourage members to use this method for a quicker check-in experience.
:::

:::warning
Each adult's mobile phone must be on their person record for phone search to find them. If a family can't be found by phone, search by last name, or ask a volunteer for help getting their [phone number added](../../b1-admin/people/adding-people.md). First-time visitors can use [Register as guest](#registering-as-a-guest) when it is enabled.
:::

## Other Buttons on the Lookup Screen

Below the keypad you may see up to three more actions, depending on how the church and this tablet are set up:

- **Scan code** -- for families who already [checked in on their phone](./scanning-codes.md) and need labels printed.
- **Register as guest** -- shown when the church has turned on QR guest registration (see below).
- **Check out** -- shown only on [manned stations](../getting-started/admin-settings.md#self-service-and-manned-stations); opens the [check-out flow](./checking-out.md).

If the kiosk sits untouched on this screen long enough, the church's [idle screen](../getting-started/kiosk-display.md#idle-screen) slideshow starts (when one is configured). Tap anywhere to return to the keypad.

## Registering as a Guest

If your church has QR guest registration turned on, a **Register as guest** link appears below the keypad. First-time visitors can use it to add their family to the church's system before checking in.

1. Tap **Register as guest**. A window opens with a QR code and the message **Scan to register as a guest**.
2. Choose how to fill in the [guest registration form](../../b1-church/checkin/guest-registration.md):
   - **On your phone** -- Scan the QR code, fill in the form on your phone, and tap **OK** to close the window.
   - **On the kiosk** -- Tap **Register here** to open the same form right on the tablet. Fill it in and tap **Register**, then tap **Done** (or the back arrow) to return to the lookup screen.
3. Once registered, search for your family by name or phone number to check in.

:::info
The form opened with **Register here** stays on the guest registration page -- visitors can't browse to other parts of the church website from the kiosk. If no one touches or types in the form for 2 minutes, the kiosk closes it and returns to the lookup screen. Each time the form closes, anything typed into it is cleared so the next family starts with a blank form.
:::

Church staff turn this on and choose the form's fields in B1 Admin; see [Guest Registration via QR Code](../../b1-admin/attendance/check-in.md#guest-registration-via-qr-code).

## Printing Labels for a Phone Check-In

Families who already checked in on their phone through [B1 Church self check-in](../../b1-church/checkin/self-checkin.md) can print their name tags at the kiosk without looking themselves up: tap **Scan code** and hold the phone's QR code up to the camera. A station can also be set to open on the scanner. Both are covered in [Scanning a Phone Check-In Code](./scanning-codes.md).
