# Getting Started with B1 Church Checkin

<div class="article-intro">

Setting up B1 Church Checkin takes just a few minutes. This section walks you through installing the app on an iPad or Android tablet, logging in with your ChurchApps account, optionally configuring a network label printer, and protecting the kiosk's admin settings with a PIN.

</div>

## What You Need

- **An iPad or Android tablet** -- A 10-inch tablet works best for the kiosk experience. The app runs in portrait orientation.
- **A ChurchApps account with check-in access** -- The email and password you use for B1 Admin or other ChurchApps products, with the **Checkin** permission at your church (see [Check-In Kiosk Accounts](../../b1-admin/settings/roles-permissions.md#check-in-kiosk-accounts)).
- **Wi-Fi** -- The kiosk needs an internet connection for every check-in; see [Troubleshooting and Offline Behavior](./troubleshooting.md).
- **A network label printer** (optional) -- A Brother QL or Zebra label printer on the same Wi-Fi network, for printing name labels and parent pickup slips. The app can scan your local network to find it.

## Installation

1. Open the app store on your tablet: the [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/b1-church-check-in/id6775081998) on iPad, the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=church.b1.checkin) on Android, or the [Amazon App Store](https://www.amazon.com/Live-Church-Solutions-B1-Check-In/dp/B0FW5HKRB5/) on a Fire tablet.
2. Search for **B1 Church Check-In**.
3. Tap **Install** (or **Get**) and wait for the download to finish.
4. Open the app. You will see a splash screen while it initializes and checks for updates.

## First-Time Setup

When you launch the app for the first time, you will go through these steps:

1. **[Log in](./logging-in.md)** -- Enter your email and password (and a two-factor code if the account uses one).
2. **[Select your church](./logging-in.md#selecting-your-church)** -- Choose which church this kiosk should serve.
3. **[Set up a printer](./printer-setup.md)** -- Open the hidden Admin Settings, choose a network printer, and run a test print. Skip this if you do not need labels.
4. **[Set a kiosk PIN](./admin-settings.md#protecting-the-kiosk-with-a-pin)** -- Lock the Admin Settings so families cannot change the kiosk's configuration.
5. **Choose the station type** -- Leave the kiosk in self-service mode, or turn on [Manned station mode](./admin-settings.md#self-service-and-manned-stations) for a volunteer-run desk with check-out tools.

After the first login, the app saves your sign-in and church selection. On future launches, it will skip straight to the services screen automatically.

:::info
The app checks for over-the-air updates each time it starts. If an update is available, it downloads and applies automatically before you reach the login or services screen.
:::

:::tip
Make sure your church administrator has configured services, service times, and groups in B1 Admin before setting up the kiosk. See the [attendance setup guide](../../b1-admin/attendance/setup.md) for details.
:::
