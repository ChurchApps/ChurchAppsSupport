# B1 Church Checkin Overview

<div class="article-intro">

B1 Church Checkin is a self-service kiosk app for iPads and Android tablets. It lets church members look up their family, assign children to groups or classes, and print name labels and parent pickup slips -- all without needing a volunteer at the desk. Switch a tablet to manned station mode and it also handles check-out, pickup verification, paging, and label reprints.

## How It Works

Place an iPad or Android tablet in your lobby or welcome area. Members walk up, search for their household by phone number or last name, review their family, choose which groups each person should attend, and tap **Check In**. The app prints name labels and pickup slips automatically, then resets for the next family.

## Key Features

- **Self-service member lookup** -- Search by phone number or last name to find your household quickly; results appear as you type.
- **Phone check-in scanning** -- Families who checked in on their phone hold up their QR code and the kiosk prints their labels. A station can be set to start on the scanner.
- **Check-in announcement** -- Show families a message from the church before they choose who to check in.
- **Household review** -- See all family members with photos, allergy notes, and current group assignments at a glance.
- **Group assignment** -- Assign each family member to the right class or group for the selected service time, with age and grade guidance and closed-room warnings.
- **Guest check-in** -- Add a guest to your household on the spot with a first and last name and an optional email.
- **QR guest registration** -- Display a QR code that visitors can scan to [register themselves](../b1-church/checkin/guest-registration.md) on their own phone before checking in, or let them tap **Register here** to fill in the same form on the kiosk.
- **Label printing** -- Print name labels for children and parent pickup slips with unique security codes, using the default layout or labels designed in B1 Admin.
- **Room safety limits** -- Capacity, volunteer ratios, and check-in windows set in B1 Admin are enforced at check-in time.
- **Attendance milestones** -- Confetti celebrates a person's 5th, 10th, 15th... week in a row.
- **Check-out and child safety** -- Manned stations verify the security code or find a family by name, show trusted and not-authorized pickup people, page parents by text, reprint labels, and send an emergency broadcast.
- **Network printer support** -- Discover Brother QL and Zebra label printers on your Wi-Fi directly from the app, or enter one by IP address, then run a test print.
- **Kiosk PIN** -- Protect the hidden Admin Settings with a 4-6 digit PIN.
- **Auto-login** -- After the first login, the app remembers your sign-in and goes straight to the services screen on future launches.
- **Multi-church support** -- If your account is linked to more than one church, you can choose which one to check into.
- **Church branding and idle screen** -- The app shows your church logo and app colors, an optional background image, and a slideshow screensaver when the kiosk is idle.
- **Twelve languages** -- The kiosk follows the tablet's language setting.

:::tip
B1 Church Checkin works best on a 10-inch tablet (iPad or Android) mounted upright where families can easily reach it; the app runs in portrait orientation. Get the app from the [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/b1-church-check-in/id6775081998), [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=church.b1.checkin), or [Amazon App Store](https://www.amazon.com/Live-Church-Solutions-B1-Check-In/dp/B0FW5HKRB5/).
:::

Before using B1 Church Checkin, your church administrator needs to configure services, service times, and groups in B1 Admin. The account that signs in to the kiosk needs the **Checkin** attendance permission; see [Check-In Kiosk Accounts](../b1-admin/settings/roles-permissions.md#check-in-kiosk-accounts).
