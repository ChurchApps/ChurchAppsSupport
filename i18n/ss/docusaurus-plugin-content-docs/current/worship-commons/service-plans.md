# Emalungiselelo Esevisi kuWorshipCommons

<div class="article-intro">

Luhlelo lwesevisi kuWorshipCommons luhlu lwetihubo lweSonto. Beka tihubo ngalokulandzelana, khetsa likhiye, ikhapho, nemacembu kulowo nalowo, bese wabelana ngelinki linye nelikhwaya lakho. Kuluhlelo ungavula kubukwa kwesiteji lesikhalweni somculi, ushicilele incwadzi, noma uphume sonkhe sethu ku-softhiwe yakho yekwetula.

</div>

:::info
Emalungiselelo esevisi agcinwa kusiphequluli sakho sewebhu, hhayi ku-akhawunti yakho, futsi awudzingi kungena ngemvume ukuze uwasebentise. Linki lekwabelana lichaza sonkhe sethu, ngako gcina linki uma ushintsha tidivayisi. Uma libandla lakho lihlela tinkonzo ku-B1 Admin, bheka [Emalungiselelo](../b1-admin/serving/plans.md) esikhundleni salo.
:::

## Kwakha Luhlelo Lwesevisi

1. Chofoza **Emalungiselelo esevisi** kumenyu ephezulu.
2. Bhala ligama, njengo "Lisontfo 14 Sheyini" noma "Busuku bebantfwana", bese uchofoza **Dala luhlelo lwesevisi**.

Ungaphinde uqale luhlelo kunoma yiliphi ihubo. Ekhasini lehubo, chofoza **+ Engeza ku-luhlelo lwesevisi**, bhale ligama ngaphansi kaloluhlu, bese uchofoza **Dala**.

## Kwengeta Tihubo

1. Vula ikhasi lehubo. Khetsa likhiye lofuna kuqala, ngoba ihubo lingena kuluhlelo ngalelikhiye lelikhombiswa esikrinini.
2. Chofoza **+ Engeza ku-luhlelo lwesevisi**.
3. Chofoza ligama laluhlelo. Umsikiti wekuhlola ukhombisa kutsi ihubo lisekuhlelweni walo. Chofoza futsi kuwo kuze ulikhiphe.

Inkinobho itawuveta ngaleso sikhatsi **✓ Kuyo** lilandzelwe ligama laluhlelo. Chofoza sixhumanisi **Vula** lelinegama laluhlelo kulowo wefasitere lowavela, kuze uye kuluhlelo.

## Kuhlela Uhla

Vula luhlelo kusuka ekhasini **Emalungiselelo esevisi**. Kulihubo ngalinye ungenta:

- **Kuhlela kabusha** -- Sebentisa tinkinobho **↑** ne**↓**, noma hudula ihubo ube endzaweni lenyenti. Chofoza **Susa** kuze ulikhiphe.
- **Likhiye** -- Khetsa kulelikhiye lelishicilelwe **Lishicilelwe** lehubo, noma kunalelinye likhiye lofuna (lolutfolakala ngaphansi kwe-**Kubuka kuqala (noma yikuphi likhiye)**).
- **Ikhapho** -- Khetsa umtsamo wekhapho. Luhlelo lukhombisa tindlela tekudlala tikhodi.
- **Tikhatsi** -- Setsa kulandzelana kwemavesi nekhorasi. Sebentisa tikhalo kuze uhambise sikhatsi, **×** kuze ukhiphe, futsi **+ Engeza sikhatsi** kuze wengete esinye (njengekhorasi lelibuyelwa). Chofoza **Buyela kulokuzenkelako** kuze ubuyele kulandzelano lwejwayelekile lwehubo.
- **Kuhlelwa** -- Bhala umbhalo lomfutsi wemculi, njengo "Khorasi ×2 ekugcineni, livesi lekugcina ngaphandle kwemculo".

Phezulu kuluhlelo kukhombisa inombolo yetihubo nobude lobucishe besethi. Tinguquko tigcinwa ngekuzenkelako.

## Kwabelana Nelikhwaya Lakho

1. Chofoza **Linki lekwabelana**. Linki liyakopishwa kubhodi lakho lekukopisha.
2. Ilumele liqembu lakho ngemyalezo wetekisi noma nge-imeyili.

Noma ngubani lovula linki uyabona luhlelo lonemakhiye wonkhe, ticongo, emacembu, nemanothi, ngaphandle kwekudzingeka i-akhawunti. Bangachofoza **Gcina likhopi** kuze bagcine likhopi labo lelibhalwako kusiphequluli sabo.

Ekhasini **Emalungiselelo esevisi** ungaphinde **Yabelana**, **Tfuta kabusha**, **Phindaphinda**, noma **Susa** luhlelo.

## Kusebentisa Luhlelo ngeSonto

- **Imodi yesiteji** -- Ikhombisa ichathi linye ngesikrini ngasinye, lenhle ngetabhuletha lesikhalweni somculi. Sebentisa tikhalo (noma tinkinobho **←** ne**→**) kuze uhambe phakathi kwetihubo, netinkinobho tebukhulu bembhalo kuze ichathi libe likhulu. Imodi yesiteji itama kugcina sikrini singalali; ibha ikhombisa uma **Sikrini sigcina sivukile**.
- **Shicilela incwadzi** -- Shicilela wonkhe macharts ngalokulandzelana, ichathi linye ngekhasi ngalinye, nekhava lelitfula tihubo netikhiye. Khetsa **Umbhalo lomkhulu** noma **Kukhombisa tikhodi**, bese uchofoza **Shicilela**.
- **Kopisha ye-OnSong / Planning Center** -- Kopisha wonkhe ma-chart ngetikhiye netikhatsi lotikhetsile, ulungele kunamathisela.

## Kuphuma Ngesethi

Chofoza **FreeShow**, **OpenLP**, **ProPresenter**, noma **PowerPoint** kuze ulande sonkhe setihubo kulelithuluzi, ngetikhiye netikhatsi lotikhetsile.

Chofoza **Ludzaba lwelibandla** kuze ulande i-ZIP lenechathi lehubo ngalinye ngelikhiye lakho, emagama, emasilayidi, kuncoma, luhlu lwetihubo, nefayela linye le-LICENSE.txt.

:::warning
Tihubo letilayisensi ye-CC BY-SA ayifakwa kuludzaba lelibandla ngaphandle kwekutsi ukhonkhe **Ludzaba lwekwabelana ngetihubo (ifaka tihubo te-CC BY-SA)**. Uma utifaka, konkhe lokwentiwe kusuka kuloludzaba kufanele kwabelwane ngalelayisensi lefanananako. Ma-chart, imodi yesiteji, nencwadzi tihlala tifaka letihubo.
:::
