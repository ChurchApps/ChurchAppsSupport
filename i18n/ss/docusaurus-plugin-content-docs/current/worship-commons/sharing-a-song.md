# Ukwabelana ngengoma ku-WorshipCommons

<div class="article-intro">

Uma ubhale ingoma futhi ufuna amabandla ayihlabelele ngokukhululeka, ungayabelana nayo ku-WorshipCommons. Ungabelana futhi ngokuhumusha noma ngokuhlelwa kwengoma esesilalini selaibhulali. Yonke ingoma ifundwa umuntu ngaphambi kokuba ivele ukhanye, ngokuvamile phakathi kwezinsuku ezimbalwa.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Ngaphambi Kokuqala</h4>

- Ngena ku-WorshipCommons. Bheka [I-akhawunti Yakho nIkhasi Lombhali](./your-account.md).
- Kumele ube neminyaka engu-18 noma ngaphezulu, futhi kumele ube ubhale ingoma noma ulawule ilungelo layo lokushicilela. Wonke ababhali abahlanganyelayo kumele bavume ngokubhaliwe.
- Lungiselela amazwi, nendlela eyodwa okungenani ibandla elingafunda ngayo umculo: irekhodi ledemo (irekhodi ngefoni liyamukeleka), amanothi omculo, ifayela le-MIDI, noma isixhumanisi sevidiyo.

</div>

## Ukuqala Isicelo

1. Chofoza **For Songwriters** kumenyu ephezulu.
2. Landela izinyathelo ezine ekhasini: **The song**, **Files**, **License**, kanye **Your word**. Chofoza igama lesinyathelo phezulu ukuze uye kulo.

Umsebenzi wakho ulondolozwa njengedrafti ngokuzenzakalelayo uma ingoma sekine isihloko -- qaphela **Draft saved** phezulu kwefomu. Ungasuka bese ubuyela emuva kusuka ku-**My submissions** ngokuchofoza **Continue**.

:::info
Ababhali abafuna ukukhipha ingoma ngaphandle kokuyilayisha bangakopisha isaziso sokukhishwa kusuka ekhasini **Call for songs** (elixhunyiwe ngaphansi kwawo wonke amakhasi).
:::

## Isinyathelo 1: Ingoma

1. Ngaphansi kwe-**What are you adding?**, khetha **New song**, **Translation of an existing song**, noma **Arrangement of an existing song**.
2. Faka **Title**.
3. Ku-**Writer(s)**, bhala wonke ababhali abahlanganyelayo ngendlela efanele ukuthi bavele kuma-chord chart.
4. Engeza **Year written**, **Original key**, kanye **Tempo (BPM)**. Ukhiye ufundwa kusuka ku-chord yakho yokuqala kuze wena ukhethe owakho.
5. Chofoza **Themes** ezifanelekile. Chofoza **More themes** ukuze uthole uhlu olugcwele.
6. Khetha **Language** yengoma futhi, uma isuselwe esigabeni esithile, engeza **Scripture reference** (njengesibonelo, Isaya 40:4). Amabandla abona isigaba ekhasini lengoma.
7. Uma amabandla esevele ebike ingoma ku-CCLI, engeza **CCLI number (optional)** ukuze kungashintshi lutho kubo.
8. Namathela amazwi ku-**Lyrics and chords**, isigaba ngasinye ngesikhathi, ngasinye siqale ngegama laso (Verse 1, Chorus, njll.). Ama-chord akhethiwe -- wawabeka phakathi kwamabhulaketi asikwele phambi kwesigaxa, njengo `[G]Holy, holy`. Ungachofoza futhi **Load from a ChordPro or text file**.

I-**Chart preview** iveza ngokucacile ukuthi amabandla azombona kanjani ingoma yakho, nezinkinga zilandwe ngaphansi kwayo.

:::warning
Uma ingoma yakho ifana nenye esesilalini, uzobona isexwayiso nesixhumanisi kuyo. Uma kuyingoma efanayo, yivule uphakamise ukuhlelwa kwayo esikhundleni sokulayisha ikhophi.
:::

### Ukwabelana ngokuhumusha noma ngokuhlelwa

Uma ukhetha **Translation of an existing song** noma **Arrangement of an existing song**:

1. Ngaphansi kwe-**The original song**, sesha ngesihloko esilalini bese ukhetha ingoma yokuqala kuhlu.
2. Ngokuhumusha, gcwalisa **Translator** bese ukhetha **Language** entsha yengoma (kumele ihluke kweyokuqala). Ngokuhlelwa, gcwalisa **Arranger**.
3. Qedela elinye ikhala lesinyathelo njengokujwayelekile. Ukuhlelwa kuqala ngesihloko, ababhali, nokhiye bengoma yokuqala.

I-WorshipCommons ixhumanisa amakhasi amabili engoma ndawonye, ngaleyo ndlela yombili. Amafayela awukhetho, ngoba ingoma yokuqala isivele inomculo.

:::info
Amalayisense athile awavumeli ukuhunyushwa noma ukuhlelwa. Lezi ngoma zivela zingatholakali kuhlu, zimakwe njengezidinga imvume yombhali. Uma wena ngokwakho ushicilele ingoma yokuqala, usengakwazi ukungeza ukuhunyushwa kwayo.
:::

## Isinyathelo 2: Amafayela

Yisa ifayela ebhokisini, noma uchofoze ukuze ukhethe. Ibhokisi ngalinye libala izinhlobo zamafayela elamukelayo nobukhulu bawo obuphezulu.

- **Demo recording** -- MP3, WAV, noma M4A
- **Accompaniment track** -- Umculo ngaphandle kwezwi eliholayo
- **Sheet music** -- Lead sheet noma vocal score njenge-PDF noma MusicXML
- **MIDI melody** -- Amabandla angayidlala ebhrawuzeni njengokubukwa kwepiyano
- **Multitracks** -- I-ZIP yezingxenye, ifayela elilodwa ngalinye lengxenye, ngalinye liqala ku-bar 1
- **Cover art** -- JPG, PNG, noma WebP
- **Video link** -- Ikheli le-YouTube (noma elinye) lapho amabandla angalalela khona ingoma

Uma ulayisha irekhodi, hlola **This recording is mine (or I have the owner's permission to share it).**

## Isinyathelo 3: Ilayisense

Okokuqala khetha okunikezayo: **The composition** kuphela, noma **The composition and a master recording**.

Bese ukhetha **Composition license**. Zonke izinketho zenza ingoma ibe mahhala ukuhlabelelwa kuze kuphele. Ziyahluka ngokuthi yini ogcina yona:

- **Free for worship** (kuyanconywa) -- Amabandla angahlabelela, aveze, ashicilele, asakaze, ahumushe, ahlele futhi ahlanganise. Wena ugcina ukuthengiswa kwerekhodi nokwamanothi omculo, ukusetshenziswa emidwebeni yevidiyo, amakhonsathi, umsakazo, kanye nobunikazi bengoma.
- **CC BY 4.0** -- Noma ubani angayisebenzisa ngokuthi yini, kubandakanya ukusetshenziswa kwezohwebo, uma nje ebonga.
- **Public domain** -- Wonke umuntu angayisebenzisa ngakho konke. Awugcini lutho (isinikezo esingapheli se-CC0).

Chofoza **Read the license.** ngaphansi kwenketho ukuze uyifunde ngokugcwele.

Uma futhi unikeza irekhodi le-master, kuvela isinyathelo se-**Master recording**. Layisha umxube ophelele bese ukhetha ilayisense yawo -- ingahluka kweyengoma.

## Isinyathelo 4: Izwi Lakho

Funda isifinyezo, bese uchofoza zonke izinkamba ukuqinisekisa ukuthi uneminyaka engu-18 noma ngaphezulu; ubhale ingoma (noma ulawule ilungelo layo lokushicilela); bonke ababhali abahlanganyelayo bavumile; akukho sishicileli noma inhlangano yamalungelo esiyikhiphele ilungelo lakho lokwenza lesi sinikezo; uyaqonda ukuthi ukusetshenziswa kwamabandla kungapheli; nokuthi amazwi nomculo kwabhalwa ngabantu, kungakhiwanga yi-AI.

:::warning
Amabandla angagcina yonke ikhophi noma kamuva ucela ukuthi ingoma isuswe. Yabelana kuphela ngengoma oyilungele ukuyinikeza ibandla ngokuphelele.
:::

## Ukuthumela Ukuze Kubhekwe

1. Chofoza **Add it to the commons**. Uma kukhona okushiyo, uhlu luvela ngaphezulu kwenkinobho -- chofoza into ukuze uyifinyelele.
2. Linda **Thank you — it's in review**.

Landela ingoma ku-**My submissions**. Uma umbhoneli ecela izinguquko, isimo sayo siguquka sibe **Changes requested** nesaziso sakhe sivele phezulu kwefomu uma uchofoza **Continue**. Uma isivunyiwe, ibonakala njenge-**Live**.
