# Likhasi Lengoma ye-WorshipCommons

<div class="article-intro">

Yonkhe ingoma ku-WorshipCommons inelikhasi layo lemagama nama-chords, indlela yekulalela umculo, emathulusi ekujwayelana, ama-chart lalungele kushicilelwa, nemafayela lakulandwa. Konkhe lokuselikhasi kuyasebenta ngaphandle kokungena ngemvume, ngaphandle kokugcina ingoma ku-akhawunti yakho.

</div>

## Lokukhona Phezulu

Phezulu kwelikhasi kukhombisa ikhava yengoma, litayitela, umugca wekucala, umbhali nemnyaka, nelibito letune lelihubo uma likhona. Ngaphansi kwalokho uzakubona:

- **Sibonakaliso selilungelo** -- Njengo **Public domain**, **WC** (Mahhala ekukhonteni), noma sibonakaliso se-Creative Commons njenge **CC BY**.
- **Tindzikimba nemeter** -- Chofoza ithegi yendzikimba noma ye-meter ukuze utfole amanye emahubo ayifanako.
- **Key, BPM, time signature, nesikhatsi lesilinganiselwe sekuhlabelela**, kanye ne **Recommended key** uma ingoma inayo.

Ngakwesokunene kukhona emisebenti lemikhulu:

- **Lead worship** -- Ivula umbono wemagama nemculo esikrinini lesigcwele. Bona [Kwethula Nokukhipha Emahubo](./presenting-and-exporting.md).
- **+ Add to service plan** -- Yengeta ingoma, ngesikhiye lesikhombisa esikrinini, kuleyo yetinhlelo [temhlangano](./service-plans.md) yakho.
- **Heart (Save song)** -- Igcina ingoma ku-akhawunti yakho. Uzakucelwa ukuba ungene kuqala.
- **Songwriter's page** -- Iyavela uma umbhali engezile tixhumanisi lapho emabandla angasekela khona umsebenti wakhe.

Chofoza ligama lombhali ukuze uvule [likhasi lombhali](./your-account.md#writer-pages), lelibala tonkhe tingoma takhe kule laybrari.

## Kulalela Ingoma

Uma ingoma inemsindvo, umshayeli uyavela ngaphansi kwelitayitela. Chofoza inkinobho yekudlala ukuze uyilalele.

- Uma umbhali alabelane ngelirekhodi, uzakuva **Demo recording** noma **Master recording**.
- Nangaphandle kwalokho, uzakuva **Piano preview** -- piano lidlala umugca wemelodi. I-piano preview ilandela sikhiye losikhetsa ekhasini, kotsi uyilalela ngalesikhiye lowatohlabelela ngaso.

## Chords & Emagama

Ithebhu ye-**Chords & lyrics** ikhombisa ichati. Sebentisa ithulusi lelisemkhatsini walo ukuze ulilungiselele iqembu lakho:

1. **Key** -- Khetsa sikhiye lesisha, wonkhe ma-chord ashintje. Sikhiye lesiyinhloko simakwe ngo **(original)**.
2. **Capo** -- Khetsa umtfambo we-capo. Ichati ikhombisa tindlela tema-chord lekufanele uwadlale (njengo **2 — C shapes**), kanti ingoma isalingana nesikhiye losikhetsile.
3. **Transpose** -- Chofoza **−** noma **+** ukuze wehlise noma ukhuphule sikhiye ngesigamu sesitepisi ngasikhatsi.
4. **Display** -- Vala **Chords** ukuze ubone emagama kuphela, noma vula **Nashville** ukuze ukhombise ma-chord ngetinombolo.
5. **Text size** ne **Columns** -- Yenta umbhalo ube mkhulu, noma ukhombise ichati ngemakholomu lamabili.

Hlwitsa noma thinta noma yimuphi umchord ukuze ubone indlela yekuwadlala ngegitala nangepiyano. Emahubo lamade, sebentisa tinkinobho ze-**Jump to** ukuze uye ngqo kuvesi noma kukhorasi.

:::info
Emahubo lanemagama kuphela awabonisi sikhiye, capo, noma tilawulo te-chord, ngoba kute ma-chord lekufanele ashintjwe.
:::

Ngaphansi kwechati utfola:

- **Print / PDF** -- Ivula ichati lelilungele kushicilelwa ngesikhiye, capo, nemasethingi we-chord losikhetsile. Kulelikhasi, khetsa **Text size** (kuhlanganisa **Large print**), khetsa **2 columns** noma **Show chords**, bese uchofoza **Print**. Sebentisa ingxoxo yekushicilela yesiphequluli sakho ukuze uyigcine njenge-PDF.
- **ChordPro** ne **Lyrics (TXT)** -- Landa ingoma njengefayela le-ChordPro noma lombhalo lolula.
- **Copy lyrics** -- Ikhopisha emagama (ngaphandle kwema-chord) ku-clipboard yakho.
- **Chart PDF** -- Iyavela uma ingoma inePDF yechati lelilungile.

Umugca losezantsi utsi kumele yini ingoma ibikwe ku-CCLI. Emahubo lamaningi ku-WorshipCommons awadzingi lokwenta lokunjalo.

## Umculo Lobhalwe

Emahubo lanembhalo wemculo akhombisa ithebhu ye-**Sheet music**. Iyakhombisa melodi ngesikhiye losikhetsile. Chofoza **Full score with all parts →** ukuze uvule i-score ephelele, lapho ungakhetsa **Key** ne **Part** bese uchofoza **Print**. Uma umbhali alayisha i-PDF yemculo lobhalwe, iyavela naphi, kanye namakhonkco **Open full size →** ne **Download PDF**.

## Mayelana Nemalungelo

Ithebhu ye-**About & rights** iphendula umbuzo wekutsi "lesi sihlabelelo sivela kuphi, futsi singasisebentisa yini?" Ikhombisa:

- Umbhali, inkomba yemtfombo webhayibheli lengoma, tindzikimba, nemeter yayo
- **Sing it to another tune** -- Emanye emahubo etune lelifanana ne-meter
- **Contributors & changes** -- Labo abasebentise kulengoma, nemashintsho lavunyelwe
- **Rights** -- Loku lelilungelo lakunika, loku lingakukhuthalisi, nalokufanele ukukwenta, kanye ne **Copy attribution** ukukhopisha umugca wekukhredita wencwadzi yemimemo noma wesibonakaliso

## Emathulusi Wekujwayelana

Iphaneli ye-**Practice** isita iqembu lakho likhuthalele ekuphraktiseni:

- **Tempo** -- Nciphisa noma ushesha umculo (50%–150%) usebentisa sisindvo noma tinkinobho ze **−** ne **+**.
- **Practice parts** -- Uma umculo unetindzima letinyenti (njenge soprano, alto, tenor, ne bass), chofoza indzima kuze uyilalele yodvwa, noma **All** kuze uzilalele tonkhe kanye.
- **Metronome** -- Chofoza **▶ Metronome** ukuze uvele ukukhadzeka ngesikhatsi lesikhona.
- **Play** inothi -- Idlala inothi lekucala lesikhiye lelisekhasini, ukuze umsindvo welibandla ulingane.

## Ukulanda Nekuboniswa

Iphaneli ye-**Downloads** ihlala ikhipha **Chord chart (print)** ngesikhiye lesikhona ne-capo lakho. Ngekuncike kulokwabelanwe ngumbhali, ungabona nje **Composition pack**, **Audio pack**, noma **Multitracks (ZIP)**.

Iphaneli ye-**Projection** ivula i-lyrics projector ikhiphe emaslayidi. Bona [Kwethula Nokukhipha Emahubo](./presenting-and-exporting.md).

## Umbhalo, Emahubo Ahlobene, Nokuhumusha

Ngaphansi kwelikhasi:

- **Scripture connection** -- Ikhombisa inkomba yemtfombo webhayibheli lengoma nemagama emtfombo lacuketwe ku-World English Bible. Uma ingoma ingakabi nenkomba, chofoza **Propose one →** ukuze uncome lenye.
- **Related songs** -- Tinguquko letinye temahubo ngalolulwimi, nemahubo lanemagama lafanako.
- **Translations** -- Ingoma ngetinye tilimi. Chofoza **Add one →** ukuze wabelane ngekuhumusha (bona [Kwabelana Ngengoma](./sharing-a-song.md)). Uma lilungelo lengoma lingavumeli kuhumusha, uzakubona **Translations need the writer's permission.**
