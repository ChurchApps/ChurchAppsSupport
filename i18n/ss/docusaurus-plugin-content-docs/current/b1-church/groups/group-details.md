# Imininingwane Yeliqembu

<div class="article-intro">

Nawucindzetela eliqembu kulikhasi lemaqembu, uyavulelwa likhasi lemininingwane yeliqembu. Loku ubona kuncike ekutsi usunguniwe yini ngemvume, tilungu letingeniwe ngemvume tibona tikhatsi letengetelelako netindzawo letingetelelako.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Ungakaqali</h4>

- Thola liqembu lokufuna kulibuka ngekuhamba [emaqembwini](./browsing-groups.md).
- Kuze ube nelitfuba lekufinyelela kuzo zonkhe tikhatsi, [ngena](../getting-started/logging-in.md) ku-akhawunti yakho.

</div>

## Umbono Wesivakashi (Lapho Ungakangeni)

Nawungakangeni, uyakubona lokulandzelako:

- **Sihloko** -- Libito leliqembu nesikhatsi nendzawo lelihlangana kuyo. Ku-khompyutha, sitfombe seliqembu sibonakala ngenhla kwalo.
- **Ngena ukuze ujoyine** -- Inkinobho ingaphansi kwelibito leliqembu. Ngena (noma wakhe i-akhawunti) ukuze ujoyine liqembu noma ucele kujoyina. Ngemva kwekungena, uyabuyiselwa eliqembini futsi uyatsatfwa ungene ngqo esigabeni sekujoyina.
- **Mayelana** -- Incazelo yonkhe yeliqembu lechaza injongo yalo nalokulindzeleke.
- **Bahholi** -- Tibito tebahholi beliqembu. Ungacindzetela libito lemuntfu ukuze ubone liprofayili lakhe.
- **Emicimbi Lelandzelako** -- Kuya kutsi kutfole imicimbi lelitsatfu lelandzelako yeliqembu, ngayinye ibonisa lusuku, sikhatsi, nelitayitela lemcimbi, nelinki lethi **Bhalisa** nakuba umcimbi unemabhaliselo. Uma liqembu lingenayo imicimbi lelandzelako, lesigaba siyafihlakala.

## Umbono Wetilungu (Lapho Usungeniwe)

Nawusungeniwe kodvwa awulilungu leliqembu, uyakubona sihloko seliqembu nesigaba sekuMayelana, kanye nenkhetho yekujoyina liqembu (bona [Kujoyina Liqembu](#joining-a-group) ngentasi). Tigaba letichazwe lapha letilandzelako tiyavela nawuba lilungu.

Nawuba lilungu, sihloko seliqembu sibonisa libito, lilanga lemhlangano, sikhatsi, nendzawo, kanye ne-nkinobho ethi **Vula i-chat** nangabe libandla lakho livule i-chat yemaqembu. Nawuhola liqembu, ligama lethi **Uyahola · Umholi** liyavela ngaphansi kwelibito. Ku-khompyutha, sihloko sibekwe ngakwesihlalo sangesewunxele nesitfombe seliqembu, futsi tigaba tiyavela ngakwesewunyakatfo kwasho.

Likhasi lakhiwe ngetigaba letibekwe ngesikhatsi-sinye. Ngayinye ibonisa kubukwa lokufishane, futsi linki lelisesihlokweni selisigaba livula kubukwa lokugcwele; cindzetela **Buyela ku-** *libito leliqembu* ukuze ubuyele. Tigaba letitfolakalako ngalena:

- **Umcimbi lolandzelako** -- Umcimbi lolandzelako weliqembu, ninebhatoni ethi **Ngiyeya** / **Mhlawumbe** / **Angeke ngiye** (bona [Ukuphendvula Kwemicimbi](./event-rsvps.md)). Umcimbi lotfarhela ubhalisa unelinki lethi **Bhalisa**; nasewubhalisile, simo sakho -- **Ubhalisiwe**, **Kulindzeleke**, noma **Ulindzele** -- siyavela endzaweni yaso (kulikhadi lelitsi nakulucwaningo lwemicimbi lwekugcwele), nawucindzetela sitawuvula [tibhaliselo takho](../events/my-registrations.md). Cindzetela **Yonkhe imicimbi** ukuze uvule ikhalenda yeliqembu: sebentisa tibhamuza kuhamba etinyangeni, futsi shintja phakatsi kwe-**Ikhalenda** (gilidi lenyanga lapho ucindzetela lilanga ukuze ubone imicimbi yalo) ne-**Lutfo** (imicimbi yenyanga ngekuhleleka kwetinsuku, iqenjwe ngalilanga). Nawubuka inyanga yamanje, lutfo luyaqala ngalelanga, ngako lubonisa lokusalela kuphela. Nawuba nemicimbi yeliqembu netigcamelo, ungasihlunga ngatigcamelo.
- **Lesifundo salelisonto** -- Nangabe liqembu lakho linelinkhulumo lehlelo le-Lessons.church futsi lesifundo salelisonto sinemvelo yekubhekela emtsandvweni wemtsandvo wekukhaya wemzali, siyavela ngaphansi kwe-**Umcimbi lolandzelako** (kulabo basingamalungu kuphela).
- **Imihlangano** -- Imihlangano yeliqembu lelisakhona, lelisha kuqala. Ngayinye ibonisa lusuku, kutsi bangaki labekhona (njengekutsi "8 kuli-12 labekhona") noma **Lelandzelako** nangabe lusuku lusembikwaneni, kanye nemaphepha emholi wemhlangano lowo, lagcine kutfolakala kwemabold, tinhlu, netinkhomba. Cindzetela **Yonkhe imihlangano** ukuze ubone yonkhe. Umhlangano uvela kuphela nangabe unemaphepha noma kuba khona kwebantfu. Bona [Emaphepha Emhlangano Nekuba Khona](#meeting-notes-and-attendance) ngentasi.
- **Emaphepha emholi** -- Abonakala kubaholi beliqembu kuphela. Ibonisa emaphepha emholi lamabili lasha kakhulu; cindzetela **Vula emaphepha** ukuze ufunde wonkhe futsi ufake lelitsha. Bona [Emaphepha Emholi](#leader-notes) ngentasi.
- **Tifomu** -- Tifomu lelibandla lakho lelihlanganise neliqembu, kuze kube tintsatfu ngesikhatsi-sinye. Tilungu tibona tifomu letivulekile, ngayinye inenkinobho ethi **Gcwalisa**. Bahholi bona nato tifomu letivalekile (letimakwe ethi **Ivaliwe**), kanye nekutsi kunemphendvulo lingaki ngayinye, kanye netifomu tebhalisela temicimbi yeliqembu (letimakwe ethi **Bhalisela umcimbi**). Cindzetela **Yonkhe tifomu** ukuze ubone tonkhe. Ivela kuphela nangabe liqembu linetifomu letihlanganisiwe. Bona [Tifomu Letihlanganisiwe](#linked-forms) ngentasi.
- **Imilayeto** -- Imilayeto yemiyalezo yekuchamba kweqembu lelisha kakhulu. Cindzetela **Vula i-chat** ukuze ufunde futsi ufake umlayeto. Ibonakala kuphela kotilungu, futsi kuphela nangabe libandla lakho livule i-chat yemaqembu kuleliqembu. Bona [Tinkhulumisano Temaqembu](./conversations.md) ngetinkhulumo letinengetelelako.
- **Tilungu** -- Kubukwa kwetilungu temaqembu (bahholi kutiqala) nalobalwa tilungu netibahholi. Cindzetela **Yonkhe tilungu** ukuze ubone lubhalo lonkhe, lolungafuneka ufuna ngaku **Funa Tilungu**. Cindzetela libito lelilungu ukuze uvakashele liprofayili lakhe ku[Lusuku Lwetilungu](../community/member-directory.md).
- **Tincenye** -- Emafayela lamancane lalabelwanako netinkhomba letilabelwanako letincane letilandzelako kulelliqembu. Cindzetela **Yonkhe tincenye** ukuze ubone tonkhe; ungalanda emafayela lalayiswako noma uvule tinkhomba letihlanganisiwe.
- **Tikhatsi** -- Likhasi lenkhonzo lelilandzelako leliqembu. Cindzetela **Yonkhe tikhatsi** ukuze ubone tonkhe. Ivela kuphela nangabe liqembu linetikhatsi.
- **Mayelana** -- Incazelo yonkhe yeliqembu, ikhombisa ngekufomatwa kwetemtfwalo letinemakwa. Ivela kuphela nangabe liqembu linencazelo.

Tilungu futsi zibona sixhumanisi se **Shiya Liqembu** ngaphansi kwelikhasi.

## Tikhatsi Temholi

Nawuyimholi weliqembu, unetikhatsi letengetelelako tekuhlela:

- **Dala futsi uhlele imicimbi** -- Cindzetela **Yonkhe imicimbi**, bese **Engeta Umcimbi** ukuze wengete umcimbi ekhalendeni yeliqembu. Nawudala umcimbi, ungabhukha emakamelo nemitfombo kanye nawo -- khetsa emakamelo nemitfombo letfolakalako, setsa sikhatsi sekulungiselela nekuhlanta uma kufuneka, futsi sicelo sekubhukha sitawuthunyelwa ngekuzenta kuze siphasiswe.
- **Hlela imicimbi lephindvwayo** -- Nawuvula umcimbi lophindvwayo kute uyihlele, B1 iyakubuta kucala ukuthi ufuna kushintja nasiphi lusuku: **Lusuku lolu kuphela**, **Lolu nalo lonkhe tinsuku letilandzelako**, noma **Tonkhe tinsuku**. Khetsa lenye bese ucindzetela **Londoloza** ukuze uvule umcimbi. Nawukhetsa **Lusuku lolu kuphela**, inkinobho ethi **Iphindvwayo** iyafihlakala, futsi tinguquko takho (noma kususwa) tisebenta kulolusuku lulodvwa.
- **Susa imicimbi** -- Cindzetela sitfombe senhlanganisela lesecaleni komcimbi, bese ucindzetela **Susa umcimbi** futsi uciniseke. Nakuba umcimbi lophindvwayo, kususwa kulandzela tinsuku lotikhetsile ngesikhatsi kuvulwa -- lolusuku lulodvwa, lolu nalo lonkhe tinsuku letilandzelako, noma lonkhe lolutsambo. Lusuku lolususiwe alusabonakali ekhalendeni yeliqembu noma emicimbeni lelandzelako yetilungu.
- **Setsa tikhumbuzo temicimbi** -- Nawuhlela umcimbi losuvele ukhona, vula **Thumela tikhumbuzo** ukuze ukhumbuze bantfu ngaphambi kwekuba umcimbi uqale. Khetsa lapho tiyakuphuma khona, i-**Sikhatsi Sosuku**, i-**Ngubani okufanele akhunbuzwe?**, nemylayeto **Lokungakakhethi** Kutsi **Umlayeto**, bese ukhetsa **Push**, **I-imeyili**, noma, nangabe libandla lakho linesisetelo sekutfumela emasi, **Umbhalo** ngaphansi kwe-**Thumela Nga**. Emasi ayaya kutilungu kuphela tetikhumbuzo temasi ahlelwe ku[Tikhetelo Tesaziso](../getting-started/notification-preferences.md) tabo.
- **Phatsa tincenye** -- Layisha emafayela (kufika ku-100 MB yesitoreji), engeta tinkhomba, noma susa tincenye letikhona.
- **Bhala emaphepha emhlangano futsi ulandzele kuba khona** -- Sebentisa **Engeta umlayeto** ne-**Tfola kuba khona** kucadzeni le**Mihlangano**. Bona [Emaphepha Emhlangano Nekuba Khona](#meeting-notes-and-attendance).
- **Gcina emaphepha emholi labucayi** -- Sebentisa sigaba se**Emaphepha emholi**. Bona [Emaphepha Emholi](#leader-notes).
- **Funda futsi uphrinte tiphendvulo temafomu** -- Sebentisa sigaba se**Tifomu**. Bona [Tifomu Letihlanganisiwe](#linked-forms).

Ukutfutsa libito leliqembu, incazelo, noma lolubhalo lwetilungu, sebentisa B1 Admin. Bona [Tilungu Telqembu](../../b1-admin/groups/group-members.md).

### Emaphepha Emhlangano Nekuba Khona {#meeting-notes-and-attendance}

Bahholi beliqembu, nebantfu labanemvume yekutfola kuba khona, babona **Engeta umlayeto** ne-**Tfola kuba khona** ekucaleni kwesigaba se**Mihlangano**. Tilungu tibona lolucwaningo lwemihlangano nemaphepha lafanako, kutsi labo labangakakhoni kuba khona ngesonto lilinye bakwati kulandzela.

Kubhala sifinyelelo semhlangano:

1. Cindzetela **Engeta umlayeto**.
2. Khetsa **Lusuku** lemhlangano. Liqala ngemhlangano wakho lowusedvute wangaphambili lesontfweni lelidlulile, noma lusuku lwanamuhla. Ungakhetsa lusuku lolutawuza ukuze ubhale umlayeto ngaphambi kwemhlangano.
3. Bhala umlayeto ebhokisini le**Emaphepha** -- njengekutsi kwabakhulunywa, timemo, noma ticelo temkhuleko. Bhalwa-bhala uvumela wengete kumnandzi, tinhlu, netinkhomba.
4. Cindzetela **Gcina**.

Nangabe lusuku selinaphepha, liyavulwa ukuze uliguqule. Sikrini se**Tfola kuba khona** sinebhokisi lelifanako le**Emaphepha** ngaphansi kwelusuku, ngako ungabhala sifinyelelo ngesikhatsi utfola kuba khona. Bahholi futsi bangacindzetela umhlangano ohlwini kuze uvule kuba khona kwawo nemaphepha. Emaphepha emhlangano ngemaphepha lafanako lebonwa bantfu labasebenti ku**Tinkhatsi** temaqembu kuB1 Admin -- bona [Kurekhodwa Kuba Khona](../../b1-admin/attendance/recording-attendance.md#session-notes).

### Emaphepha Emholi {#leader-notes}

**Emaphepha emholi** ngenkhulumo lephrivethi yebaholi beliqembu -- njengekuvikela lokulandzelako noma tinkinga tekukhathalela. Ngubo bodvwa bahholi beliqembu labangayibona noma bayifake kuyo. Tilungu letinye atikaze tiyibone, futsi nebasebenti bebandla abayiboni nangabe futsi bayibahholi beliqembu.

1. Kulesigaba se**Emaphepha emholi**, cindzetela **Vula emaphepha** (noma cindzetela noma iphephe ilinye).
2. Bhala umlayeto wakho ebhokisini langaphansi bese uyathuma. Gcizelela umbhalo ukuze wente ubhalwe ngemnandzi noma ube ngumugca -- bona [Ukufomatha Umlayeto Wakho](./conversations.md#formatting-your-message).

Kuze kube kutsi kukhona lomuntfu lofake, likhasi libonisa "Awukaphi emaphepha" nesikhumbuzo esitsi "Bahholi balelliqembu kuphela labangabona lemaphepha." Bahholi babona emaphepha lafanako likhasi leliqembu kuB1 Admin -- bona [Tilungu Telqembu](../../b1-admin/groups/group-members.md#leader-notes).

### Tifomu Letihlanganisiwe {#linked-forms}

Bantfu bebandla bangahlanganisa ifomu neliqembu lakho ku-B1 Admin -- njengekutsi likhadi lesicelo semkhuleko noma lemvume yekuya ohambweni (bona [Kuhlanganisa Ifomu Neliqembu](../../b1-admin/forms/creating-forms.md#linking-a-form-to-a-group)). Tifomu letihlanganisiwe titfolakala kusigaba se**Tifomu** setiqembu. Tilungu tacindzetela **Gcwalisa** ukuze tivule ifomu futsi titfumele.

Bahholi futsi bangafunda futsi baphrinte tiphendvulo:

1. Kusigaba se**Tifomu**, cindzetela ifomu (noma cindzetela **Yonkhe tifomu** bese ukhetsa lenye). Tibhalisela temicimbi yeliqembu letibuta imibuto yekubhalisa nato tiyalodwa lapha.
2. Lolutfo lubonisa umuntfu ngamunye lophendvulile nelusuku lalatfumela ngalo. Tiphendvulo tebantfu labangangeniwe tibonisa njenge-**Angatiwa**.
3. Cindzetela umuntfu kuze ubone tiphendvulo takhe, nemibuto ngayinye ibonisa ngenhla kwaleyo phendvulo.
4. Cindzetela **Phrinta** ukuze uphrinte tiphendvulo talomuntfu, noma cindzetela **Phrinta konkhe** ngenhla kwelutfo ukuze uphrinte yonkhe tiphendvulo teyifomu.

Iphrinti inelogo yebandla lakho (i-**Light Background Logo** lebekwa ku-B1 Admin, nangabe ilayishwe), nelibito lalo kuphakathi ngenhla, bese kulandzela libito lefomu, kulandzelwe tiphendvulo ngato ngatome, ticalwe libito lakhe nelusuku lalatfumela ngalo. Bahholi bangafunda tiphendvulo kodvwa abakwati kuzishintja noma kuhlela ifomu. Cindzetela **Yonkhe tifomu** ukuze ubuyele ohlwini.

## Kujoyina Liqembu

Nawusungeniwe futsi awusilungu, inkinobho yekujoyina iyavela ekhasini lemininingwane yeliqembu. Libito lenkinobho lincike kumgomo wekujoyina weliqembu:

- **Joyina Liqembu** -- Liqembu livulekile. Ucindzetela lencane ukuze wengetwe ngekushesha njengelilungu.
- **Cela Kujoyina** -- Liqembu lidinga kuvunywa. Ucindzetela lencane kuvula ibhokisi lapho ungafaka umlayeto longakhethi kumholi weliqembu, bese uthumela sicelo sakho. Bona [Kucela Kujoyina Liqembu](./join-requests.md) ngemininingwane yekuphatsa ticelo takho.

Nakutsi sicelo sakho sesithunyeliwe, inkinobho itfolwa yisaziso se-**Sicelo Silindzele**: "Sicelo sakho sekujoyina sithunyelwe. Umholi weliqembu utawusibuka."

Nawungakangeni futsi ucindzeta **Ngena ukuze ujoyine**, uyabuyela eliqembini ngemva kwekungena futsi sigaba sekujoyina siyaqala ngekuzenta: liqembu livulekile likungeta ngako ngekushesha, kanti liqembu lelidinga kuvunywa livula ibhokisi le**Cela Kujoyina**.

Nangabe ningabonakali tinkinobho letinhlanu, liqembu livaliwe futsi tilungu kufanele tengetwe ngesandla ngumholi noma umphathi.

:::info
Kufanele ungene ngemvume ukuze ujoyine noma ucele kujoyina liqembu. Nangabe liqembu livaliwe futsi ufuna kujoyina, xhumana nelihhovisi lebandla lakho noma nenye yebaholi beliqembu.
:::
