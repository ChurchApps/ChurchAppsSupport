# Umlandvo Wekunikela

<div class="article-intro">

Uma usungene, ungabona umlandvo ogcwele wetinikelo takho letindlulile futsi wentele emitifiketi yekunikela ngetinjongo tetintela. Umlandvo wetinikelo takho utfolakala ephepheni le-**Kunikela** lesikhatsini semalungu.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Ngaphambi Kokuqala</h4>

- Kufanele ube [usungeniwe](../getting-started/logging-in.md) ku-akhawunti yakho ye-B1.church.
- Kufanele usewentile kunikela lokungenani kunye. Bona [Kwenta Kunikela](./making-donations.md) kuze uqale.

</div>

## Kubuka Umlandvo Wakho

1. Ngena ku-akhawunti yakho ye-B1.church.
2. Vula iphepha le-**Kunikela** (ithebhu ye-**Nikela** yelibandla lakho), noma uthinte **Umlandvo** esigabeni se-**Kunikela** kuDeshibhodi yakho yeKhaya ([bona](../getting-started/navigating.md#home)).
3. Thinta **Umlandvo wonkhe** kuze ubone tinikelo takho letindlulile. (Sixhumanisi se-**Umlandvo** esiKhayeni sivula lombono ngqo.)

Phezulu, khetsa umnyaka (noma **Konkhe**) kuze ubone inani lakho lalesikhatsi, kutsi tinikelo letingaki letibandakanyekile, nekutsi tingaki letisesele tingakagcinwa. Uma tinikelo lesikhatsini lesitsite tinikelwe ngetimali letingetiwe ngaphezu kwetimbili, inani liguculiswa libe ngemali yelibandla lakho, futsi sicashunwana **Kuguculiswe ngemitengo yamanje yekushintjashintja kwemali** sitawuvela ngaphansi kwaso.

Ngaphansi kwenani, tinikelo takho tihlelwe ngetinyanga, ngalinye likhombisa:

- **Sikhwama** -- Sikhwama lebekwentiwe kuso kunikela (njengeSikhwama Sejwayelekile, Sikhwama Sekwakha).
- **Lilanga** kanye **Indlela** -- Lilanga lekunikelwe ngalo nendlela lokhokhe ngayo (njenge "Ikhadi - Visa" noma "Ibhange - ACH").
- **Inani** -- Inani lekunikela, ngemali lekunikelwe ngayo.

:::info
Tinikelo letisacubungulwa tikhombisa ibheji **Ilindile** ngaphansi kwenani.
:::

Thinta **Buyela Ku-Kunikela** kuze ubuyele ephepheni le-Kunikela.

## Uma Libandla Lakho Likhawula Umlandvo Wetinikelo

Libandla lakho likhetsa kutsi emalungu angabona kangakanani umlandvo wetinikelo -- bona [Emitifiketi Yekunikela](../../b1-admin/donations/giving-statements.md#what-members-see-in-b1church):

- **Iminyaka yonkhe** (ngokuzenzakalelako) -- Konkhe lolu phepha lukhona lutfolakala.
- **Kuya ngalomnyaka lowucacisiwe** -- Tinikelo letentiwe ngemva kwalomnyaka atisayi kuvela emlandvweni wakho, emananini, nakumitifiketi, futsi iphepha le-Kunikela likhombisa inani lalomnyaka esikhundleni salomnyaka wamanje. Emabandla avamise kwenta loku kuze agcine umnyaka wamanje ngetulo kuze aqedze kulungisa.
- **Kufihliwe** -- Inani lomnyaka, **Umsebenti wakamuva**, **Umlandvo wonkhe**, nelithulusi **Phrinta Sitatimende** kuyasuswa ephepheni le-Kunikela. Usengakwazi kunikela nekuphatha tinikelo letiphindwaphindwako kanye netindlela tekukhokha.

Uma uvula sitatimende salomnyaka lelibandla lakho lingakakhululi, uyobona umlayezo lothi "Lesitatimende sekunikela asitfolakali. Xhumana nelibandla lakho kuze utfole likhophi."

Libandla lakho lingase futsi lingeze **inothi lekunikela** -- umlayezo lomfishane njengenqubo yalo yekubuyisa tinikelo. Liyavela phezulu ephepheni le-Kunikela, ekuhlinzekeni kunikela, nase-**Umlandvo wonkhe**, futsi liphrintwa ngaphansi kwetitatimende takho tekunikela.

## Uma Libandla Lakho Lingatfoli Kunikela Ngeinternethi

Nanoma libandla lakho lingakasetfwa kunikela ngeinternethi, usengabona tinikelo letirekhodwe njengetimali netishecki. Vula iphepha le-**Kunikela** ungene ngemvume, futsi uyobona umlayezo lothi "Kunikela ngeinternethi akusetfwa kulelibandla kusenjalo. Tinikelo letirekhodwe netitatimende takho tisentasi ngaphansi." Inani lakho lomnyaka, **Umsebenti wakamuva**, **Umlandvo wonkhe**, nelithulusi **Phrinta Sitatimende** konkhe kuyasebenta njengejwayelekile. Inkinobho **Nikela**, inkinobho **Phinda Kunikela Lekugcina**, nemacembu **Lokuphindwaphindwako** kanye **Tindlela Tekukhokha** akukhonjiswa. Sigaba se-**Kunikela** eKhayeni lakho nasikhombisa inani lakho kanye nesixhumanisi se-**Umlandvo**, ngaphandle kwenkinobho **Nikela**.

## Kuphrinta Emitifiketi Nekuyidlulisa

Thinta **Phrinta Sitatimende** phezulu ephepheni le-Kunikela kuze uvule sitatimende sekunikela lesiphrintekako lesimbonisa umnyaka wekhalenda wamanje (noma umnyaka lomatfupha lelibandla lakho lewukhululile) kuthebhu lensha. Kuze uphrinte omunye umnyaka, vula **Umlandvo wonkhe**, khetsa lowo mnyaka, bese uthinta **Phrinta Sitatimende** eceleni kwenani lesiphelele.

Sitatimende sigxilwe ephepheni futsi siqala ngelogo yelibandla lakho (uma libandla lakho lelilayishile), umnyaka, sikhatsi lesimbonako (kusukela ngalolusuku lwe-1 kuJanuwari kuya ku-31 kuDisemba), kanye nelilanga lekukhishwa kwaso. Ngaphansi kwaloko kuvela ligama lakho nemininingwane yakho yekuxhumana ngaseceleni kwaligama nelikheli lelibandla lakho, sifinyezo setinikelo takho tonkhe kanye nesamba sesikhwama ngasinye, netabula yetinikelo tonkhe ngalinye nelilanga, indlela, sikhwama, nenani layo. Ekugcineni kuvela inothi lesiciniseka kutsi sitatimende sibandakanya tonkhe tinikelo letemukelwe libandla lakho kuwe ngalowo mnyaka, kulandzelwe sisho lesifishane sekutsi libandla liyinhlangano lebhalisiwe yekusita ngaphandle kwenzuzo, nekutsi akukho tintfo noma tinsita letinikelwe ngekutfola tinikelo lapho kuvela khona, ngaphandle kwetinzuzo letingabonakali tebuKhristu. Inothi lekunikela lelibandla lakho, uma linalo, liphrintwa ngemva kwaloko.

:::tip
Phrinta sitatimende sakho ekuqaleni kwalomnyaka lomusha, ngaphambi kokuba usidzinge ekufakeni tintela, ngesikhatsi tinikelo temnyaka wangaphambilini tisasha emlandvweni wakho.
:::

## Tinikelo Letiphindwaphindwako

Uma unato tinikelo letihleliwe, sigaba se-**Lokuphindwaphindwako** ephepheni le-Kunikela sikhombisa ngalinye ngalinye ngenani lalo, sikhwama, sikhatsi, kanye nendlela yekukhokha. Thinta **Phatha** kuze uvule **Tinikelo letiphindwaphindwako netindlela tekukhokha**, lapho ungatishintsha khona.

Uma tinikelo letiphindwaphindwako tikhokhwa nge-**Stripe**, ungakwazi **kuyimisa** kunekuyisusa -- chofoza sithonjana sekumisa eceleni kwenikelo bese uyaqinisa. Kunikela kuyoyeka kuze uchofoze sithonjana lesifanako kuze **uqalise kabusha**; tinikelo letimisiwe timakwa ngekutsi **Limisiwe** ohlwini. Tinikelo letiphindwaphindwako ngabahlinzeki betinkhokhelo letinye tingashintjwa noma tisuswe, kodvwa hhayi timisiwe.

Kuze wengeze,ususe, noma ushintje tindlela tekukhokha letigcinwe, thinta **Phatha** esigabeni se-**Tindlela tekukhokha**.
