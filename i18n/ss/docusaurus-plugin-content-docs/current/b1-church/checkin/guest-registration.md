# Kubhalisa Tihambeli

<div class="article-intro">

Uma uvakashela licawe kokusha, ungabhalisa wena nemndeni wakho njengetihambeli usebenzisa likhasi lekubhalisa tihambeli. Lokhu kudala irekhodi kusistimu yelicawe, kutsi bavolontiya bakhone kukukhomba nawufika.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Ngaphambi Kokuqala</h4>

- Licawe lakho kudzingeka libe nekubhalisa tihambeli ngeQR livuliwe. Uma lingakavuleki, likhasi libonisa "Kubhalisa tihambeli akuvulekile kulelicawe."
- Skena ikhodi ye-QR lebonakala ku-kiosk yekukhomba, thepha **Register here** ku-kiosk, noma utfole sixhumanisi kuvolontiya welicawe

</div>

## Kusebenta Kwekubhalisa Tihambeli

Kubhalisa tihambeli kuklelelwe tavakashi labangakabi ne-akhawunti. Ku-kiosk yekukhomba yelicawe, thepha **Register as guest** kutsi ubonise ikhodi ye-QR lelisixhumanisa nelikhasi lekubhalisa. Ungaphindze utfole sixhumanisi kuvolontiya noma kumsebenti losemnyango, noma ufumane ku-flyer noma ku-webhusayithi yelicawe.

Awunayo ifoni lesendleleni? Ku-windzi yefanako ye-QR ku-kiosk, thepha **Register here** kutsi ugcwalise lifomu ku-kiosk ngalo. Bona [Kubhalisa Njengetihambeli](../../b1-checkin/check-in/looking-up-members.md#registering-as-a-guest) kutsi lokhu kusebenta njani ku-kiosk.

Likhasi livula nalelitfo **Guest Registration**. Uma licawe lengeze sitfombe sekuphepha, sitawuvela phezulu kwalikhasi; kungenjalo uya kubona licoliti lelincane.

## Kubhalisa Umndzeni Wakho

1. **Skena ikhodi ye-QR** ku-kiosk yekukhomba noma vula sixhumanisi sekubhalisa tihambeli ku-foni yakho. (Uma uthepha **Register here** ku-kiosk, lifomu selivulekile.)
2. Gcwalisa ikhadi le-**Primary Contact**. **First Name** ne-**Last Name** kuyadzingeka njalo. Kuncike kutsi licawe lakho lifuna ini, ungabona:
   - **Title** (njengo Mr, Mrs, Dr, noma Pastor), ngaphambi kelibito
   - **Email** ne-**Phone**
   - **Marital Status**
   - **Home Address** -- **Street Address**, **Address Line 2**, **City**, **State**, ne-**Zip**. Lokhu kwabelwana ngako ngumndzeni wonkhe.
3. Lifomu liqala ngekhadi lesibili le-**Family Member 2**. Faka libito lakhe lekucala nelekugcina, noma uthepha **X** ekhoneni lekhadi kutsi ulisuse uma ubhalisa wedvwa.
4. Kutsi ungeze labanye bantfu, thepha **Add Family Member**. Ikhadi lelisha litawugcwalisa kutsi libito lelikhulu lemuntfu lolilungu lelicala libe lelifanako nalelo le-primary contact. Ungabhalisa bantfu labafika ku-10 ngesikhatsi sinye.
5. Uma licawe lakho lidzinga lokuvunyelwe, ikhadi lemuntfu ngamunye lingaphinde libe nemasimu lanjenge **Title**, **Phone**, **Birth Date**, **Gender**, ne-**Grade**. Wonkhe amakhadi ngemva kwe-primary contact angase acele ne-**Household Role** -- **Spouse**, **Child**, noma **Other** -- kutsi licawe lati kutsi umuntfu ngamunye uhlobene njani ne-primary contact.
6. Uma licawe linebhokisi le-**Comments** ngaphansi kwetilungu temndeni, yisebentisele kutsi wabelane nanoma yini lofuna licawe liyati (kuze kufike kumasinyi langu-1,000).
7. Thepha **Register** kutsi uthume.

Tinsimu letimakwe ngenkamba (`*`) ayadzingeka. Uma kukhona letingagcwaliswanga, lifomu libonisa "Please fill in every required field." futsi akukho lokuthunyelwa kuze uzigcwalise.

## Ngemva Kokubhalisa

Nawuthumela lifomu, kuvela sikrini sekuqinisekisa nemilayeto: "Registration Complete -- You're all set! A staff member will check you in shortly."

Kubhalisa kwakho kudala emarekhodi esisekelo selicawe:

- **Indlu** idalwa ngelibito lekugcina le-primary contact, nale primary contact ibe yinhloko yendlu.
- **Emarekhodi emuntfu** adalwa ngemuntfu ngamunye ngesimo sesilungu "Visitor". Lilungu ngalinye lemndeni litfola indzima yemndeni lowukhetsile, noma **Other** uma kungabuzwanga.
- Netinhlamvu letikubhalile tilondvolotwa njengesitfwebeli ekurekhodweni kwe-primary contact, kutsi bo-staff belicawe bakhone kukufundza.
- Uma licawe likhetse liqembu letihambeli letitsha, umndzeni wakho uyangeniswa kuleliqembu. Licawe lingase futsi lirekhodwe kutsi ukhona uma ubhalisa ngesikhatsi selinye lamasevisi alelo qembu.
- Umvolontiya noma umphathi welicawe angakukhomba wena nemndeni wakho ngekusebentisa i-[B1 Checkin](../../b1-checkin/check-in/looking-up-members.md) kiosk.

:::info
Uma ubhalisa futsi ngelibito lefanako nelefanako ye-imeyili noma inombolo yefoni, emarekhodi elicawe ayakwati futsi asebentise iprofayili yakho lekhona kunekutsi yengeze lesibili. Labanye bantfu bayengezwa njengabatsha. Uma sewubhalisile, ungafuna libito lakho noma inombolo yefoni yakho ku-kiosk.
:::

:::tip
Uma lifomu likucela ikheli lakho le-imeyili, kusita licawe kutsi likulandzele ngemva kwekuvakashela kwakho.
:::
