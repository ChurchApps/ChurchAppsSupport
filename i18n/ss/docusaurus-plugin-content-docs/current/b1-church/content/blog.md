# Bulogi

<div class="article-intro">

Likhasa le-Bulogi libahlisa tindzaba, tibuyekezo, kanye nemahlaya lacondzile kusuka esontweni lakho. Tinhlelo tihlelwe ngetigaba kanye netinhlamvu, kute ukwazi kuhamba ngekutsi utfola tihloko letikutsandzako kakhulu.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Ngaphambi Kokuqala</h4>

- Akudzingeki kungena ngemvume kuze ufundze tinhlelo tebulogi. Likhasa le-Bulogi litfolakala kubo bonkhe.
- Sonto lakho kufanele libe nesihlahla sinye lesishicilelwe kuze likhasa le-Bulogi livele.

</div>

## Kuhamba Phakatsi Kwetinhlelo

1. Yiya ekhasini le-**Bulogi** kusuka kusakhiwo sesayithi lesonto lakho.
2. Tinhlelo titfolakala kusuka kwetintsha kuya kwetindzala, 10 ngekhasi ngalinye. Sebentisa tixhumanisi te-**Lakudzala**/**Lakusha** ngaphansi kuze uyohamba phakatsi kwetinhlelo letidzala.
3. Chofoza kuyo inhlelo ukuze uyifundze yonkhe.

## Kuhlunga Ngetigaba Noma Tinhlamvu

Chofoza **ligama lesigaba** noma **tinhlamvu** kunoma iyiphi inhlelo, noma kuluhlu lwebulogi, kuze uhlunge uhlu lube netinhlelo letihambisana nalokuphokelelwako. Lokhu kwenta kutsi kube lula kutfola tindzaba mayelana nalesihloko noma lenkhatsi ethile.

## Tinhlelo Letihambisanako

Ekuphelweni kwenhlelo ngalinye, uyawutfola sigaba **Tinhlelo Letihambisanako** esiphakamisa tinye tinhlelo letisuka esigabeni lesifanako.

## Kubhalisela nge-RSS

Lilonkhe ibulogi yesonto licashile i-RSS feed lengahlanganiswa netisebentisi te-feed readers nemasistimu lenta tintfo ngekuzenta, kuze uteme nyalo lapho inhlelo lesha ifakwa, ngaphandle kwekubuyela ngesandla kuyobhekisisa.

## Kutfolakala

Likhasa le-Bulogi liyatfolakala uma sonto lakho lishicilele inhlelo sihlahla sinye. Uma ungakaboni sixhumanisi se-**Bulogi** ekusakhiweni kwesayithi lesonto lakho, sonto lakho lingase lingakasetfwa lokhu. Xhumana nomphatsi wesonto lakho ukuze utfole lulwati lwengetiwe.
