# Kuphatsa Tiskrini Netiqembu

<div class="article-intro">

Likhasi le-**Screens** ku-FreePlay Admin libala yonkhe i-TV letibambisene nebandla lakho, kanye nalokusebentako manje nekutsi ikulungele yini Sonto. Lapha futsi ungaphatsa **tiqembu tetiskrini**. Tiskrini letisesiqembwini lesinye tabelana ngekhalenda linye nelupu lwekuzenzekela lolunye.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Ngaphambi Kokuqala</h4>

- Ngena ku-FreePlay Admin ku-[admin.freeplay.church](https://admin.freeplay.church)
- [Bambisana okungenani tiskrini sinye](./pairing-a-screen.md)

</div>

## Likhasi Le-Screens

Ngasetulu, tinombolo letintsatfu tikunika kuhlola kwesimo ngekushesha:

- **Online manje** - Tiskrini letingenile kute kube ngemaminithi lo-15 lasendzaleni
- **Silungele Sonto** - Tiskrini letihleliwe malini letikhombisa kutsi konkhe kulandziwe kuze kube Sonto lelilandzelako
- **Tidzinga kunakekelwa** - Tiskrini letingenayo ku-inthanethi noma letingakanaki emafayela

Ngaphansi kwaloko, sebentisa **Sesha tiskrini** kuze utfole tiskrini ngelebhuli, ngeliqembu, noma ngalokusebentako. Timphawu tesihlungo tinciphisa lutfo ku-**Tonkhe**, **Ehleliwe**, **Ngesandla**, **Tidzinga kunakekelwa**, noma ku-liqembu linye. Shintjashintja phakathi kwemibonakalo **Tabula** ne-**Gridi** ngesivalo lesisesobunxeni.

Ngalinye sikrini likhombisa:

- **Lebhuli** nemodeli yedivayisi
- **Iqembu**
- **Imodi** - **Ehleliwe** noma **Ngesandla**
- **Lokusebentako manje** - Lokusekhombisweni manje. Tiskrini letingesandla likhombisa **Ekilasini · Umfundisi ucindzetela Qala**
- **Silungele kuze** - Lidethi lelisikrini sekulandziwe kuze kube lilanga leliya kulo. Uma emafayela angalandziwanga, kuvela **N emafayela akhashile** esikhundleni salokho. Sikrini lesisha sikhombisa **Silindzele kusync yekucala**
- **Kubonwe kwekugcina** - Ngasiphi sikhatsi sikrini sekugcina ngena ngaso. Sikrini lesingenile isikhatsi eside likhombisa **Offline**, nalesingakaze sixhume likhombisa **Akukaze kubonwe**

Sebentisa **Etfula** kuze udlale ikhalenda letiskrini kusiphequluli sakho, noma **Hlela** kuze ushintje tilungiselelo tayo.

## Kubuka Sikrini

Cofa lebhuli lesikrini kuze uvule likhasi layo. Ngakwesobunxele:

- **Etfula kusiphequluli** - Dlala ikhalenda lalesikrini ngekugcwele kusiphequluli sakho, njengoba bekutawubukeka kuTV. Bona [Ukwethula Kusuka Kusiphequluli](./scheduled-playback.md#presenting-from-a-browser)
- **Hlela** - Shintja lebhuli, iqembu, noma imodi
- **Okunye** > **Susa kubambisana** - Nqamula i-TV. Iyabuyela ekuveleni likhodi lekubambisana

Lokusele kwelikhasi kunemacala lalaka:

- **Manje nelandzelako** - Lokusebentako manje nekutsi kuze nini, kulandzelwe yiko konkhe lokuhleliwe kuze kube ngemalanga lasikhombisa lalandzelako. Cofa **Vula ikhalenda** kuze uyefika kukhalenda laleliqembu
- **Tilandziwe** - **Silungele kuze** lidethi lapho konkhe kulandziwe khona, noma lutfo lwemafayela akhashile
- **Idivayisi** - I-ID yedivayisi, sitfombe, imodeli, linguve le-FreePlay app, ikheli le-IP, sitoreji lesisetjentisiwe nalesisele, kubonwe kwekugcina, nemodi
- **Lokuseduze** - Umsebenti lomusha kakhulu lobikwe nguleso sikrini

:::tip
Uma sikrini sikhombisa emafayela akhashile, qinisekisa kutsi sitikhonkhile futsi sinesitoreji lesisele. I-TV itawulanda emafayela akhashile ngayo ngeyayo ngalesikhatsi lelandzelako lapho ingena khona.
:::

## Kuhlela Sikrini

1. Cofa **Hlela** ngasesikrinini, noma likhasini layo.
2. Shintja **Lebhuli**, **Iqembu**, noma **Imodi** (**Ehleliwe** noma **Ngesandla**).
3. Cofa **Gcina**.

Kufaka sikrini eliqembwini lelinye kuyishintsha isiyolandzela ikhalenda laleliqembu.

## Tiqembu Tetiskrini

Iqembu letiskrini ngulutsi lwetiskrini letidlala lokufanako ngesikhatsi sinye, njengo "Lobby" noma "Kids Wing". Ngalinye iqembu linekhalenda layo futsi lingaba ne-**luphu lwekuzenzekela** loludlala noma nini lapho kungekho lokuhleliwe khona.

Kuphatsa tiqembu, cofa **Tiqembu** ngetulu kwelikhasi le-**Tiskrini**. Lutfo lwe-**Tiqembu tetiskrini** lukhombisa ligama leliqembu ngalinye, kutsi linetiskrini letingaki, neluphu lwalo lwekuzenzekela. Cofa **Tiskrini** kuze ubuyele emuva.

### Kungeta Iqembu

1. Ekubukweni kwe-**Tiqembu tetiskrini**, cofa **Engeta iqembu**.
2. Faka **Ligama**, njengalesibonelo "Lobby".
3. Khetsa uhlelo lwemsebenti lwe-**Luphu lwekuzenzekela**, noma usishiye ku-**Akukho — isikrini siyacima**.
4. Cofa **Gcina**.

Ungaphinde udale iqembu ngesikhatsi ubambisana sikrini ngekukhetsa **＋ Iqembu lelisha…** esimeni se-**Iqembu**.

### Kuhlela Noma Kususa Iqembu

1. Cofa **Hlela** ngasemucweni weliqembu.
2. Shintja ligama noma luphu lwekuzenzekela, beseucofa **Gcina**.
3. Kuze ususe iliqembu, cofa **Susa** bese uyaciniseka.

:::warning
Kususa iliqembu kuphinde kususe nekukhetsa kwaliqembu. Tiskrini taleliqembu tiyacima kuze utifake eliqembwini lelinye.
:::
