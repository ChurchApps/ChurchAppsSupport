# Kukhetsa Idivayisi ye-FreePlay

<div class="article-intro">

I-FreePlay isebenta ku-Amazon Fire TV, Android TV, ne-Apple TV. Leli khasi likutjela ngqo ukuthi yiphi idivayisi okufanele uyithenge ngetilevishini tetikhulu takho — kanye nalena lebalulekile ngaphezu kwaleyo, yiphi okufanele uyigweme.

</div>

## Impendulo Ngekushesha

Emabandla amaningi, thenga i-**Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K Max**. Inesitoreji se-16 GB (kabili kwetinti letiningi — kubalulekile ekulandzeni tifundvo ukuze udlale ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi), iprosesa lesisheshayo, i-Wi-Fi lenamandla, futsi isebenta nge-Fire OS, lapho i-FreePlay isekela khona ngokuphelele.

Ema-Fire TV Stick lamadala osewanawo (i-Fire TV Stick 4K, emamodeli amadala e-HD/Lite) nawo asebenta. Uma idivayisi ingafaka ama-app kusuka ku-Amazon Appstore, ingasebenta i-FreePlay.

## Ema-Fire TV Stick ane-Vega OS

Ema-Fire TV Stick lamasha kakhulu lamabhajethi e-Amazon, i-**Fire TV Stick HD (2026, isizukulwane sesi-2)** ne-**Fire TV Stick 4K Select**, asebenta i-**Vega OS** yeAmazon esikhundleni se-Fire OS. I-FreePlay isebenta ku-Vega OS: sayihlola ngoNovemba 2026 ku-Fire TV Stick HD (isizukulwane sesi-2) esebenta i-Vega OS 1.2. I-FreePlay yafakwa kusuka ku-Amazon Appstore, yahlanganiswa nekhalenda, yadlala tifundvo.

Ukuze uhlole ukuthi unayiphi, vula **Settings > My Fire TV > About**. I-Fire OS ibonisa inguqulo ethi "Fire OS". I-Vega OS ibonisa inguqulo efana ne-"OS 1.2".

## Tidivayisi Ekufanele Ukuzigwema

- **Roku**: I-FreePlay ayitfolakali kumadivayisi e-Roku.

## Kungani Sitoreji Silulekile

Umsebenti lomkhulu we-FreePlay kukudlala tifundvo **ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi** ekuseni kweSonto — abasebenti bayakulandza kusengaphambili lokuqukethwe kwalesi viki, bese tibukeko tidlala kusuka kusitoreji sedivayisi. Tifundvo letilandziwe tingatsatsa emagigabhayithi lamaningi, futsi inhlelo yekusebenta isebentisa lengxenye lenkhulu yesitoreji lesitfunyelwe ngalo idivayisi. Ilanga le-16 GB ye-Fire TV Stick 4K Max linika indzawo lenele yemavikhi lamaningi wetinhlelo; tidivayisi ze-8 GB ziyasebenta kodvwa zingadzinga kukhipha tilandziwe letindala kaningi.

## Kufaka i-FreePlay

Uma sewutfolile idivayisi yakho, kufaka kuthatsa cishe imizuzwana lemibili — bona [Qalisa](./index.md):

- **Fire TV:** Amazon Appstore → funa "FreePlay" ([sixhumanisi lesiqondile](https://www.amazon.com/Live-Church-Solutions-FreePLay/dp/B0GMC4S3RS/))
- **Android TV / Google TV (kubandzakanya ne-Onn):** Google Play Store → funa "FreePlay" ([sixhumanisi lesiqondile](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=church.freeplay))
- **Apple TV:** App Store → funa "FreePlay" ([sixhumanisi lesiqondile](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/freeplay-church/id6758970546))
