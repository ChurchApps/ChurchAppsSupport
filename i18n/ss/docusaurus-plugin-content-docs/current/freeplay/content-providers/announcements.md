# Tisaziso

<div class="article-intro">

FreePlay ingadlala ifoda lemaslayidi wetisaziso ku-TV yakho -- nasi sibonelo, esigodzini noma ngaphambi kokuba sifundvo siqale. Ukhetsa ifoda komunye wabahlinzeki bekuqukethwe abaxhunyiwe, FreePlay ilanda emaslayidi wayo kumshini, kuvele sitfombe se**Tisaziso** ebhaleni lechakijika ukuze ukwazi kuqala ngalokuphindwaphindwako nanoma yinini.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Ngaphambi Kokuqala</h4>

- [Xhuma kumhlinzeki wekuqukethwe](./connecting-providers.md) lobamba emaslayidi wakho wetisaziso, njengeDropbox noma OneDrive
- Beka tifanekiso noma tividiyo lofuna kuphindwaphindwa ndzawonye esikhwameni esinye kulowo mhlinzeki

</div>

## Kukhetsa Ifoda Letisaziso

1. Vula **Tilungiselelo** ngaphansi kwebhalo lechakijika, bese ukhetsa **Bahlinzeki**
2. Khetsa umhlinzeki lexhunyiwe lobamba emaslayidi wakho (utfola sikhangiso se**Lixhunyiwe**) ukuze uvule **Tilungiselelo Tomhlinzeki** wakhe
3. Khetsa **Sebentisa Letisaziso**
4. Iwindzi le**Khetsa Ifoda Letisaziso** liyavula. Hamba phakathi kwemafoda uze ufike lalobamba emaslayidi wakho, bese ukhetsa leyo foda (noma nanoma iyiphi ifayela lingaphakathi kwayo)
5. FreePlay ilanda emaslayidi ibuyele ku**Tilungiselelo Tomhlinzeki**, lapho umugca usakhombisa khona i**Iyaphindwaphinda "*igama lefoda*" -- *inombolo* emaslayidi alandiwe**

Ifoda linye kuphela lisetjentiswa tisaziso ngesikhatsi sinye. Ukukhetsa ifoda kumunye umhlinzeki kutsatfa indzawo yalelo langaphambili.

## Kudlala Tisaziso

Emaslayidi sekulandiwe, sitfombe se**Tisaziso** sivela eduze kwaphezulu kwebhalo lechakijika (ngaphansi kwe**Luhlelo Lwanamuhla**, uma i-TV yakho iyikhombisa). Khetsa kuso ukuze uqale ukuphindwaphinda.

- Tifanekiso tihlala esikrinini emasekhondi langu-15, ngaphandle uma umhlinzeki anika lelifayela sikhatsi sayo siqobo
- Tividiyoidlala kuze kuphele ngaphambi kokuya kulandzelako
- Ngemuva kwelisiladi lekugcina, kuphindwaphinda kuqale kulokwekucala
- Uma ifoda linevidiyo linye kuphela, iphindwaphinda ngaphandle kwekuhamba

Kuphindwaphinda kudlala emafayeleni agcinwe kumshini, ngako kuyaqhubeka nangaphandle kwesixhumanisi se-inthanethi. Ungasebentisa tilawuli letilawulo te-TV ifana nakunoma yikuphi lokunye kuqukethwe -- bona [Kudlala Lokuqukethwe](./playing-content.md).

## Kubuyekeza Emaslayidi

FreePlay ihlola ifoda letisaziso njalo lapho lohlelo lusebenta, ilande emaslayidi lamasha, futsi isuse emaslayidi lasulwa efoldeni.

Kuhlola ngalokusheshako, vula **Tilungiselelo Tomhlinzeki** talowo mhlinzeki bese ukhetsa **Hlola Tibuyekezo Tetisaziso**. Umugca utfola **Icocekile** nenombolo yemaslayidi uma ukuhlola sekuphelile.

:::info
Uma i-FreePlay ingafinyelela umhlinzeki, noma ifoda lingabuyela lingenalutfo, iyaqhubeka nekudlala emaslayidi lesewanawo. Ukuze uyeke kusebentisa tisaziso ngalokuphelele, vala njengobe kuchazwe ngaphansi.
:::

:::warning
Lisilayidi lelitfutfuswe ngenguqulo lentsha ngaphansi kwelifayela lelifanako ayilandwa futsi. Ukuze utjintje lisilayidi, yengeze njengelifayela lelisha bese usula lelindzala.
:::

## Kuvala Tisaziso

Vula **Tilungiselelo Tomhlinzeki** talowo mhlinzeki bese ukhetsa **Sebentisa Letisaziso** futsi. I-FreePlay iyeka kusebentisa leyo foda, isuse emaslayidi alandiwe kumshini, futsi isuse **Tisaziso** ebhaleni lechakijika.

Kukhipha umhlinzeki kuphindze kuvale tisaziso letitfolwe kuye.
