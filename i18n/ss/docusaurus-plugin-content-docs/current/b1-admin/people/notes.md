# Emanothi

<div class="article-intro">

Likhasi **Emanothi** liqoqa emanothi lamasha kusuka kuyo yonkhe i-B1 Admin endzaweni leyinye, kute utfole kucinisa emalunga ekunakekelweni kwebavelonkhe, emalandvo, nekuhlelela ngaphandle kwekuvula ilekhodi ngalinye. Lilandzisa emanothi abhalelwe bantfu, emaqembu lowubusako, emicimbi yekukhalenda, kanye netikhadi temsebenti, kusuka kwalasha kuya kwalaphansi, futsi linxusa ngalinye libuyisele endzaweni lebhalelwe kuyo.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Ngaphambi Kokuqala</h4>

- Likhasi **Emanothi** livela kumenyu uma unemvume ya **Hlela Bantfu** noma uhola nakancane liqembu linye. Bona [Kabelana Tindzima](./roles-permissions.md).
- Likhasi likhombisa kuphela emanothi osevumelekile kuwafundza. Litab ngalinye lilandela imitsetso lefanako nalerekhodi lemanothi lalo (bona [Litab Ngalinye Likhombisa Ni](#what-each-tab-shows)).

</div>

## Kuvula Likhasi Lemanothi

1. Vula [Imenyu yekuhamba](../introduction.md#getting-around-with-the-jump-menu) (ibha yekufuna lephezulu kwesobunxele kwe-B1 Admin) bese uvula **Bantfu**.
2. Chofoza **Emanothi**.
3. Likhasi livulela kulithab **Bantfu**. Sebenta ematab alo ekhanda lelikhasi ukushintjana phakatsi kwe**Bantfu**, **Emaqembu**, **Emicimbi**, kanye ne**Tinhlelo Lekusebenta**.

## Kufunda Lolwhlu

Linothi ngalinye lolwhlu likhombisa:

- **Lapho libhalelwe khona** -- ligama lemuntfu, ligama leliqembu, sihloko semcimbi, noma sihloko setikhadi temsebenti. Chofoza ligama kuvula lerekhodi bese ufunda incoko lephelele noma wengeze linothi.
- **Lesiphephile** -- liphawu emanothini emuntfu lavela ku-Emanothi Lasiphephile lemuntfu.
- **Lowabhala nesikhatsi** -- "Ngu" ligama lomlobi, kulandzelwe lilanga nesikhatsi lasetjentiswe ngaso.
- **Linothi ngalo** -- emanothi lamade afinyeleleka lamfishane kulolwhlu. Vula lerekhodi kuze ufunde linothi lonkhe.

Lolwhlu lukhombisa emanothi lamasha kakhulu kucala. Chofoza **Layisha letinye** ekugcineni kuze uvele emadzala.

Emanothi abhalelwe umuntfu losasusiwe noma losehlanganiswe nelinye lerekhodi, akavelelu ethabini **Bantfu**.

Likhasi Lemanothi alikwazi kuhleleka, lifundeka kuphela. Kuze wengeze, uhlele noma uphendvule linothi, chofoza ligama lalorekhodi kuze uyivule.

## Litab Ngalinye Likhombisa Ni {#what-each-tab-shows}

| Litab | Emanothi akhombisiwe | Labo labakwati kuwabona | Kuchofoza ligama kuvula |
|-------|----------------------|-------------------------|-------------------------|
| **Bantfu** | Emanothi emalekhodi emuntfu | Bantfu labanemvume ye**Hlela Bantfu**. Emanothi lasiphephile dzinga ne**Bona Emanothi Lasiphephile** | Emanothi emuntfu |
| **Emaqembu** | [Emanothi emholi](../groups/group-members.md#leader-notes) emaqembwini | Kuphela emaholi alelinye liqembu ngisho nakaBusi | Emanothi aleliqembu |
| **Emicimbi** | [Emanothi emcimbi](../calendars/creating-calendars.md#event-notes) | Sisebenti lesikwati kuhlela lokuqukethwe noma kuphatha emakhalenda, kanye nalabanye bantfu lavunyelwe yisetingi ye**Labo labangabona emanothi** yemcimbi | Ikhalenda leliqembu lemcimbi |
| **Tinhlelo Lekusebenta** | Emanothi emakhadini [etinhlelo tekusebenta](../serving/workflows.md#working-the-board) | Noma ngubani lokwati kuvula likhadi, kuhlanganisa umuntfu noma liqembu lelabelwe kulo | Ibhodi lethinhlelo tekusebenta lelikhadi | 

Uma litab lingana nalutfo lolungakabonakali, litsi kanjalo -- njengasekutsini, "Akukho manothi emaqembwini lohola"

:::tip
Emathredi emanothi anesithombe sesigidi sekwati tesaziso. Chofoza kuso kuze utfole saziso semanothi amasha kulelithredi (sitfombe siyashintjana ngako nje), bese uphindza uchofoze kuze uyayicima. Lamasethingi ekuvulwa nekuvalwa awukaze avele njengemanothi likhasini Lemanothi.
:::
