# Ukubuyekeza Ticelo Zokususa Iakhawunti

<div class="article-intro">

Lapho licebo linekhofi Yekuphatsa Ukuvunyelwa Kwemakhasi (Directory Approval Group), kususwa kwe-akhawunti akusenti ngaphandle kwencozo. Sicelo selungu sishintsha sibe ngumsebenti lekhofi yakho yekuvunyela ibuyekeze ngaphambi kwekuba kususwe lutfo. Leli khasi lichaza kutsi sicelo senziwa kanjani, kutsi ungakuvumela noma ungakwenqaba kanjani, kanye nalokwenteka ngasinye.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Ngaphambi Kokuqala</h4>

- Kufanele kube nelikhofi **Directory Approval Group** elilungiselelwe ngaphansi kwe-**Mobile &rarr; Member portal**. Ngaphandle kwalelo, kuchofoza **Delete my account** ekhasini le-Profile kusasusa i-akhawunti ngekushesha, ngaphandle kwesinyatelo sekubuyekeza. Bona [Mobile App Settings](../settings/mobile-app.md).
- Kuvuma noma kunqaba sicelo kudinga lilungelo **People &gt; Edit**.

</div>

## Indlela Lilungu Lenta Ngayo Sicelo Sekususwa

Sicelo sekususwa kwe-akhawunti senziwa ekhasini le-**My Profile** — leli khasi lelabelwana le-akhawunti lichazwe ku-[Managing Your Profile](./managing-profile.md) — ngaphansi kwesigaba se-**Account Deletion**. Uma kulungiselelwe likhofi lekuvunyela, ukuqinisekisa sicelo akususi lutfo kwasekuqaleni. Esikhundleni salokho, kwenta lokulandzelako:

1. Kudala umsebenti lovulekile lobizwa ngelithi **"Account deletion request"**, lonikwe likhofi Directory Approval Group, ngaphansi kwe-**Serving &rarr; My Work**.
2. Kuvala inkinobho i-**Delete my account** kulowo muntfu, futsi kubonakalise umlayezo wekutsi sicelo sisalinde kubuyekezwa.

Kufaka sicelo lesinye ngesikhatsi lesinye sisavulekile kuvula kabusha umsebenti lofanako — umuntfu munye angaba nesicelo sekususwa lesilindile ngesikhatsi sinye kuphela.

## Ukubuyekeza Sicelo

1. Yana ku-**Serving &rarr; My Work** (noma **Assigned to My Groups** ku-dashboard yakho, lapho kuvela khona [ticelo tekushintsha kwemaprofayili](./approving-profile-changes.md)).
2. Vula umsebenti lobizwa ngelithi **"Account deletion request from *Name*"**.
3. Uyakubona tenti letimbili: **Approve deletion** kanye ne-**Decline**.

### Kuvuma

Qinisekisa **"Permanently anonymize this person's record and remove their login? This cannot be undone."** Lokhu kutfutfukisa tinhlobonhlobo letivamile letichaza umuntfu ngetinani letivamile (kufanana nalokusetjentiswa yisenzo se-**Data Management &gt; Anonymize** kulwati lomuntfu — bona [Data Security](../settings/data-security.md)) futsi kususe logini yakhe. Umsebenti uvala wedvwa, futsi lilungu litfolakala ngekutsi sicelo salo sivunyelwe.

### Kunqaba

Kunqaba kudinga sizatfu, ngoba i-GDPR ivumela kuphikiswa kwesicelo sekususwa kuphela ngenca yemtsetfo ekhetsekile:

- **Kugcinwa ngemtsetfo** (tirekhodi tetimbuso, tekutfutfukisa, noma temsebenti)
- **Kudzingeka ngenca yelicala lemtsetfo**
- **Lokunye** — chaza ebhokisini lemabhalo (okungenani emagama langu-10)

Lilungu litfolakala ngesinqumo nangesizatfu losinikile, futsi lingafaka kabusha sicelo salo noma lidlulisele kumphathi wemtsetfo uma lingavumelani.

:::info
Tindzawo tinemalanga langu-30 kuphendvula sicelo sekususwa. Umsebenti unemalanga langu-28 wekuphetfwa, futsi likhofi lekuvunyela litfola tesikhumbutso letizenzekelako uma lisavulekile emalanga langu-21 nalangu-27.
:::

:::tip
Ticelo tekususwa kanye netekushintsha kwemaprofayili tisebentisa likhofi lefanako lekuvunyela Directory Approval Group kanye nalofanako lekubuyekeza lelisekelwe emisebentini (Tasks) — bona [Approving Profile Changes](./approving-profile-changes.md) uma nawe udzinga kubuyekeza ticelo tekuvuselela ilitfunga.
:::
