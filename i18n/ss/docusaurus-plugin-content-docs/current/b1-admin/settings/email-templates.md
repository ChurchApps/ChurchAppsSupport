# Timodeli Timeyili

<div class="article-intro">

Timodeli Timeyili ikuvumela ukubaulondoloze lokuqukethwe kwemeyili lokungasetjentiswa kabusha -- umlayezo wekwemukela, sikhumbuzo selwati, kanye nombulelo wekuniketa -- kuze wena (noma [inkambu yemisebenti](../serving/workflows.md)) ukwati kuyithumela ngekucindzetela kunye kuphela kunekutsi uyibhale kusukela ekuqaleni njalo. Uhlu lolufanako luphindze luvele tindlela temilayezo yetekisi yemabhokisi etekisi yeliqembu kanye nemuntfu.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Ngaphambi Kokuqala</h4>

- Udzinga kufinyelela kusifunda sekuMiselwa kwe-B1 Admin.

</div>

## Kufinyelela Timodeli Timeyili

1. Ku-B1 Admin, vula [Imenyu Yokuhamba](../introduction.md#getting-around-with-the-jump-menu) (ibha yekufuna ngentsandvo-kwesobunxele) bese unwesa **Tilungiselelo**.
2. Cindzetela **Timodeli Timeyili**.
3. Uyakubona uhlu lwetimodeli letikhona netihloko tato, tigaba, kanye nelilanga lekugcina lekushintjwa kwato.

## Kwakha Imodeli

1. Cindzetela **Imodeli Lensha**.
2. Faka **Ligama Lemodeli** lekutsi ulikhombe ohlwini, bese ukhetsa **Tigaba** (Lijeneralini, Imicimbi, Emaqembu, Kuniketa, Kwemukela, noma Tekisi) kuze kusite ekuhleleni timodeli takho. Khetsa **Tekisi** ukuze wente imodeli yemilayezo yetekisi kunaloko (bona [Timodeli Tetekisi](#text-templates) lingentasi).
3. Faka umugca **Sihloko**.
4. Bhala **Umbhalo** usebenzisa umhleli wombhalo lophucuke.
5. Cindzetela **Gcina**.

## Tinkhala Tetimaki

Cindzetela ibhaji yenkhala yemaki ngaphezulu kwe-Sihloko noma Umbhalo ukuze uyifake kulapho ikhesa lakho likhona -- cindzetela kulombhalo lapho ufuna khona inkhala kuqala, bese ucindzetela ibhaji. Ikhesa lakho lihlala lapho lilikhona, ngako ungaqhubeka nekubhala ngemuva kwenkhala lefakiwe. Uma ucindzetela ibhaji yoMbhalo ngaphandle kwekucindzetela kuMbhalo kuqala, inkhala itfole ekugcineni kombhalo. Ngesikhatsi imeyili isitfunyelwe, inkhala ngayinye itfolwa yindzawo lapho kukhona khona lolwazi lwemuntfu lolwamukelako:

- `{{firstName}}`, `{{lastName}}`, `{{displayName}}` -- Ligama lalowo lolwamukelako
- `{{email}}` -- Likheli lemeyili lalowo lolwamukelako
- `{{churchName}}` -- Ligama lebandla lakho

## Timodeli Tetekisi

Imodeli lenegigaba **Tekisi** ngumlayezo wombhalo lolula wekutfumela ngetekisi kunekutsi ube yimeyili:

- Akunasihloko. Umhleli wombhalo lophucuke utfutfukiswa ngebhokisi lilula le**Umlayezo Wetekisi** lelibamba kuya kubo b-1,600 wetinhlamvu.
- Tibhaji tetinkhala temaki ngaphezulu kwebhokisi tifaka `{{firstName}}`, `{{lastName}}`, `{{displayName}}`, `{{email}}`, noma `{{churchName}}` endzaweni yekhesa lakho.
- Timodeli tetekisi tiyavela kusikhetsi se**Layisha Imodeli (ngekukhetsa)** sewindzi yetekisi yeliqembu [leliqembu](../groups/group-members.md#texting-group-members) noma yemuntfu munye. Kukhetsa imodeli kugcwalisa umlayezo, futsi ungakhona kuwuhlela ngaphambi kokuwutfumela.
- Asitfshwa ebhokisini letikhetsi temodeli yemeyili, kuhlanganise nesenzo se**Tfumela imeyili** semsebenti.

Kutfumela ngetekisi kudzinga [mhlinzeki wetekisi](./church-settings.md#texting) lolotfunyelwe.

## Kubuketa Imodeli

Cindzetela **Buketa** kuze ubone sihloko nombhalo sakho bekunemanani lesampula ngetinkhala temaki, ngaphambi kwekutsi uyigcine noma uyitfumele.

## Kusebentisa Imodeli

Timodeli letigciniwe titfolakala kutsi tikhetsise lapho ubhala imeyili yabantu noma yeliqembu, futsi njengesenzo kuma-[Workflows](../serving/workflows.md). Ngaphambi kwekutsi libandla lakho litfumele tona, ithimba le-ChurchApps kudzingeka lisivume kutfumela imeyili yeliqembu kanye nje. Bona [Kuvulela Imeyili Yeliqembu Ebandleni Lakho](../groups/group-members.md#turning-on-group-email-for-your-church).

## Kuhlela Nekususa

Cindzetela sithombe se**Hlela** ngecetulo lemodeli kuze uyivuselele, noma sithombe se**Susa** kuze uyisuse ngasikhatsi sinye.
