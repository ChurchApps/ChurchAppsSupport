# Tilungiselelo Telicawe

<div class="article-intro">

Iphepha Letilungiselelo Telicawe ngumndzeni lapho ulungisa khona lolwazi lwelicawe lakho lwasisekelo, emininingwane yekutfola, kanye nemabhuloho (branding). Lemininingwane isetfwa kuwo wonkhe emathuluzi e-ChurchApps, kubandzakanya nelive lakho le-B1.church kanye ne-B1 App.

## Kuhlela Lolwazi Lwelicawe Lakho

1. Ku-B1 Admin, vula [Imenyu Ye-Jump](../introduction.md#getting-around-with-the-jump-menu) (indzawo yekufuna ngaphezulu ngesobunxele), undzise **Tilungiselelo**, bese ucindzetela **Tilungiselelo**.
2. Esigabeni se**Lolwazi Lwelicawe** (phezulu kwaleliphepha), ucindzetele **Hlela**.
3. Buyekeza noma yimaphi emagceke alandzelako:
   - **Ligama Lelicawe** -- ligama lobonakala kuwo wonkhe emikhiqizo ye-ChurchApps.
   - **Likheli** -- likheli lendzawo lelicawe lakho.
   - **Lolwazi Lwekutfola** -- inombolo yefoni, i-imeyili, nemininingwane yekutfola yalokunye.
   - **Indzawo Yesikhatsi** -- indzawo yesikhatsi yelicawe lakho. Bona [Indzawo Yesikhatsi](#time-zone) lapha ngezansi.
4. Ucindzetele **Gcina** ukuze usebenzise tinguquko takho.

## Kusetfwa Kwe-Subdomain Yakho

Lelicawe lakho litfola i-subdomain lemahhala ku-**yourchurch.1.church**. Lena yiyo likheli lekhompyuta lapho emalungu nemavakashi angafinyelela khona kukhona kwelicawe lakho ku-inthanethi.

1. Ephepheni Letilungiselelo, ucindzetele **Hlela** esigabeni se**Lolwazi Lwelicawe** bese uthola indzawo ye**Subdomain**.
2. Faka i-subdomain oyifisako (ngesibonelo, “gracechurch” ye-gracechurch.1.church).
3. Gcina tinguquko takho.

:::info
I-subdomain yakho kufanele ibe yodvwa kuwo wonkhe emacawe e-ChurchApps. Uma ligama lotifisela selisele satfwa, zama kwengeta ligama ledolobha lakho noma sifundza (ngesibonelo, “gracechurch-dallas”).
:::

Uma ufuna emavakashi afinyelele websayithi yakho ngedomeyini yakho (ngesibonelo, **`www.gracechurch.org`**), bona [Domeyini Yemtsetfo](./custom-domain.md).

## Kulungisa Imabhuloho

Yenta kahle indlela lelicawe lakho lebonakala ngayo kuwo wonkhe emathuluzi e-ChurchApps:

1. Layisha i-**logo yelicawe** lakho ngekucindzetela indzawo ye-logo bese ukhetsa lifayela lesithombe.
2. Engeta letinye **tithombe telicawe** tetisetfwa kuwebhusayithi yakho kanye ne[ka-app yemobhayili](./mobile-app.md).

:::tip
Ukuze uphumelele kakhulu, sebenzisa i-logo lenemuva lekhanyako ngefomethi ye-PNG. Lokhu kuciniseka kutsi ibonakala kahle kuzo zombili emuva lelikhanyako nelimnyama.
:::

## Lusuku Lwekucala Lwesonto

Khetsa lusuku lolutsite emakhalenda akho. Lincenye ye**Lusuku Lwekucala Lwesonto** esigabeni se**Lolwazi Lwelicawe** ikhomba ku**Lisontfo** ngekutsandzeka, kodvwa ingasetfwa kunoma lusuku. Lishintjwe, liyahlonishwa kuwo emakhalenda e-B1 Admin nasepetfwini lemalungu le-B1.church -- emakhalenda emaqembu, emakhalenda lahleliwe, kanye nomhleli wetehlakalo wonkhe uhlela macala emaviki aqala ngelusuku olukhetsile.

## Indzawo Yesikhatsi

Lincenye ye**Indzawo Yesikhatsi** esigabeni se**Lolwazi Lwelicawe** isetsa indzawo yesikhatsi yelicawe lakho (ngesibonelo, *America/Chicago*). Khetsa **Asikho** uyishiye ingenalutfo. Uma sesigcinwe, indzawo itawuvela kusifinyeto selolwazi Lwelicawe.

I-B1 isebenzisa leyo ndzawo yesikhatsi kumasikhatsi ahlanganyelwa yonkhe lelicawe, njengetinkumbuliso tetehlakalo kanye nemasheduli etilevishini ze[FreePlay](../../freeplay/scheduled-mode/scheduling-the-calendar.md) takho. [Emasheduli wekungena](../attendance/setup.md#setting-a-check-in-schedule) ilinganiswa esikhatsini sendzawo yelicawe lelisebenta [likhampasi](./campuses.md) kucala, futsi isebenzisa indzawo yesikhatsi yelicawe kuphela uma likhampasi lelo lingenayo. Uma emakhampasi akho wonkhe ase-indzaweni yesikhatsi yinye, yisete kulelicawe kanye nakulilinye likhampasi ngalinye.

## Sifunda (Lifomethi Yedeti Nelucingo)

Sicalo se**Sifunda** silawula indlela yekubhalwa kwetindzaba netikhatsi kuwo wonkhe i-B1. Ngekuzenisa, tindzaba tisebenzisa lifomethi yase-United States (ngesibonelo, “Sep 28, 2026” ne“9/28/2026”). Emacawe angaphandle kwe-US angaguculela kulifomethi yawo -- ngesibonelo, ngekukhetsa English (United Kingdom) kukhombisa “28 Sept 2026” ne“28/09/2026” esikhundleni salokho.

1. Ephepheni Letilungiselelo, ucindzetele **Sifunda** kuhlu lwesandla sekhohlo, bese ucindzetela **Hlela** esigabeni se**Sifunda**.
2. Khetsa sifunda sakho kulincenye ye**Sifunda**. Liphindzo ngalinye likhombisa deti lesibonelo kuze ubone kahle kutawubukeka kanjani emadetini.
3. Ucindzetele **Gcina**.

Sigaba se**Sifunda** kulandzela sibonisa sifunda lokhetsiwe, sibonelo se**Lifomethi Yedeti**, kanye nelifomethi ye**Tinombolo Tefoni**.

### Lifomethi Yenombolo Yefoni

Sigaba lesifanako se**Sifunda** linesetingi ye**Tinombolo Tefoni** lelawula indlela tinombolo tefoni letifakwa ngayo kurekhodi lomuntu:

- **Yemhlaba (nekhodi yelive)** -- lokuzenisako. Tindzawo tefoni tibonisa sikhetsi selibhendzela lelive bese tigcina tinombolo nekhodi yelive (ngesibonelo, +1 918 555 1234).
- **Yendzawo (njengoba ubhala)** -- tindzawo tefoni tiba ngemabhokisi emabhalwa lavamile futsi tigcina tinombolo njengoba uzibhala, ngaphandle kwekhodi yelive (ngesibonelo, 0701 234 5678). Khetsa lokhu uma lelicawe lakho libhala tinombolo ngelifomethi yendzawo futsi lifuna i-B1 ingangeni khodi yelive.

Uma i**Yendzawo** ikhethiwe, sigaba se**Sifunda** sibonisa futsi “Tinombolo tefoni tendzawo” ecaleni kwesifunda sakho.

:::tip
Kutfumela imiyalezo kusebenza kahle kakhulu uma tinombolo tifaka nekhodi yelive. Uma utfumela imiyalezo kusuka ku-B1, gcina lifomethi ye**Yemhlaba**, noma uciniseke kutsi tinombolo longatibhala kuModi Yendzawo tifaka nekhodi yelive.
:::

Sifunda sakho sisebenta kumadeti nasemasikhatsini kuwo wonkhe B1 Admin nasewebhusayithini yakho ye-B1.church kanye nasepetfwini lemalungu, kubandzakanya tinkhulumo, tipositi tebhulogi, emakhalenda emaqembu, kanye nemacebo ekukhonta, kuze emalungu abone emadetini ngalifomethi lefanana nalena yebasebenti bakho.

## Kutfumela Imiyalezo {#texting}

Xhuma umhlinzeki wekutfumela imiyalezo ukuze utfumele imiyalezo ye-SMS emuntfwini noma kuwo wonkhe emaqembu kusuka ku-B1 Admin. Imiyalezo itfumelwa ngalokhangako kumhlinzeki, ngalokusho emitengo yakhe nemikhawulo yakhe isebenta.

1. Ephepheni Letilungiselelo, ucindzetele **Kutfumela Imiyalezo** kuhlu lwesandla sekhohlo, bese ucindzetela **Hlela** esigabeni se**Kutfumela Imiyalezo**.
2. Khetsa **Umhlinzeki**:
   - **Clearstream** -- faka i-**API Key**. Yakha enye kuma-Account Settings akho e-Clearstream ngaphansi kwe-API Keys.
   - **Text In Church** -- faka i-**API Key**. Cela kusekelwa kwe-Text In Church kutsi ukhone kutfola i-developer API kucala, bese wakha likhiyi esigabeni se-Account Settings > Developer API.
   - **Nalo Solutions** (Ghana) -- faka likhiyi lekugunyaza lelisuka ku-akhawunti yakho ye-Nalo Solutions njenge-**API Key**, kanye ne-**Sender ID** (kuya kutinhlamvu ze-11) lelivunywe yi-Nalo kuwe.
   - **Twilio** -- sebenzisa i-akhawunti yelicawe lakho ye-Twilio; i-Twilio ikubhilisa ngqo. Kopisha i-**Account SID** ne-**Auth Token** kusuka ku-[Twilio Console](https://console.twilio.com), bese ufaka i-**From Number** -- inombolo yefoni ye-Twilio ebhalisiwe ye-A2P 10DLC, ngalifomethi lefana na `+15551234567`. Ngemva kokugcina, namathela i-inbound webhook URL (bona ngaphansi) ku-webhook yenombolo ye-**A message comes in** ku-Twilio, kuze timphendvulo te-STOP futsi ticondziwe nakuB1.
3. Ucindzetele **Gcina**.

Ukuyeka kutfumela imiyalezo, setela **Umhlinzeki** ku-**Asikho** bese ugcina. Lokhu kususa umhlinzeki lologciniwe.

### Kukhipha Labo Labaphendvula ngeSTOP

Umhlinzeki akugcinwa, vula kabusha sigaba se**Kutfumela Imiyalezo** ngeku**Hlela**. Ngaphansi kwetilungiselelo temhlinzeki, indzawo ye**Inbound (STOP) webhook URL** lefundeka kuphela ikhombisa likheli lekhompyuta lelikhetsekile kulelicawe lakho. Cindzetela lendzawo ukuze ukhetse likheli, ukopishe, bese ukumatfela kulisethingi ye-webhook yemiyalezo lengenako (yemphendvulo) yemhlinzeki wakho wekutfumela imiyalezo. Lokhu kusebenta nanoma ngumuphi wemhlinzeki labetulwe ngenhla longadlulisela timphendvulo letingenako ku-webhook.

Ngemva kalokho, uma umuntu aphendvula ku**STOP** (noma STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, OPTOUT, noma REVOKE) emyalezweni wakho, i-B1 ifuna bonkhe labanenombolo yemakhasi efanana, ibamarka njengalabakhishwe, futsi icima tinkumbuliso tabo temiyalezo. Bayeshelwa-ke kumiyalezo yemaqembu. Timphendvulo letinye tiyashaywa.

:::info
Lokhu kusebenzisa lisethingi lefanako yekukhishwa njengalena ye**Fihla mina ehlwini lwemalungu**, ngako labo labaphendvula ku-STOP bayakhombisa njenge**Ifihlakele kulihlu** ephrofayilini yabo. Indzawo itfola kuphela nakucinywe umhlinzeki lologciniwe -- uma ushintsha imenyu yemhlinzeki ibe lowehlukile, gcina kucala ukuze ubone i-URL futsi.
:::

Umhlinzeki akuxhunyiwe, labasebenti labanemvume yekutfumela imiyalezo babona inkinobho ye**Text** ephepheni lelilungu (**Text this group**) nelomuntu lonenombolo yemakhasi (**Send text message**). Tibhale umyalezo wakho bese ucindzetela **Send**. Lencenye iyabala tinhlamvu ne-SMS segments. Lelilungu, likhombisa kutsi bangaki emalungu layakutfola umyalezo ngaphambi kwekutfumela:

- Emalungu langenayo inombolo yemakhasi lerekhodiwe ayayeshelwa.
- Emalungu lakhetsa **Fihla mina ehlwini lwemalungu** ayabalwa njengalabakhishwe futsi ayeshelwa.
- Emalungu emndeni labelana ngenombolo yemakhasi linye atfola umyalezo kanye nje.

### Kwenta Imiyalezo Ibe Yomuntu Ngetinkhundla Tekuhlanganisa {#personalizing-texts-with-merge-fields}

Ngaphansi kwebhokisi lemyalezo, ifenisi yekutfumela ikhombisa tinchayi tetindzawo letibekiwe: **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Display Name**, ne**Church Name**. Cindzetela inchayi kuze ufake indzawo yayo (`{{firstName}}`, `{{lastName}}`, `{{displayName}}`, noma `{{churchName}}`) endzaweni yekhesha. Umyalezo utfumelwa, indzawo ngayinye itsatfwa ngemininingwane yalowo nalowo ofuna kuwutfola, ngalokho umyalezo wemaqembu lofana na `Sawubona {{firstName}}, sibonana ngeSonto!` ufika kulilungu ngalinye ngeliga lalo. Tindzawo tisebenta kumiyalezo yemaqembu nakumiyalezo yemuntfu munye. Imiyalezo yemaqembu iphindze ibe nenchayi ye**Group Name** (`{{groupName}}`), leyatawufakelwa ligama lelilungu.

Ukuze uphindze usebenlise umyalezo, uwugcine njenge[thempulethi yemyalezo](./email-templates.md#text-templates) (thempulethi ye-imeyili lenesigaba se**Text**) bese uyikhetsa kusuka ku**Load Template (optional)** ngaphezulu kwewindowu ye-Text.

:::info
Mkhawulo wetinhlamvu lo-1,600 utfumelela umyalezo njengoba uwubhala. Ngemva kwekugcwaliswa kwetindzawo, noma yimuphi umbhalo lodlula tinhlamvu ta-1,600 uyasikwa kulelo bude.
:::

Imiyalezo ingaphinda itfumelwe ngekutsi ngekhatsi kwesinyatelo se[workflow](../serving/workflows.md#sending-a-text) nesenzo se**Send Text**, lesebentisa umhlinzeki lofanako netindzawo.

## Kugcinwa Kwemafayela

Ngokuzenisa, emafayela lolayisha kuwebhusayithi yakho (ngekhatsi kwe[Files](../website/files.md)) kanye nasemasigabeni lalandzelako etinkhatsi asebentisa kugcinwa lokusitfakelwa kwe-B1 lokumahhala, kuya kulamamegabhayiti langu-100. Uma udzinga indzawu lenkhulu, ungaxhuma kugcinwa kwelifu lakho kunalokho -- emalayisho lamasha ayakufika ngqo ku-akhawunti yakho ngaphandle kwemkhawulo wesiwukuhlinzeka.

1. Ephepheni Letilungiselelo, ucindzetele **Kugcinwa Kwemafayela** kuhlu lwesandla sekhohlo, bese ucindzetela **Hlela** esigabeni se**Kugcinwa Kwemafayela**.
2. Khetsa umhlinzeki: **Google Drive**, **Dropbox**, **OneDrive**, noma i**S3-compatible bucket** (i-AWS S3, i-Cloudflare R2, i-Backblaze B2, njll.).
3. Ku-Google Drive, Dropbox, noma OneDrive, ucindzetele **Connect** bese ungena ukuze ugunyaze kufinyelela. Ku-S3-compatible bucket, faka likhiyi lakho lekufinyelela, imfihlo, libito le-bucket, nesisekelo se-URL lesidzabukako.
4. Ucindzetele **Gcina**.

:::info
Lokhu kuthinta kuphela emalayisho lamasha kuFayela lewebhusayithi yakho nasemasigabeni lafanako. Tithombe temagalari, tithombe letincane, ama-logo, kanye netithombe temuntfu kuhlala njalo ekugcinweni kwe-B1 lokuzenisako.
:::

## Kukhushulwa Kwemabanga

Uma ulandzela **Ibanga** ngetingane nemafundzi, i-B1 ingakhuphula bonkhe ibanga linye ngelilanga olikhetsako (ngesibonelo, ngo-1 ka-August) mhlawumbe kunekutsi udzinge kuhlela ilwazi ngalinye ngesandla.

1. Ephepheni Letilungiselelo, ucindzetele **Kukhushulwa Kwemabanga** kuhlu lwesandla sekhohlo, bese ucindzetela **Hlela** esigabeni se**Kukhushulwa Kwemabanga**.
2. Vula iswichi (ikhombisa **Ivuliwe**) bese ukhetsa **Inyanga** ne**Lilanga** lapho kutawukhushulwa khona emabanga ngalonyaka ngamunye. Ngalolo suku, wonkhe lowo lonebanga utawukhushulwa ibanga linye, kanti labafundzi besi-12 bayoba **Bagradwetiwe**.
3. Gcina tinguquko takho.

Ukuyeka kukhushulwa ngekuzenisa, vala iswichi ikhombise **Ivaliwe** bese ugcina. Lilanga lekukhushulwa liyasuswa, futsi emabanga ngeke aphinde atfutfuke ngekwawo.

## Kubhalisa Bavolontiya {#volunteer-signup}

Ngokuzenisa, emalungu ahlola [ekubhalisweni kwebavolontiya](../../b1-church/serving/volunteer-signup.md) abona sonkhe sonkhe lesitfwala lesitako lesinetindzawo letitfolakalako. Uma lolohlu lulenkhulu, ungakhombisa kuphela sonto lelilandzelako lalinye ngalinye lohlobo lwemacebo.

1. Ephepheni Letilungiselelo, ucindzetele **Kubhalisa Bavolontiya** kuhlu lwesandla sekhohlo, bese ucindzetela **Hlela** esigabeni se**Kubhalisa Bavolontiya**.
2. Vula **Khombisa kuphela sonto lelilandzelako lalinye ngalinye lohlobo lwemacebo**.
3. Gcina tinguquko takho.

Uma lesi sethingi sivuliwe, lohlobo ngalunye lwemacebo lukhombisa kuphela lelicebo lalo lelilandzelako. Sonto lelilandzelako livela ngemva kwaloyo lilanga kudlulile. Umugca we**Emaviki Akhonjiwe** esigabeni ufundza **Kuphela sonto lelilandzelako lalinye ngalinye lohlobo lwemacebo** noma **Wonkhe emaviki latako**.

## Imfihlo Yekunikela {#giving-privacy}

Labo labanemvume yekunikela ngalokuvamile babona tinhlanganisela tekunikela komuntu esirekhodini lakhe [lomuntu](../people/adding-people.md#editing-details). Uma ungathanda kungabi netinani esikrinini njalo lapho irekhodi livulwa, ungawafihla kuze kube kutsi umuntu acele.

1. Ephepheni Letilungiselelo, ucindzetele **Imfihlo Yekunikela** kuhlu lwesandla sekhohlo, bese ucindzetela **Hlela** esigabeni se**Imfihlo Yekunikela**.
2. Vula **Fihla kunikela esirekhodini lomuntu kuze kucindzetelwe**.
3. Gcina tinguquko takho.

Uma lesi sethingi sivuliwe, likhadi lekunikela kurekhodi lomuntu litsi “Kunikela kufihliwe kumarekhodi emuntfu.” Cindzetela **Khombisa kunikela** kuze ulayishe bese ukhombisa kunikela komuntu loyo. Liyafihleka futsi uma uvula omunye umuntu. Kutsi ngubani labakhona kubona kunikela akushintjwa: kuphela labanemvume yekunikela ngabo bakhona babona likhadi.

## Kungenisa Nekuphuma

Lixhumanisi le**Import/Export** ngaphansi kwegama lelicawe lakho ephepheni Letilungiselelo livula ithuluzi lelikhethekile kuwindowu lentsha yesiphequluli. Sebentisa lokhu ukuze:

- Ngenisa tinkhulumo temalungu kusuka kwesinye sikhungo sekuphatha licawe.
- Phuma ngedatha yakho ye-ChurchApps ngenhloso yekugcina ngemtsetfo noma yekudlulisa.

Lokhu kusita kakhulu uma usetsa lelicawe lakho kucala futsi udzinga kudlulisa emarekhodi akhona ngekungena ku-ChurchApps.

:::warning
Uma ungenisa datha, gcina emarekhodi akho akhona kucala. Imisebenti yekungenisa yengeta datha esistimini sakho futsi ingadala tingeniselo letiphindzaphindzekako uma yentiwa kabili.
:::
