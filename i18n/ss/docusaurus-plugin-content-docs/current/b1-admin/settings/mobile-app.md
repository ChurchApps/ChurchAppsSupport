# Mobile App Ezicondzweni

<div class="article-intro">

Ikhasi le-Mobile App Ezicondzweni likuvumela kulungiselela emathebhu wekunavigethi avela kubonakalo bemalungu eBandla lakho ku **B1.church mobile experience (PWA)**. Ulawula ukuthi yimaphi amathebhu abonakalako, ukuthi ahlanganiswe kuphi, nendlela avetwa ngayo.

## Ukufinyelela Izilungiselelo Zekunavigethi

1. Ku-B1 Admin, vula [Jump menu](../introduction.md#getting-around-with-the-jump-menu) (ibha yokusesha ephezulu kwesobunxele) bese unweba **Mobile**.
2. Chofoza **Navigation** (`/mobile/navigation`).
3. Ikhasi le-Navigation libonisa amathebhu wakho wohlelo lamanje.

## Ukungeza Ithebhu Elisha

1. Chofoza inkinobho **Add Tab** phezulu kwekhasi.
2. Gcwalisa imininingwane yethebhu:
   - **Name** -- Igama elizobonakala kuthebhu (isibonelo, "Sermons" noma "Give").
   - **Icon** -- Chofoza isikhethi sesithonjana ukuze ukhethe isithonjana sethebhu lakho.
   - **Image** -- Chofoza **Select Image** (noma **Change Image**) ukuze unikeze ithebhu lakho isithombe. Amathebhu anesithombe avela njengematayela wesithombe kuSikrini sokuQala sohlelo -- bheka [Home Screen Tiles](#home-screen-tiles).
   - **Tab Type** -- Khetha kumakhetho afana ne-Bible, Live Stream, Donation, Website, nokunye.
   - **URL** -- Faka ikheli lewebhu ethebhu kufanele liyixhumanise kulo.
   - **Visibility** -- Lawula ukuthi ngubani ongalibona leli thebhu (wonke umuntu, amalungu kuphela, njll.). Uma kuthebhu le-**Member Directory**, lokhu kulawula kuphela ukuthi ngubani obona ithebhu ngokwalo; ngubani ongavula uhla lwamalungu kusethwa yi-**Show in Directory** ekhasini le-Member portal (bheka [Member Directory & Messaging Settings](#member-directory--messaging-settings)).
3. Chofoza **Save Tab** ukuze wengeze kuhlelo lwakho.

## Ukuhlela Ithebhu Elikhona

1. Chofoza noma iyiphi ithebhu ekhona ohlwini lwe **App Tabs**.
2. Buyekeza igama lethebhu, isithonjana, i-URL, uhlobo, noma izilungiselelo zokubonakala.
3. Chofoza **Save Tab** ukuze usebenzise izinguquko zakho.

## Ukuhlela Kabusha Amathebhu

Ungashintsha ukuthi amathebhu avela ngayiphi indlela ohlelweni lweselula. Hudula bese uwisa amathebhu ohlwini ukuze uwahlele kabusha. Ukuhleleka okubonakala kulelikhasi kuyafana nokuhleleka amalungu akho azokubona kuhlelo.

:::info
Amanye amathebhu angavela ngokuzenzakalelayo uma izimo ezithile zifinyelelwa -- isibonelo, ithebhu le-Live Stream lingavela uma kukhona isitriimu esisebenzayo. Amathebhu angeziwe ngesandla anikeza ulawulo olugcwele ngokuthi amalungu akho abone ini nini.
:::

:::tip
Gcina inani lamathebhu lilawuleka. Amathebhu amathathu kuya kwayisihlanu asebenza kahle emabandleni amaningi. Amathebhu amaningi kakhulu angadida ukunavigetha kumalungu akho.
:::

## Izilungiselelo Zohla Lwamalungu Nokuthumelelana

Into ethi **Member portal** ebhokisini elifanayo le-Mobile ibamba izilungiselelo ezilawula uhla lwamalungu nemilayezo yangasese kuB1.church:

- **Directory Approval Group** -- Iqembu elibuyekeza izibuyekezo zohla lwamalungu, kanye nezicelo [zokususwa kwe-akhawunti](../profile/account-deletion.md), ngaphambi kokuba zisebenze.
- **Show in Directory** -- Ngubani ongavula uhla lwamalungu nokuthi ubani olulwini: **Admins Only**, **Members and Above** (okuzenzakalelayo), **Regular Attendees and Above**, noma **Visitors and Above**. Abantu esimweni sabo sobulungu esingaphansi kwalelo zinga okhethile abakubonis uhla futhi abalulwini. **Admins Only** ivala uhla kubo bonke ngaphandle kwesitaff esinelungelo le-People &gt; View (isimo se-Staff sodwa asanele), abangakwazi ukuvula uhla bese bebona bonke, nanoma yini lokhu okusethiwe kuyo. Izivakashi azifakwa ngalutho, njengoba noma ubani ozibhalisela ku-inthanethi uqala njengesivakashi.
- **Visibility Preference** -- Isetha okuzenzakalelayo kuBandla lonke kumalungu angakakhethi izilungiselelo zawo. **Address**, **Phone Number**, kanye **Email** ngayinye inedrobho yayo, enamazinga amahlanu afanayo atholakala kuwo wonke amabalulwa okubonakala:
  - **Everyone** -- kuyabonakala kunoma ubani, kufaka phakathi izivakashi ezingaziwa
  - **Members** -- kuyabonakala kubantu abanerekhodi le-Member noma le-Staff kuphela
  - **Groups Only** -- kuyabonakala kubantu abahlangana neqembu elifanayo nalo muntu, kanye nesitaff
  - **My Group Leaders and Staff** -- kuyabonakala kubaholi bequmbu lalo muntu elilo, kanye nesitaff
  - **Staff Only** -- kuyabonakala kusitaff kuphela (noma ubani isimo sakhe sobulungu esiyi-Staff, noma onelungelo le-People &gt; View), nakumuntu ngokwakhe

  "Staff" kulawa mazinga kusho noma ubani isimo sakhe sobulungu siyi-Staff noma onelungelo le-People &gt; View. Izinguquko zamaqembu zisebenza ngokushesha: umuntu ojoyina iqembu, noma owenziwa umholi wequmbu, angabona imininingwane ehambisana ne-**Groups Only** noma ne-**My Group Leaders and Staff** yamalungu alelo qembu ngaphandle kokuphuma ngena futhi.

  Amalungu angasetha phezu kwalezi zinhlobo ezizenzakalelayo erekhodi lawo siqu ethebhu le-**Privacy** lephrofayili yawo ku-B1.church PWA -- bheka [Editing Your Profile](/docs/b1-church/getting-started/me-page).
- **Minimum Age for Private Messages** -- Ukulawula kokuphepha kwabantwana. I-B1 ngeke ivule ingxoxo **entsha** yemilayezo yangasese uma omunye wabantu abahlanganyela kuyo engaphansi kwale minyaka, ngokusekelwe kusuku lokuzalwa (indima yasendlini isetshenziswa njengesipho uma kungekho suku lokuzalwa olugciniwe). Abantu abangaphansi kwaleyo minyaka basabonakala ngokugcwele kuhla lwamalungu -- kuvinjelwa ukuxhumana ngqo kuphela, **kuzo zombili izinhlangothi**, kubo bonke, kufaka phakathi isitaff. Ingxoxo yamaqembu nokuxhumana nabazali bomntwana kusasebenza. Okukhethwayo yi-Off, 13, 16, noma 18; okuzenzakalelayo yi-**18**. Izingxoxo ezikhona azithintwa.

:::tip
Ngoba ukuhlolwa kweminyaka kuncike emasukwini okuzalwa, qiniseka ukuthi amasuku okuzalwa abantwana eBandleni lakho agcwalisiwe. Lesi silungiselelo siphethe emndenini ofanayo wezilawuli zokuphepha zokungena [check-in safety controls](../attendance/checkin-safety.md).
:::

### Umyalezo Wokungena Esikrinini Sokuqala

Izivakashi ezivula isikrini sokuqala sohlelo [Home screen](/docs/b1-church/getting-started/navigating#home) ngaphandle kokungena zibona umyalezo omfishane -- ngokuzenzakalelayo, *"Sign in to see your groups, giving, and more."* -- eduze kwenkinobho ethi **Sign In**. Izilungiselelo ze**Home screen sign-in prompt** ekhasini elifanayo le-Member portal (`/mobile/b1-mobile`) zikuvumela ukuthi uwushintshe:

- **Show sign-in prompt on the app home screen** -- Vala lokhu ukuze ufihle kokubili umyalezo kanye nenkinobho ye**Sign In** esikrinini sokuqala. Izivakashi zisakwazi ukungena kusuka kwimenyu yohlelo.
- **Sign-in prompt text** -- Shintsha amagama okuzenzakalelayo ngomyalezo wakho (kuya kumapharamitha angu-150). Shiya kungenalutho ukuze usebenzise okuzenzakalelayo. Lebhokisi likhubazekile uma umyalezo uvalwe.

Chofoza **Save** ukuze usebenzise. Umyalezo othi **Saved** uqinisekisa inguquko. Ukugcina kubuyekeza izilungiselelo zohlelo ezigciniwe, ngakho inguquko ibonakala ngesikhathi esilandelayo lapho isikrini sokuqala silayisha.

### Amathayela Esikrini Sokuqala

Amathebhu anesithombe (bheka **Image** ku-[Adding a New Tab](#adding-a-new-tab)) avela esikrinini sokuqala sohlelo [Home screen](/docs/b1-church/getting-started/navigating#home) njengamathayela esithombe, ngaphansi kwesigaba esinesihloko segama leBandla lakho. Lokhu kufaka ithebhu le-Verse of the Day, uma uyinika isithombe. Amathebhu esithombe asavela nasebhalweni lamathebhu.

Isilungiselelo se**Home screen tiles** ekhasini elifanayo le-Member portal (`/mobile/b1-mobile`) silawula ukuthi lawa mathayela ahleleke kanjani. Khetha **Layout**:

- **Grid** -- Amathayela amabili ngomugqa ngamunye (okuzenzakalelayo). Uma inani lamathayela lingelona elilinganayo, ithayela lokuqala lithatha ububanzi bonke.
- **Scrolling row** -- Umugqa oyodwa wamathayela amalungu awaswayipha ngakwesobunxele nakwesokudla.
- **Scrolling (automatic)** -- Umugqa ofanayo wokuswayipha, kodwa ophinde unyakaze wodwa, uhambele phambili ithayela linye cishe njalo ngemizuzwana emine, uphinde uqale kwithayela lokuqala ngemuva kokuphela kwawo. Uyama uma umlayezi ethinta, ehambisa isandla phezu kwawo, noma efaka ngokhiye kumugqa, bese uqhubeka emva kwemizuzwana embalwa. Kumadivayisi asethelwe ukunciphisa ukunyakaza, umugqa uhlala ume futhi usebenza njenge-**Scrolling row**.
- **List** -- Uhlu olunesithombe esincane eduze kwegama ngalinye lethebhu.

Kuzo zombili izindlela zokuswayipha, uma kukhona amathayela amaningi kunalawo angangena kuwo wonke ububanzi besikrini, umugqa wamachashazi uvela ngaphansi kwamathayela, enye yonke ithayela, nethayela lamanje lithi ligcwele. Amalungu angathepha ichashazi ukuze aye kuthayela elithile.

Chofoza **Save** ukuze usebenzise.

### Izigaba Zesikrini Sokuqala

Amalungu angenile njalo abona umlando wawo wokuhambela nemafomu awathumele kuKhasi le-[Me page](/docs/b1-church/getting-started/me-page). Okhethweni lwe**Home screen sections** kuleli khasi elifanayo le-Member portal (`/mobile/b1-mobile`) lungazikhombisa nasesikrinini sokuqala:

- **Show My Attendance on Home** -- Ingeza isigaba se**My Attendance** esibonisa ukuthi ilungu lahambela kangaki emalanga angu-90 adlule, izindawo ezimbili zakamuva zokuhamba, kanye nesixhumanisi se**History** esiya ohlwini lwakho lonke lokuhamba.
- **Show My Forms on Home** -- Ingeza isigaba se**My Forms** esinemafomu amabili amalungu akamuva athumele, kanye nesixhumanisi se**All forms**.

Zombili zivaliwe ngokuzenzakalelayo. Isigaba sivela kuphela kumalungu angenile futhi anerekhodi elilodwa noma ngaphezulu okufanele libonakale. Chofoza **Save** ukuze usebenzise.

## Lapho Amathebhu Lezi Abonakala Khona

Amathebhu olungiselela lapha abonakaliswa ku-**B1.church PWA** amalungu akho afakayo kunoma yiliphi ikhasi ku-`https://yourchurchname.b1.church`. Izinguquko ozenza kulelikhasi ziyabonakala ngesikhathi esilandelayo lapho ilungu livula uhlelo.

Ku-foni, ibha yamathebhu aphansi ibonisa **Home**, kuya kwamathathu wamathebhu akho, kanye **More**; kukhompyutha, ikhanda libonisa kuya kwayisihlanu. Izikrini ezakhelwe ngaphakathi (njengeSermons, Giving, noma Groups) zithola izikhala zazo kuqala, bese amathebhu we-**Internal Page** egcwalisa izikhala ezisele. Amathebhu we-**External URL** ne-**Verse of the Day** akhe akuthole isikhala. Lonke ithebhu, kufaka phakathi lezo ezingafakeki, ibhalwe ekhasini le-**More** lohlelo.

Amathebhu abekwe ngaphakathi kwethebhu le-**Folder** yiwona ayingxenye, abonakala kuphela esikrinini sesikhwama, elivulwa ngamalungu ngokuchofoza isikhwama. Leso sikrini sibonisa amathebhu njengamathayela esithombe uma wonke enesithombe, noma njengohlu uma kungenjalo. Isikhwama esingenawo amathebhu alungele ilungu ngalinye sifihliwe kulowo lungu. Bheka [Folder Tabs](../../b1-church/getting-started/navigating.md#folder-tabs). Bheka [Navigating B1App](../../b1-church/getting-started/navigating.md) ukuze uthole ukuthi amalungu abona ini.
