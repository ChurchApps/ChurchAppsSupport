# Tingoma

<div class="article-intro">

Likhasi Letingoma liphatsa lilabhulari yakho yetingoma tekukhonta. Gcina yonkhe imininingwane yetingoma, emagama, netindlandlela endzaweni yinye kuze ithimba lakho lekukhonta likwati kulungiselela tinsita letitako futsi ligcine umculo lolingananako.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Ngaphambi Kokuqala</h4>

- Ku-[Menyu Yekuhamba Ngekushesha](../introduction.md#getting-around-with-the-jump-menu) ku-B1 Admin, khetha **Serving > Tingoma** ukuze ufinyelele likhasi Letingoma
- Uma kufanele, lungiselela [tinhlelo tetinsita](./plans.md) kuze ukwazi kufaka tingoma ngqo esigabeni se-[Hlelo Lwensita](./service-order.md)

</div>

## Ukuhamba Ngelilabhulari Yakho Yetingoma

Uma uvula likhasi **Tingoma**, tingoma takho tibhalwe etafuleni. Umugca ngamunye ukhombisa sithombe sesifinyeto sengoma, sihloko, umculi, nesikhathi sayo. Sebenzisa tilawuli tekhasi ngaphansi kuhlu ukuze uhambe ngelilabhulari lelikhulu, noma funa ukufinyelela lokudzingekako.

## Kufuna Tingoma

1. Chofoza ebhokisini **Funa Tingoma** ekuphepheni kwekhasi Letingoma.
2. Bhala **sihloko** sengoma noma ligama lomculi.
3. Uhlu luyahlungwa njengoba ubhala, lukhombise tingoma letifanako kusuka elilabhulari lakho nenani letifanako. Chofoza **Sula** ukuze ukhombise tonkhe tingoma futsi.

## Kwengeta Ingoma

1. Chofoza **Engeta Ingoma** ekuphepheni kwekhasi Letingoma.
2. Ku-**Funa Ingoma**, funa emfuleti wetingoma wangaphandle ngo-**Sihloko noma Umculi**.
3. Khetha ingoma ofuna kuyengeta, noma chofoza **Dala Ngesandla** uma ungayitfoli.
4. Ingoma itawengetwa elilabhulari lakho nemininingwane yayo yalokusisekelo ilungiselwe kusenguye.

:::tip
Ngemva kokungeta ingoma, chofoza kuyo ukuze uyihlole futsi ulungise imininingwane yayo, engete tindlandlela, noma unamathise tixhumanisi tangaphandle njenge-tividiyo ze-YouTube noma emacwecwe emakhodi.
:::

### Tingoma Letikhululekile Ku-WorshipCommons

Imiphumela yakho yekufuna ingafaka nesigaba se-**WorshipCommons — mahhala** ecaleni kwemiphumela ejwayelekile. Lezi tingoma letilungele Sonto evela ku-[WorshipCommons](https://worshipcommons.org) letibhekwe futsi letanikezwe welalela kukhonta. Umphumela ngamunye ukhombisa libheji lelilayisensi — **Mahhala kukhonta** noma **Lilungelo lelivulekile** — kuze wati ticinisekile ngetimo ngaphambi kokuyengeta.

- Chofoza umphumela we-WorshipCommons ukuze uwungeze elilabhulari lakho, njengemiphumela yekufuna ejwayelekile.
- Chofoza **Buka ku-WorshipCommons** ukuze uvule lulwatfo lwekuqala ethebhu lelisha.

Kuphela tingoma letidlule kuhlolwa kwe-WorshipCommons letivela lapha, ngako lolukhetfo lungaba lincane kunemfuleti yetingoma lenkhulu.

Ukuze ubhalise lilabhulari lelugcwele le-WorshipCommons, ushicilele emacwecwe, noma ukhiphe tibonakaliso, bona [tincwadzi te-WorshipCommons](../../worship-commons/index.md).

## Kususa Tingoma

Chofoza ibhokisi lelisecaleni kwengoma yinye noma letinyenti (noma ibhokisi lelisekhanda ukukhetsa tonkhe tingoma letikhonjiwe), bese uchofoza **Susa Lokukhethiwe** ebhari lelivela futsi uciniseke. Chofoza **Sula** ukuze ukhansele kukhetsa.

## Kubuka Nokuhlela Ingoma

Chofoza sihloko sengoma ukuze uvule ikhasi lemininingwane yayo. Sihloko, umculi, nemininingwane njenge-albhamu, khiye, sikhatsi, i-BPM, nemetha tivela ngakwesobunxele, kanye netindlandlela netixhumanisi tangaphandle. Kusuka lapha ungenta:

- **Hlela imininingwane yengoma** -- Chofoza **Hlela** ukuze uvuselele sihloko, umculi, sikhatsi, nedatha lenye. Chofoza **Kwenile** uma uqedile. Ukuze ususe ingoma elilabhulari lakho, chofoza **Hlela** bese **Susa ingoma**.
- **Phatsa tindlandlela** -- Ingoma ngayinye ingaba netindlandlela letinyenti (njengetikhiye noma tinguquko letehlukene). Chofoza **Engeta Indlandlela** ukuze wakhe lenye, bese uchofoza ligama lendlandlela ngaphansi kuka **Tindlandlela** ukuze uyivule.
- **Engeta emagama** -- Bhala noma uvuselele emagama kulesinye sindlandlela usebentisa inotheshini ye-ChordPro (njenge `[G]Amazing grace`). Kuhlolwa lokuphilako lusecaleni kwemhleli luveta lichati lemakhodi lalungisiwe njengoba ubhala, kuze ubone kahle lokutawubonwa ithimba lakho.
- **Setha khiye** -- Cacisa khiye yemculo yalesinye sindlandlela usebentisa ikhetsi lekhiye ecaleni kwemagama. Ikhetsi likhombisa tikhiye njengoba bantu bemculo bajwayelekile kubhala tona (C, Db, D, Eb, E, F, F#, G, Ab, A, Bb, B). Kushintja kwekhiye kutaguqula emakhodi akhombisiwe ngekushesha, kokubili ekhasini lendlandlela nalapho uvula ingoma kusuka ohlelweni lwetinsita, kuze ithimba lakho lihlale libona emakhodi ekhiye lebayidlalako. Emakhodi akhishwa ngokuhambisana nekhiye: tikhiye letilinyatelo njenge-Bb noma Eb tikhombisa emagama emakhodi anelinyatelo (Bb, Eb, Ab) esikhundleni setiphawu letinyuka (#).
- **Engeta tixhumanisi tangaphandle** -- Hambisa tixhumanisi emitfombeni njenge-tividiyo ze-YouTube, emacwecwe emakhodi, noma umculo lobhalwe lenye indzawo.

## Tindlandlela

Indlandlela imelela inguquko lehlukile yengoma, njengencenye lesheshako lesimanje kuneculo lolunyelelako lolwesiko, noma ingoma lefanele ikhiye lehlukile ngelithimba lelihlukile. Tindlandlela letinhlobonhlobo tengoma lefanako tiyabelana ngesihloko nomculi, kodvwa tinemisho yato lemele:

- **Khiye** — ikhiye lemculo lendlandlela lediliwa ngayo
- **BPM** — ishaya lemculo ngetinkhapha ngemzuzu
- **Sikhatsi** — sikhatsi ngemizuzwana
- **Metha** — sibalo sesikhathi somculo (njenge-4/4, 3/4, 6/8)
- **Ukulandelana** — inhlelo yemakhodi/tingcenye (njenge-Intro → Vesi → Kwaciniswa → Ibhuloho)
- **Emagama** — ekubonakaliseni noma ekuprojekthiseni
- **Tixhumanisi tangaphandle** — emacwecwe emakhodi, emarekhodi e-YouTube, umculo lobhalwe, noma tinye tintfo letitsite

Lapho wengeta ingoma ku-[Hlelo Lwensita](./service-order.md), ungakhetsa lendlandlela lozoyisebentisa kulelo nsita lelithile, kuze ithimba lakho lihlale lijwayela futsi lidlale inguquko lefanele.

### Umsindvo Wekuphrakthisa

Batfoli bekukhonta bangancamelana umsindvo wekuphrakthisa kulendlandlela, kuze ithimba likwati kulalela ngaphambi kwekuphrakthisa:

1. Vula ingoma bese ukhetsa lendlandlela.
2. Chofoza **Layisha Umsindvo**.
3. Khetha ifayela le-MP3, M4A, noma AAC (kuya ku-25 MB — i-WAV ayemukelwa, ukugcina kusetjentiswa kwesitoreji kuncane).
4. Gcina. Ithrekhi iyadlala lapho kunento njalo lapho lendlandlela ivela khona — ekhasini lendlandlela ku-B1 Admin nakuhlelo lwensita lapho ilungu lethimba livula khona ku-portal yelilunga le-B1.church. Tinto tohlelo netinemsindvo tibonisa sibonakaliso se-music-note esikhundleni sesithombe sesifinyeto.

Kususa lendlandlela nako kususa ifayela lomsindvo lelilayishiwe.

:::info
Tingoma letisemtfolamphilo wakho tingangetwa ngqo ku-[Hlelo Lwensita](./service-order.md) ngaphakathi kwesinsita, kuhlanganisa imininingwane yengoma nendlandlela ekhetsiwe nalesinsita lesithile.
:::
