# Kungena

<div class="article-intro">

I-B1 Admin isekela kungena ngekhatsi emasevisi ngesisetjentiswa lesihlanganyelwa se-**B1 Checkin**. Emalunga angangena ngekhatsi nemindeni yawo kumakhiyosiki noma kutidivayisi letinikelwe lapho afika, kwenta inqubo isheshe futsi inciphise umsebenti betinsita. Konkhe kungena ngekhatsi kurekhodwa ngekuzenzekela njengekuba khona.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Ngaphambi Kokuqala</h4>

- Emakampasi akho, tikhatsi temasevisi, kanye namaqembu kufanele kumiswe ku-[Setfwa Kwekuba Khona](setup.md).
- Udzinga [bantfu kwidatabase yakho](../people/adding-people.md) nemindeni [households](../people/adding-people.md#managing-households) lesetfwe kutsi emindeni ikwati kungena ndzawonye.
- Uyadzinga ithebhulethi futsi ngenhlanhla iphrinta yetilebula ye-Brother (bona [tincomo temcondvo](#recommended-hardware) ngentasi).

</div>

## Indlela Lisebenta Ngayo

I-app ye-B1 Checkin ixhumana nesetfwa sakho sekuba khona se-B1 Admin. Lilungu lapho lingena ngekhatsi, kuba khona kwalo kurekhodwa ngekuzenzekela endzaweni yekhampasi, sikhatsi sesevisi, nelilungu lelifanele. Awudzingi kufaka kuba khona ngesandla kulabo labasebentisa luhlelo lwekungena ngekhatsi.

## Kumisa Kungena Ngekhatsi

1. **Lungisa sakhiwo sekuba khona kuqala.** Ku-B1 Admin, yiya ku-**People > Attendance** bese uchofoza **Setup** kutsi uqinisekise kutsi emakampasi, tikhatsi temasevisi, kanye namaqembu atfolakala. I-app yekungena ngekhatsi incike kulolu setfwa. Ngekukhetsa, nika ilixeshana ngalinye lesikhatsi sesevisi [sikhatsi sekungena ngekhatsi](setup.md#setting-a-check-in-schedule) kutsi kungena ngekhatsi kuvule futsi kuvale ngekuzenzekela sonkhe sonto. Tikhatsi tisebentisa sikhatsi lesisemakhampasini sesevisi, noma sikhatsi lesisebandleni lakho uma likhampasi lingakasetfwa; ikhiyosiki iveta tikhatsi temasevisi lamavindzi awo avalekile njenge **Closed**, futsi itsi emindenini nalapho kungena khona kuvulwa khona. Bona [Setfwa Kwekuba Khona](setup.md) kutsi uthole lokuningiliswe.
2. **Faka i-app ye-B1 Checkin** kutidivayisi lotihlose kusebentisa. I-app itfolakala kulamatfuba lalandzelako:
   - **iPad/iOS:** [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/b1-church-check-in/id6775081998)
   - **Android/Samsung Tablets:** [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=church.b1.checkin)
   - **Amazon Fire Tablets:** [Amazon App Store](https://www.amazon.com/Live-Church-Solutions-B1-Check-In/dp/B0FW5HKRB5/)
3. **Ngena ku-app ye-B1 Checkin** usebentisa i-akhawunti lenelungelo lekuba khona lelithi **Checkin** (noma **Edit Attendance**). Bona [Ti-akhawunti Te-Kiosk Yekungena Ngekhatsi](../settings/roles-permissions.md#check-in-kiosk-accounts).
4. **Khetha likhampasi nesikhatsi sesevisi** salesihlangano lamanje.
5. Emalunga manje angafuna ligama lawo kutidivayisi bese angena ngekhatsi.

:::tip
Beka tidivayisi tekungena ngekhatsi endzaweni lebonakala futsi lefinyeleleka kalula, njengasemnyango wencenye noma emadeskini ekwamukela. Sibhengezo lesifishane ngesikhatsi sesevisi sisita emalungu kutsi ati kutsi lenkhetho itfolakala.
:::

:::tip
Uma libandla lakho linemakhampasi lamanyenti, kufanele uphindze wente kumisa lokufanele likhampasi ngalinye ku-[Setfwa Kwekuba Khona](setup.md). Ngalinye lidivayisi lekungena ngekhatsi lingasetfwa kukhampasi lelihlukile.
:::

## Tincomo Tetihardware

**Matablethi** -- noma yini kulaba isebenta kahle ne-app:

- **Lilula:** Samsung Galaxy Tab A7 Lite 8.7"
- **Sikrini lesikhulu:** Samsung Galaxy Tab A8 10.5"
- **Lenetingiyo letincane:** Amazon Fire HD 10

**Tiphrinta** -- tekungena ngekhatsi tisebentisa tiphrinta tetilebula te-Brother kuphrinta emabheji emagama:

- **Lekuhle kakhulu:** Brother QL-1110NWB (isekela matablethi lamanyenti ngeBluetooth ne-WiFi)
- **Lihle:** Brother QL-810W (isekela matablethi lamanyenti nge-WiFi)
- **Lenetingiyo letincane:** Brother QL-1100 (i-WiFi kuphela)

**Tilebula:** Brother DK-1201 (1-1/7" x 3-1/2")

:::warning
Kuphela tiphrinta tetilebula te-Brother ihambisana ne-app ye-B1 Checkin. Tiphrinta letinye tetinye timakhampani ngeke tisebente ekuphrinteni emabheji emagama.
:::

:::info
Landzela imiyalo yekusetfwa kwephrinta yakho kutsi uyixhumanise kunethiwekhi ye-WiFi lefanako nethebhulethi yakho. Ungatfola tiphrofayivi te-Brother nemihlahlandlela yekusetfwa ku-[webhusayithi yekusekela ye-Brother](https://support.brother.com).
:::

## Kwenta Kubonakala Kwekhiyosiki

Ungakwenta kubonakale nemizwa ye-app ye-B1 Checkin ifanane nemibono yebandla lakho. Ku-B1 Admin, yiya ku-**Mobile > B1 CheckIn** bese usebentisa ikhadi le-**Kiosk Theme** kulungisa:

### Emabala

Lungisa emasethingi emabala lasikhombisa kutsi afanane nemibono yebandla lakho:

- **Primary** ne **Primary Contrast** -- Umbala lobalulekile wemkhiqizo nembala wemtfunti wawo.
- **Secondary** ne **Secondary Contrast** -- Umbala wekwengamela nembala wemtfunti wawo.
- **Header Background** ne **Subheader Background** -- Emabala etindzawo letiphezulu tekhiyosiki.
- **Button Background** ne **Button Text** -- Emabala tematikhinti lasebenta.

### Sithombe Semuva

Layisha umfanekiso longakhetfwa wemuva wetikrini letimukela nekubheka kwekhiyosiki. Lusayizi lolunconywako ngu-1920x1080 pixels.

### Sikrini Lesilalako / Screensaver

Misa screensaver levulako ngemuva kwesikhatsi sekungasebenti:

1. Vula noma valisa sikrini lesilalako.
2. Setsa **timeout** (tingaki tetisekhondi kungasebenti ngaphambi kwekutsi screensaver iqale, okungenani 10 wetisekhondi).
3. Engeta sidzingo lesisodvwa noma lelingetulu -- lilinye ngalinye linemfanekiso nesikhatsi sekuboniswa (okungenani 3 wetisekhondi).

:::tip
Sebentisa sikrini lesilalako kuboniswa timemetulo, tihlelo letizayo, noma timemetulo letinhle lapho ikhiyosiki ingasasetjentiswa.
:::

## Kubhalisa Bavakashi Ngeliqhugo Le-QR

Ikhiyosiki yekungena ngekhatsi ingakhombisa liqhugo le-QR lebavakashi labaskena ngalo kutsi batibhalise bona nemindeni yabo ngefoni yabo. Lokhu kusheshisa inqubo yekungena ngekhatsi kubavakashi labangara.

Lapho umvakashi askena liqhugo, utfutfukiselwa ku-[khasi lekubhalisa bavakashi](../../b1-church/checkin/guest-registration.md) lapho afaka khona emagama emndeni wakhe nemininingwane lenye lokhetha kutsi ibuvwe. Umsebenti angabe asakhangela khona ekhiyosikini bese amngenisa.

### Kuvula Kubhalisa Bavakashi Nge-QR

Kutsi uvule kukhombisa liqhugo le-QR:

1. Ku-B1 Admin, vula i-[Jump menu](../introduction.md#getting-around-with-the-jump-menu) (ibha lekusesha lelingentasi lesenhloko) bese unweba **Mobile**.
2. Chofoza **B1 CheckIn**.
3. Vula **QR Guest Registration** bese uchofoza **Save**.

:::note
Lesi setingi silaphansi kwe-**Mobile > B1 CheckIn** (lilungu lekhasi lelifanako nekhadi le-**Kiosk Theme**), hhayi ngaphansi kwe-Attendance. Sitfutfuku se-**Kiosk** ekhasini le-Attendance sinelinki lelisiya kulelikhasi.
:::

Lapho sekuvuliwe, tinketho letitsite talapha ngaphansi tiyavela kukhadi lelifanako le-**QR Guest Registration**. Chofoza **Save** ekhadini ngemuva kwekutsi ushintshe noma yiyiphi kato.

### Kukhetsa Tinkhundla Tekubhalisa

Uhlu lwe-**Sign-up fields** lulawula lokubuzwako yifomu lekubhalisa. **First Name** ne**Last Name** kuhlale kukhona kufomu futsi kudzingeka kuwo wonkhe emalunga emndeni. Kutinye tinkhundla, vula i-**Include** kutsi uyingenise kufomu, bese uvula i-**Required** uma bavakashi kufanele bayigcwalise. I-**Required** ingavulwa kuphela uma i-**Include** ivuliwe, futsi ukucima i-**Include** kuyayisusa.

| Inkhundla | Kubuzwa | Ifakwa ngekuzenzekela |
|-------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Title** | Lilungu lemndeni ngalinye, njengolucondvo lolwehla phansi ngaphambi kwegama (Mr, Mrs, Miss, Ms, Dr, Prof, Rev, Pastor) | Cha |
| **Email** | Umuntu wekutfola kuphela | Yebo (ngekukhetsa) |
| **Phone** | Lilungu lemndeni ngalinye. Uma kudzingeka, umuntu wekutfola kuphela udzinga kuyigcwalisa. | Yebo (ngekukhetsa) |
| **Household role** | Lilungu lemndeni ngalinye ngemuva komuntu wekutfola -- Spouse, Child, noma Other. Umuntu wekutfola uba yinhloko yemndeni lomusha. Lowo longenayo indzima uyagcinwa njenge-Other. | Yebo (ngekukhetsa) |
| **Home address** | Kanye ngemndeni -- umgwaqo, umugca wesibili wekheli, lidolobha, sifundza, nekhodi yeposi. Uma kudzingeka, konkhe ngaphandle kwemugca wesibili kufanele kugcwaliswe. Ikheli ligcinwa kulelilungu lemndeni ngalinye. | Cha |
| **Birth date** | Lilungu lemndeni ngalinye | Cha |
| **Gender** | Lilungu lemndeni ngalinye | Cha |
| **Marital status** | Umuntu wekutfola kuphela | Cha |
| **Grade** | Lilungu lemndeni ngalinye (PreK kuya ku-12, noma Graduated) | Cha |
| **Comments** | Kanye, ebhokisini lingaphansi kwemalungu emndeni (kuya ku-1,000 wetinhlamvu) | Cha |

Tinkhundla letitsi letivalekile ngeke silondvolotwe, nome kungabe umuntu uyitfumela ngenye indlela. Tikhatsi tigcinwa njengenothi ekurekhodweni komuntu wekutfola (ipostwa njenge "Guest sign-up"), kutsi uyibone ku-**Notes** lephrofayili yakhe.

### Kwengeta Bavakashi Eliqembu

Sebentisa **Add new guests to group** kutsi ufake bonkhe labo labhalisako eliqembwini lelilelinye lemaqembu akho ngekuzenzekela -- njengeliqembu lelitsi "Newcomers" noma leliqembu lesiphelo salelisonto. Khetha **None** (lokuzenzekelako) uma ungafuni kukwenta loku. Bavakashi abasoze babone noma bakhethe liqembu; lisetfwa lapha kuphela. Lishintshe ngaphambi kwesiphelo ngasinye kutsi ulandzele bavakashi besiphelo ndzawonye.

### Kurekhodwa Kwekuba Khona

Lapho liqembu likhethiwe, i-switch **Also record attendance** iyavela. Yivule kutsi bavakashi babalwe njengaba khona ngaleso sikhatsi labhalisa, ngalokuba ungadzingi kubangena ngekhatsi ngalokwehlukile.

Kuba khona kurekhodwa kuphela uma kubhalisa kwenteka ngaphakathi kwetikhatsi letihleliwe (**scheduled**) tesevisi yeliqembu lelikhethiwe:

- Liqembu kufanele libotjwe kutikhatsi temasevisi lenyenti noma letinyenti, futsi letikhatsi temasevisi kufanele tibe nesikhatsi sekungena ngekhatsi (a [check-in schedule](setup.md#setting-a-check-in-schedule)) (i-**Day** ne-**Start Time**).
- "Ngaphakathi" kusho ngaphakathi kwewindow yekungena ngekhatsi yalesikhatsi sesevisi -- kusukela emaminithini **Open check-in** ngaphambi kwekuqala kuya emaminithini **Close check-in** ngemuva kwekuphela (noma ngemuva kwekuqala, uma kungasetfwanga sikhatsi sekuphela; uma kungekho sikhatsi sekuphela nemaminithi lokuvala, kuze kuphele losuku), esikhatsini selive lesikhatsi sekungena ngekhatsi lisebentisa.
- Lilungu lemndeni ngalinye limakwa njengelikhona eliqembini kuso sonkhe sikhatsi sesevisi lesivulekile ngaleso sikhatsi, ngalolusuku lwanamuhla.
- Kubhalisa ngesikhatsi lesingecile, noma kuliqembu lesikhatsi semasevisi alo singenasikhatsi, kuyangeniswa eliqembini kodvwa akubalwa njengekuba khona.

### Sithombe Sekhanda

Ngaphansi kwe-**Header photo**, chofoza **Change photo** kutsi ukhetse noma ulayishe umfanekiso (uma kuhleleka ku-16:9) kutsi ukhombise phezulu kwekhasi lekubhalisa esikhundleni sesithonjana sekuzenzekela. Chofoza **Remove** kutsi ubuyele esithonjaneni.

### Kwabelana Ngelinki Lekubhalisa

Lapho QR Guest Registration isivuliwe, sigaba se**Share registration QR code** sivela ekugcineni kwekhadi. Lesi sigaba sikunika tindlela letimbili tekufinyelela bavakashi kufomu lekubhalisa ngaphandle kweliqhugo lekhiyosiki:

- **Copy link** -- ikopisha ikheli le-URL lekubhalisa kutsi ukwati kulipeyista ewebhusayithini yebandla lakho, kuma-imeyili, noma nasiphi sikhala se-inthanethi.
- **Download PNG** -- itsatsa liqhugo njengemfanekiso longaphrinta kuma-flyer, mabhuletini, noma kumabhodi etimphawu.

:::tip
Engeta linki lekubhalisa ku-webhusayithi yebandla lakho ekhasini le-"Plan Your Visit" noma "I'm New", kutsi bavakashi bakwati kubhalisa ngaphambi kwekufika kwabo.
:::

### Kwenteka Emva Kwekutsi Umvakashi Abhalise

I-B1 iyawubheka kucala labantfu lasebakhona ku-**People**. Lilungu lemndeni livunyelwa kumuntu lokhona uma kunemuntu lonye kuphela lonalegama lefanako nelikheli le-imeyili noma nelucingo lefanako. Lingane lengetfwe ngaphandle kwe-imeyili nelucingo livunyelwa ngegama lalo ngaphakathi komndeni lovunyelwe. Bantfu labavunyelwe basetjentiswa njengoba bakhona -- kubhalisa akusoze kushintse imininingwane yabo noma simo sabo sekulunga -- kanti nemuntu lomusha ujoyina umndeni lovunyelwe.

Lapho kungekho lovunyelwako, kubhalisa kudala umndeni lomusha lobizwa ngelibito lemuntu wekutfola, nerekhodi lemuntu ngalilungu lemndeni ngalinye. Bonkhe labasha bangeniswa ku-**People** nesimo sekulunga se-**Visitor**, nemuntu wekutfola njengenhloko yemndeni.

Bantfu labavunyelwe kanye nalabasha bayengeniswa eliqembini lelikhethiwe (uma bengakavele babe kulo) futsi kuba khona kwabo kuyarekhodwa uma lokhu kuvuliwe. Tikhatsi igcinwa njengenothi emuntwini wekutfola.

Kutsi ulandzele ngekuzenzekela, engeta i-[workflow trigger](../serving/workflows.md#triggers) njenge **Person · Created** nemncele *Membership Status is Visitor*, noma **Group · Member Joined** leliqembu lolikhetsile ngenhla.

## Kutsi Kurekhodwa Kini

Kungena ngekhatsi ngalunye kudala irekhodi lekuba khona ku-B1 Admin. Ungabona emarekhodi lalaba ekhasini le-[Attendance](tracking-attendance.md) nasekhasini lemihlangano [yeliqembu ngalinye](../groups/group-members.md) njengalawo ekubhalwe ngesandla. Ayikho inkinga ekutsi idatha ivela kanjani -- tindlela tombili tidla kumibiko lefanako.
