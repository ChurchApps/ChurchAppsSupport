# Kuhlela Tifundvo

<div class="article-intro">

Nasewuseta tikilasi takho, sinyatselo lesilandzelako kuhlela tifundvo kuze bavolontiya bakho bati ngempela lokufanele bakufundzise nekutsi nini. Kuhlela kuhlanganisa umcondvo wetifundvo ote-Lessons.church nelikhalenda lekilasi lakho.

</div>

<div class="prereqs">
<h4>Ngaphambi Kokuqala</h4>

- [Seta nine kilasi linye](./setting-up-classrooms.md) kuleposi
- [Hlola umtfombo wemcondvo](../browsing/index.md) kuze uthole iprogramu nesifundvo lofuna kufundzisa

</div>

## Kubuka Ikhalenda Lekilasi

1. Ngena uye ku **Leposi** yakho.
2. Chofoza **ikilasi** lofuna kulihlela.
3. Ibhokisi **Ikhalenda Lekufundza** libala tonkhe tifundo letihleleke ekilasini lelo, kuhlelwe ngetusuku, latsa lesitsatsa kuqala. Ngalunye lunetitfuwo lokuthi **Lelisha**, **Lamuhla**, noma **Lendlulile**, kutsi ubone ngekushesha lokufundziswe sekutsatfwe nalokutawuba kufika.

## Kungeta Sifundo Ekhalenda

1. Ebhokisini **Ikhalenda Lekufundza**, chofoza **Engeta Ikhalenda**. Uma ikilasi lingakahleleki lutfo, chofoza **Engeta Ikhalenda Lekucala** kunalokho.
2. Faka **Lusuku Lwekhalenda** lalesifundo.
3. Uma sewungeze [emahlinzeko langaphandle](../admin/third-party-providers.md), khetsa **Umhlinzeki**. Yishiye ku **Lessons.church** uze uhlele kusuka kulibhulali ye-Lessons.church.
4. Khetsa **Iprogramu** lofuna kufundzisa kuyo (njengasekuhlaleni, "Tindzaba TeBhayibheli Tebantfwana").
5. Khetsa **Sifundvo** lesiphakatsi kuleprogramu (njengasekuhlaleni, "Kudalwa Netindzaba Letindzala").
6. Khetsa **Sifundo** lesithile lofuna kusihlela.
7. Khetsa **Indzawo** lefanele lekilasi lelo. Tindzawo timele tinguchungechunge letehlukene temcondvo, letinemnyaka lethile wetingane (njengasekuhlaleni, "Sikolwana" noma "Lusha Lwesikolwana"). Kukhetsa indzawo lefanele kuciniseka kutsi bavolontiya bakho babona lokwakhelwe lesigaba semnyaka wekilasi labo.
8. Chofoza **Gcina**. Kuhlola sifundo kucala, chofoza **Buka / Phrinta** kuze sivule kuthebhu lelisha.

Sifundo sitawubonakala manje ekhalendeni lekilasi losuku lolo.

:::info
Iprogramu, sifundvo, nesifundo ngumkhakha wemcondvo ku-Lessons.church. Iprogramu inesifundvo lesiningi, ngalunye sifundvo sinesifundo lesihlukile. Tindzawo tiniketa tinguchungechunge letehlukene temcondvo lofanako letinemnyaka lethile. Bona [Iprogramu Netifundvo](../browsing/programs-and-studies.md) kuchaza konkhe.
:::

## Indlela Bavolontiya Labasebentisa Ngayo Ikhalenda

Nasewuhlele tifundo, bavolontiya bakho bangavula leposi, bakhetse ikilasi labo, babone ngekushesha lokutawuba kufika. Bangabuka umcondvo wesifundo ngaphambilini, balande tincenye labadzingako, bafike balungele. Ngeke basaba nemibuzo ngekutsi kufundziswa kini lesilandzelako.

## Tincomo Tekuhlela

- **Hlela kusengaphambili** -- Emabandla ahlela tifundo tekilasi ku-[B1 Admin](../../b1-admin/serving/scheduling-lessons.md), kubandzakanya nekusebenta lucwaningo lwemnyaka lolushicilelwe.
- **Gcina kulandzelana** -- Kulandzela iprogramu ngekulandzelana kusita bantfwana bakhe lokuvele bakufundze ngelisonto lelidlule.
- **Hlola umlandvo** -- Sebentisa lutfo lwetifundo letindlulile kuze uqinisekise kutsi ungaphindzi lomcondvo ngephutsa.

:::warning
Qinisekisa kutsi ukhetsa indzawo lefanele yemnyaka wetingane wekilasi lakho. Kukhetsa indzawo lengafanele kusho kutsi bavolontiya bakho batawubona emcondvweni lowakhelwe lelinye lemanga emnyakeni.
:::

## Lokulandzelako

Nasewuhlele tifundo, ungafuna [kwenta umcondvo wesifundo](../customization/customizing-lessons.md) ngetikilasi letithile kuze uhambisane nendlela yakho yekufundzisa nemnyaka wetingane. Nasekufika sikhatsi sekufundzisa, fundza mayelana [nekwethula](../presenting/index.md) ukuze wetfule tifundo ngalokusebenta.
